# R1 (area expert): review of C3, "Unsticking the Subject" (UnStick-CFG)

## Summary
In the first 5 of 50 steps on Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B, the method splits the CFG difference D into a spatial coarse/fine band crossed with a temporal frame-mean/frame-varying part. It then gives gain 1 to the coarse frame-mean cell and gain w to the other three cells. Outside that window it is plain CFG, at the same 2 NFE per step. The claimed insight is that CFG's w-fold extrapolation of a frame-identical coarse layout feeds RoPE spatial anchoring [2609.23658] and glues the subject to fixed screen coordinates; withholding only that cell lets the conditional branch's own displaced trajectory candidates win. The predicted evidence has four parts:
- higher RAFT flow and dynamic degree than both CFG and an "FDG along time" control [2506.19713] at equal NFE;
- lower same-position mutual consistency in the attention probe;
- a gain that is sub-additive with RoPE-λ and smaller on CogVideoX-2B (sinusoidal positions) than on RoPE backbones;
- a flow-versus-w curve that becomes monotone, with gains concentrated on high-motion prompts and alignment and identity guards within a small margin of CFG.

## Strengths
- Zero cost and trivially deployable. It is a single linear correction on two predictions that are already computed, it reduces exactly to CFG, and it composes with per-token schemes. Practitioners on 24 GB GPUs would actually use something like this.
- The proposal is unusually honest about its delta. It concedes that, as a method, it is close to FDG's general linear-transform rule [2506.19713] and that DyMoS owns the I2V principle [2605.19398]. It also builds in the key control (FDG along time, plus guidance-off in the window) and a written kill condition.
- The evaluation design knows the field's traps:
  - dynamics are anti-correlated with VBench consistency, so quality is read at matched dynamics;
  - DEVIL grades 1–2 serve as a negative control;
  - a camera-split set separates screen-space sticking from legitimate tracking;
  - the stop rule is pilot-first.
- It ties a sampler-side change to a concrete, very recent mechanistic account [2609.23658] and extends that account from the conditional branch to the guided sampler. That is an interpretability angle that a pure "dynamic degree +x" paper lacks.
- The budget is realistic: about 23 of 40 GPU-days, with deployed tooling and a reasonable 4 min per clip planning figure.

## Weaknesses
- **[major] The method delta over FDG is small, and the effect size is never stated.** UnStick-CFG is FDG's rule with a spatio-temporal (temporal-DC) transform, gain 1 on one band, and an Interval-Guidance-style window [2506.19713][2404.07724]. FDG explicitly defines its rule for "a linear and invertible frequency transformation, such as a Laplacian pyramid or wavelet transform" [2506.19713]. The paper therefore stands or falls on the causal account plus a convincing effect. Every falsifiable prediction, however, is directional ("higher than CFG and FDG along time", "within a small margin"), with no number.
  - Taken at threshold, the result is a just-significant gain in flow and dynamic degree. No prediction claims parity with, or a Pareto win over, the baselines a specialist will put in the same table. MotionCFG roughly doubles FLOW on Wan2.1-14B [2603.14073]. TrajCFG lifts Wan2.1-1.3B dynamic degree from 65.28 to 70.83 at zero NFE [2610.14153].
  - A main table in which the proposed sampler sits below MotionCFG on motion and is only "comparable" on alignment is a hard sell at CVPR.
- **[major] The mechanism probe (pillar 2) is close to circular.** In [2609.23658] the "winner" in each frame is defined post hoc as the early candidate closest to the step-49 object region; per-frame winners are read off the final trajectory [full text, Section 5]. Under that definition, "the winner lies away from the earliest-committed frame's position" is nearly a restatement of "the subject moved". Lower MC between same-position candidates also follows almost automatically once candidates at other positions win.
  - Any method that adds motion would pass the test, and the kill condition ("MC and winner unchanged while flow rises") is close to unreachable.
  - Because the stated contribution is the causal account, this matters more than it would in a methods paper. The anchoring evidence itself is also a single basketball case study (seed 20, frames 10 and 14, with seed 26 as a second visualisation) in a paper that is less than a month old and unreviewed [2609.23658]. That paper's own head ablation found the large condition-induced static content changed object size, not trajectory.
- **[major] Several "mechanism" predictions are confounded by ceiling effects and model differences.**
  - Pillar 3 (sub-additive with RoPE-λ) is what saturation alone predicts. Dynamic degree is a bounded share, and on near-static seeds any two motion boosters overlap in the seeds they fix. Sub-additivity does not distinguish a shared mechanism from a shared ceiling unless a reference pair (for example UnStick+TrajCFG, which the account predicts to be additive) is run and the test uses unbounded flow.
  - Pillar 4 (CogVideoX-2B vs. RoPE backbones) confounds positional encoding with model size, data and baseline dynamics. CogVideoX-2B is already the high-dynamics, low-smoothness outlier among small models [2502.02492], so a smaller gain there is expected from headroom alone. It also needs its own delayed window [2603.14073]. With one model on each side, this is one data point.
- **[major] There is an internal tension between pillar 8 and pillar 1, and the intervention is physically tiny.**
  - UnStick-CFG and FDG along time differ only by (w − 1)·(I − M)·L·D, the frame-varying coarse cell. The more the coarse frame-mean cell dominates the early guidance energy (pillar 8), the smaller this difference. The proposal itself cites near-invariance of the denoising objective to frame shuffling at high noise [2502.02492]. If early predictions are close to frame-constant, the two arms are nearly the same sampler in exactly the window where they differ.
  - Separately, with Wan's shift-5 schedule, steps 1–5 move σ only from 1 to about 0.978 (the digest's computation from [2609.23658], Appendix B). The guided estimate therefore enters the state with roughly 2% weight. The proposal never quantifies ‖Δx_t‖ or the ratio ‖(I − M)LD‖ / ‖MLD‖.
  - There is also a simpler, RoPE-free reading of why the frame-shared cell matters. Averaging over 21 latent frames cuts noise amplitude by about √21 ≈ 4.6, so at high noise frame-shared coarse content is the most detectable signal regardless of the positional encoding. If that SNR reading is right, the method may work while pillar 4 fails. The predictions tie "the method works" to "the RoPE story is true" more tightly than the evidence warrants.
- **[major] The pilot is exposed to regression to the mean.** The 50 pilot pairs are chosen as the lowest-flow CFG outputs. Re-sampling those seeds with any trajectory perturbation will raise their flow on average. The comparison with CFG on that set (pillar 1, and the pilot stop rule) is therefore biased upward, and only the comparison with FDG along time is clean. Claims should rest on unselected seeds.
- **[major] The guards are hand-waved where the literature predicts a cost.** The low band carries most of the prompt alignment and layout in FDG [2506.19713], and early guidance at high noise sets composition in Interval Guidance [2404.07724]. Switching guidance off early lowered both FLOW and CLIPScore on this very model [2603.14073]. The withheld cell is also where the subject's shared appearance lives. "Within a small margin of CFG" needs a number, and the matched-dynamics comparison needs a Pareto plot across w.
- **[minor] Statistics.** The main pool uses one seed per prompt over about 410 prompts. Dynamic degree is a per-video binary at a base rate of roughly 65–83%, so the paired confidence interval is about ±3–4 points, the same order as the predicted effect. The human study (150 judgements) compares only with CFG, not with the strongest baseline.
- **[minor] The analogy and terms are imprecise.** "Texture sticking" in StyleGAN3 [2106.12423], and in diffusion with frame-fixed noise [2504.03072], is a fine-detail phenomenon. The proposal withholds the coarse band and keeps the fine frame-shared cell at w, so the failure being fixed is subject position, not texture. The in-domain diffusion precedent [2504.03072] is not cited at all.
- **[minor]** SpecLoR is characterised as keeping or lowering motion, but it raised dynamic degree on MovieGen-Bench (0.754 to 0.782) [2606.11969]. The title is too long to remember.

## Overlapping prior and concurrent work
- [2506.19713] FDG. Separate CFG gains per band of any linear invertible transform, applied in x0 space. UnStick-CFG is an instance with a temporal-DC split plus a step window. This **kills method novelty**, which the authors concede; the novelty that survives is the temporal axis, the video and positional framing, and the control design.
- [2605.19398] DyMoS. Early, conditional-branch-only suppression of reference-frame dominance raises I2V motion, so it owns the principle that early frame-shared content suppresses motion. It is frame-level and I2V only, and it does not touch the CFG difference. Partial overlap with the insight; **does not kill**.
- [2502.06764] History Guidance. Guidance on copyable full-band shared content makes video static, and restricting which band is guided restores dynamics. This is a conceptual precursor on the band axis, using a different model class (DFoT). **Does not kill**.
- [2506.08456] ALG. Early low-passing of the I2V conditioning image prevents a static shortcut. Same "early shared content locks the trajectory" family, but needs a conditioning image. **Does not kill**.
- [2504.03072] How I Warped Your Noise. Diffusion inputs that are identical at the same screen coordinates in every frame (fixed noise) cause sticking, which whole-frame coherence metrics reward. This is in-domain support for the premise, mostly for fine detail, and it is missing from the related work. **Does not kill**.
- [2609.23658] The RoPE anchoring analysis, the source of the mechanism. It covers only the conditional branch, uses case-study evidence and defines the winner post hoc. It makes the guided-sampler extension new, but it also limits how strongly the probe can test it. **Does not kill**.
- [2610.14153] TrajCFG (concurrent, Oct 2026). A zero-NFE per-token CFG rule that raises dynamic degree on Wan2.1-1.3B. It works on a different axis (per token rather than along time) and is a direct competitor in the results table. **Does not kill**.
- [2603.14073] MotionCFG. An early-window CFG modification for motion on Wan2.1-1.3B, the strongest motion competitor; it also supplies the evidence that Interval Guidance lowers FLOW. **Does not kill**.
- [2606.11969] SpecLoR. Rectifies the 3D (temporal × spatial) amplitude spectrum of the early clean estimate, which includes the temporal-DC low-frequency bins that CFG inflates. It also reports a conflict with high CFG. It is the closest "early spatio-temporal spectral correction", yet it is not a baseline. **Does not kill**.
- My recency searches (since 2025-10-18) found nothing that decomposes the text-CFG difference along time in T2V. The closest new items are "Temporal Specification Guidance" (temporal-logic attention guidance) and "Motion Concept Unlearning in Video Diffusion Models" (motion-concept erasure in the CFG difference). Both are on different problems; I checked them at abstract level only (not read) and do not claim overlap.

## Missing baselines or experiments
1. **A flow-matched control for the mechanism probe.** Tune another booster (MotionCFG, TrajCFG, or a frame-varying perturbation of matched norm) to the same flow gain on the same seeds. Then show that only UnStick-CFG lowers same-position MC at matched flow. Alternatively, use a winner criterion that does not depend on the final trajectory.
2. **A norm-matched scalar control.** In the window, use a scalar w′ chosen so that the per-step change from CFG has the same norm as UnStick-CFG's. This separates "this particular cell" from "less early guidance energy"; FDG along time and guidance-off sit only at the extremes.
3. **A reference pair for the interaction test.** Run UnStick+TrajCFG or UnStick+MotionCFG, which the account predicts to be additive, next to UnStick+RoPE-λ. Read the test on unbounded RAFT flow with a per-seed paired interaction model, not on dynamic degree.
4. **SpecLoR-Zero** [2606.11969], **STG** [2411.18664] and **Enhance-A-Video** [2502.07508] as standard training-free arms on Wan. **Practitioner knobs** as baselines: lower w, Wan's sample shift, and the Wan default negative prompt, which already contains "static" terms. The proposal should say which negative prompt the w = 6 baseline uses.
5. **Diagnostics before the headline results.** Report ‖(I − M)LD‖ / ‖MLD‖ and ‖Δx_t‖ / ‖x_t‖ per window step. If the frame-varying cell is negligible, pillar 1 cannot hold, and the authors should know that on day 1.
6. **Unselected seeds for every pilot claim**, and at least 3 seeds per prompt for the three key arms (CFG, FDG along time, UnStick-CFG) in the main pool.
7. **One current backbone.** Wan2.2-TI2V-5B fits in 24 GB, and TrajCFG already reports on Wan2.2-5B [2610.14153]. Alternatively, Wan2.1-14B with offloading on a 100-prompt subset. "Works on 1.3B" alone will be read as a small-model artefact.
8. **A Pareto plot** of flow against VideoAlign TA/VQ across w for CFG, UnStick-CFG, MotionCFG and TrajCFG. The human 2AFC should include the strongest baseline, not only CFG.

## Questions for the authors
1. In steps 1–5, what fraction of ‖LD‖ is frame-varying? If early estimates are close to frame-constant [2502.02492], how can UnStick-CFG differ measurably from FDG along time?
2. How is the "winner away from the earliest-committed frame" defined? If, as in [2609.23658], it comes from the final trajectory, what probe outcome would be inconsistent with "the method simply adds motion"?
3. Why does sub-additivity with RoPE-λ show a shared mechanism rather than a shared ceiling on near-static seeds?
4. How will you separate RoPE dependence from headroom, given that CogVideoX-2B already has the highest dynamics among small models?
5. Does the gain hold on seeds not selected for low CFG flow?
6. What numerical alignment drop (VideoAlign TA, ViCLIP) would you accept? Have you checked for subject-missing or weak-identity failures, given that the subject's shared appearance sits in the withheld cell?
7. Is g_s = 1 kept fixed as w grows? Does the monotone flow-versus-w claim survive if g_s is set relative to w?
8. Your account and a simpler SNR account (frame-shared content is the only thing visible at σ ≈ 0.98) make different predictions on CogVideoX-2B. Which result would you accept as refuting RoPE anchoring while the method still works?

## Rating
- rating: 3
- confidence: 4
- p_core: 0.3. The load-bearing prediction is pillar 1: more flow and dynamic degree than both CFG and FDG along time. Three things work against it. The arms differ only in the frame-varying coarse cell, which is probably small at σ ≈ 0.98. Early guidance-off already lowered FLOW on this model [2603.14073]. And the pilot's CFG comparison is inflated by seed selection. The I2V evidence that early frame-shared content suppresses motion [2605.19398][2506.08456][2502.06764] keeps the probability from being low.
- one_point_fix: Add a flow-matched control arm, another booster tuned to the same flow gain on the same seeds, plus a norm-matched in-window scalar, and restate pillar 2 as "only UnStick-CFG lowers same-position MC at matched flow, with winners defined independently of the final trajectory". This turns the causal account, the paper's stated contribution, from a near-tautology into a real test.
