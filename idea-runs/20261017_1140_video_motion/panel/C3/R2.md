# R2 (generalist) — C3 "Unsticking the Subject" / UnStick-CFG — CVPR 2027

## Summary
UnStick-CFG is a one-line, zero-extra-NFE change to classifier-free guidance in text-to-video DiTs. In the first ~5 of 50 steps, the part of the guidance difference that is both spatially coarse and identical in every frame (temporal mean of the low-pass band) gets gain 1 instead of w, and plain CFG is used everywhere else. The claimed insight is that CFG's extrapolation pastes a screen-fixed copy of the prompt's average layout into every frame, that RoPE spatial anchoring rewards this agreement at zero spatial offset, and that subjects therefore stay at fixed screen coordinates (the StyleGAN3 "texture sticking" analogy). The predicted evidence, mainly on Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B, is:
- more RAFT flow and dynamic degree than both CFG and a whole-coarse-band control ("FDG along time") at equal cost;
- lower mutual consistency of same-position candidates;
- sub-additivity with RoPE-λ, and a larger gain on RoPE backbones than on sinusoidal CogVideoX-2B;
- flow that rises monotonically with w;
- gains concentrated on high-dynamics, fixed-camera prompts;
- alignment and identity guards that hold.

## Strengths
- **The thesis is crisp and easy to remember.** "Guidance stamps the same blurry layout at the same spot in every frame, and we stop amplifying only that" can be explained in one slide. The long-exposure picture and the texture-sticking analogy will stay with a reader outside the sub-area.
- **The method is about as simple as it gets.** It is one subtraction per step in the window, with no LLM, no hooks, no training and no extra forward pass. If it works, people will adopt it, and it composes with other CFG variants by construction.
- **The authors are honest about where the novelty lies.** They say the method is close to FDG applied along time [2506.19713] and that DyMoS owns the general principle [2605.19398]. They stake the paper on an explicit control arm with a written kill condition, and they plan a pilot with a stop rule. Reviewers trust that kind of framing.
- **The evaluation anticipates the "static video scores well" trap.** It uses DEVIL-graded prompts and D_control [2407.01094], quality at matched dynamics, static prompts as a negative control, and a camera-split prompt set that separates sticking from legitimate tracking. The temporal-average-frame sharpness test, borrowed from StyleGAN3's averaged-image diagnostic [2106.12423], is a good idea that readers will remember.
- **The exactness property is clean and falsifiable.** The change is identical in every frame, so the guided estimate's inter-frame differences equal CFG's. This rules out ghosting or cross-fade artefacts by construction and makes failures easy to interpret.
- **The budget is realistic.** About 23 of the 40 GPU-days are planned on hardware the team already has.

## Weaknesses
- **[major] The paper's distinguishing claim is the mechanism, but most mechanism pillars would pass at threshold even if the account were wrong.** The method delta over existing rules is small by the authors' own admission, so the paper is sold on the causal story (CFG feeds RoPE anchoring). At threshold, the supporting tests are weak in kind:
  - **(a) The positional-encoding contrast is confounded.** CogVideoX-2B and 5B differ in size, data and training, not only in sinusoidal versus 3D-RoPE positions. CogVideoX-2B is also already the most dynamic of the small models, with the lowest motion smoothness [2502.02492]. A ceiling effect alone would produce "a smaller gain on 2B" and confirm prediction (iv) for the wrong reason.
  - **(b) Sub-additivity with RoPE-λ is expected from almost any two motion boosters.** Diminishing returns near a common ceiling produce it, so it does not single out a shared mechanism.
  - **(c) The candidate and mutual-consistency probe is inherited from one interpretability study.** That study used a single basketball prompt and seed on the conditional branch only [2609.23658], and here the probe runs only on the 50 selected pilot seeds.

  Only prediction (i), the comparison with FDG along time, really discriminates. A reader will come away convinced that "the temporal split matters", but not that RoPE anchoring is the reason.
- **[major] As written, the story nearly predicts its own kill condition.** Prediction (viii) says the coarse frame-identical cell dominates the guidance energy in steps 1–5, which fits the evidence that early predictions are appearance-dominated and close to frame-shuffle invariant [2502.02492][2506.01144]. But the only difference between UnStick-CFG and FDG along time is (w − 1)·(I − M)·L·D, the frame-varying coarse cell. The more the premise holds, the smaller that cell and the closer the two arms become.

  The in-domain evidence also leans against the premise:
  - On Wan2.1-1.3B, withholding early guidance wholesale (Interval Guidance) lowered FLOW from 4.10 to 3.76 [2603.14073].
  - Ablating the heads that write the most condition-induced content changed object size, not trajectory [2609.23658].

  The paper needs to explain why full gain on a small residual cell decides the competition, and to report ‖(I − M)LD‖/‖LD‖ per step. Without that, the story will not hold together for a reader without the authors in the room.
- **[major] The significance and scope are narrow for a CVPR main-track audience.** All primary evidence comes from one 1.3B model from early 2025, and transfer is tested only on older CogVideoX and LTX-Video. Under-animation is asserted as "the common failure", yet Wan2.1-1.3B scores 65–83 dynamic degree on VBench depending on protocol [2610.14153][2506.01144]. The pilot mines the 50 lowest-flow outputs out of ~600, which is the tail. A current model that is more under-animated is missing: Wan2.2-TI2V-5B in T2V mode has dynamic degree 55.56 [2610.14153], and recent training-free samplers already evaluate it that way [2610.14153][2610.20822]. Without such a model, reviewers who have seen many 2026 papers on training-free motion boosting will file this as "another CFG tweak on Wan-1.3B".
- **[major] At threshold, the headline table probably does not show the method as the best motion row.** The predictions only require beating CFG and FDG along time within confidence intervals. Nothing is predicted against MotionCFG, TrajCFG, CFG-Zero* or RoPE-λ, although all of them appear in the baseline list. The bar on this model is low: MotionCFG adds only about +16% FLOW on Wan2.1-1.3B [2603.14073]. Still, a paper whose method sits mid-table on motion must win on the mechanism, which brings back the first weakness. The composition arm (UnStick-CFG + TrajCFG) could be the practical selling point, but no prediction is stated for it.
- **[minor] Selecting pilot seeds on failure biases the comparison with CFG.** The pilot seeds are chosen as CFG's lowest-flow outputs, so any perturbation that re-routes the trajectory will look better than CFG on them through regression to the mean. "The winner moves away from the earliest-committed position" is likewise partly guaranteed by the selection. FDG along time shares this benefit and partly controls for it. A matched-energy random frame-identical perturbation would control for it fully.
- **[minor] The window may be too short to change the state much.** With Wan's default shift of 5.0 and 50 FlowUniPC steps [2609.23658, App. B], steps 1–5 traverse only about 2% of the noise-to-data interpolation (σ′ from 1 to about 0.978; my own computation). The RoPE-λ precedent in the same window [2609.23658] makes an effect plausible. Still, windows should be stated in σ, and the paper should show how large the resulting change in x_t is.
- **[minor] The motivating example is ambiguous.** "A dog trotting in place while the sand slides behind it" is a valid tracking shot, not obviously a failure. The headline example should be a fixed-camera prompt. The camera-split set already exists for this purpose.
- **[minor] Presentation needs tightening.**
  - The title has 24 words of jargon; "Unsticking the Subject in Text-to-Video Guidance" says the same thing.
  - Several "theory" sentences carry less than they appear to. "At w = 6 the extrapolated part is five times the branches' own gap" is just w − 1 = 5 and holds equally for every cell, so it does not show that this cell matters more.
  - The StyleGAN3 analogy is good as intuition, but the paper itself notes it breaks down: there the positional reference carried no content, while here the withheld term is the prompt's own layout. It should not be presented as evidence.
- **[minor] The story's foundation is a stack of very recent preprints.** The anchoring mechanism [2609.23658], TrajCFG [2610.14153] and DyMoS [2605.19398] are all from 2026. Before building on the anchoring analysis, the paper should reproduce it on its own CFG baseline, for example the "stationary basketball" case.

## Overlapping prior and concurrent work
- **[2506.19713] FDG.** Separate gains for the low and high spatial bands of the guidance, under a rule that allows any linear invertible transform. Laplacian pyramid × temporal DC is such a transform, so UnStick-CFG is formally a special case. This does not kill novelty, since FDG is image-only and never splits along time, but it makes the method contribution thin, as the authors admit.
- **[2605.19398] DyMoS.** In I2V, early frame-shared reference content suppresses motion, and reducing it early in the conditional branch restores motion. It owns the general principle. It is position-independent and needs a reference frame, so it does not kill the T2V-CFG instance.
- **[2506.08456] ALG and [2502.06764] History Guidance.** In both, copyable shared content makes video static. Both fixes keep the coarse band under full guidance and release the fine band, which is the opposite of UnStick-CFG's band choice. This is not overlap but a tension a generalist will notice. The authors' answer ("a conditioning input can be placed anywhere, while the CFG term is pinned to coordinates") is plausible but untested. The fine-band ablation addresses it only in part.
- **[2609.23658] RoPE anchoring and RoPE-λ.** This is the source of the mechanism and the only competing lever on anchoring. It analyses the conditional branch only, so the CFG link is new. No kill.
- **[2603.14073] MotionCFG and [2610.14153] TrajCFG.** Zero-cost CFG variants that raise motion on the same model. They compete for the same table and do not kill novelty. MotionCFG's Interval Guidance numbers are evidence against the premise (see Weaknesses).
- **[2405.14864] MOFT.** Treats the temporal mean of video-diffusion features as content and the residual as motion, and steers sampling by latent optimisation toward a reference. It shows that the frame-mean/residual split is a standard tool, so the decomposition is not novel. The use is different (features, a motion target, no change to CFG), so no kill.
- **[2610.20822] TSG (concurrent, Oct 2026).** Adds an attention-steered temporal-logic direction on top of intact CFG, at the cost of a third pass. It targets the timing of concepts, not stasis, so there is no overlap. It found nothing closer, which supports the novelty of splitting the CFG difference itself along time.
- **[2404.07724] Interval Guidance.** Withholding all early guidance is the coarse ancestor of the method. Its Wan2.1-1.3B result (lower FLOW and lower CLIPScore [2603.14073]) is the strongest prior against the idea.

My recency searches (since 2025-10-18) on CFG plus video motion, frequency guidance for video, and guidance plus static video found no paper that splits the T2V guidance difference into frame-mean and residual parts.

## Missing baselines or experiments
- **A within-model anchoring dose-response in place of the confounded cross-model contrast.** Measure UnStick-CFG's gain over CFG on Wan2.1-1.3B across RoPE-λ ∈ {1.0, 0.85, 0.70, 0.55}, with a stated prediction that the gain shrinks monotonically toward zero as anchoring is weakened. This is the clean causal test that the CogVideoX-2B/5B comparison cannot be.
- **A matched-energy control.** Replace the withheld term with a random frame-identical coarse perturbation of equal norm. This separates "the prompt's own pinned layout glues the subject" from "any early shared-coarse perturbation (more layout diversity, cf. FDG's diversity finding [2506.19713]) adds motion".
- **One current, more under-animated backbone.** Wan2.2-TI2V-5B in T2V mode, offloaded [2610.14153][2610.20822], in place of or alongside CogVideoX-2B.
- **Per-step report of the frame-varying coarse cell's size,** ‖(I − M)LD‖/‖LD‖, next to ρ_k and s_k.
- **Windows stated in σ, not step indices,** with the size of the resulting change in x_t.
- **A stated prediction for the composition arm** (UnStick-CFG + TrajCFG versus TrajCFG alone).
- **Per-seed paired win and loss rates on the unselected pool,** showing that seeds that already move are not harmed.
- **Some of the ~150 human judgements spent on UnStick-CFG versus the strongest motion baseline,** not only versus CFG.
- **Results with an empty negative prompt.** Wan's default negative prompt includes anti-static terms; that is my recollection of the public release, not verified in a paper. MotionCFG found the negative prompt mattered little [2603.14073], but the effect should be shown to hold without it.

## Questions for the authors
1. If the coarse frame-identical cell dominates D in steps 1–5, as you predict, the frame-varying coarse cell is small. What mechanism makes full gain on that small cell decide the competition, and what value of ‖(I − M)LD‖/‖MLD‖ do you expect?
2. How will you tell RoPE dependence apart from CogVideoX-2B already being highly dynamic (a ceiling effect)? Would you accept a within-model λ dose-response as the real test?
3. Why does sub-additivity with RoPE-λ indicate a shared mechanism rather than diminishing returns near a motion ceiling? Would an additive outcome on saturating metrics really falsify the account?
4. Under shift 5.0, steps 1–5 move σ′ only from 1 to about 0.98. How large is the actual change to x_t, and does the effect persist if the window is defined by σ on a backbone with a different schedule?
5. On the fixed-camera half of the camera-split set, how often does plain CFG actually stick? If it rarely does, is the failure you target mostly the tracking-shot reading?
6. History Guidance and ALG both found that keeping coarse shared content under full guidance is compatible with motion. What result would show that your positional argument, not the band choice, explains the difference?
7. Does UnStick-CFG change layout diversity across seeds, for example in first-frame DINO or LPIPS diversity, and how do you separate "more motion" from "more varied commitments"?
