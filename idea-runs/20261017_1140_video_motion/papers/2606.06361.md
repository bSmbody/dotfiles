# Physics in 2-Steps: Locking Motion Priors Before Visual Refinement Erases Them

## What this paper does
PhaseLock (ICML 2026) is a training-free sampler for image-to-video models. It observes that a 2-step generation often has better physical consistency than the 50-step output of the same model and attributes this to phase erosion during denoising. It runs a cheap few-step pass from the same initial noise, takes the inter-frame latent difference of that output as a motion prior, and during the first half of the full 50-step run pushes the current latent's inter-frame differences toward that prior (Latent Delta Guidance), keeping the first (image-anchor) frame fixed. Evaluated mainly on CogVideoX-5B I2V, plus Wan 2.1 and LTX-Video, on Physics-IQ, PhyGenBench and VBench.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does PhaseLock's Latent Delta Guidance decompose a guidance (CFG) difference into frame-shared and frame-varying parts, or otherwise change the CFG combination rule?
No. The operator T(z) = z_{2:F} − z_{1:F−1} is applied to the sampling state, not to the conditional-minus-unconditional difference. The guidance signal is G^(k) = M^prior − T(z^(k)), with M^prior = T(z^few) from a K_few = 2 step pass, and the update is z^(k)_{2:F} ← z^(k)_{2:F} + λ(k)·G^(k), leaving frame 1 untouched as the image anchor [full text, Section 4.1–4.3 / Eq 1–2]. λ(k) decays linearly from λ0 = 0.05 over k ∈ [0, K_full/2) [Eq 3; Section 5.1 Implementation details]. CFG itself is left as in the backbone defaults [Section 5.1]. The authors say they avoid explicit low-frequency band injection or phase substitution because these "often induce high-frequency artifacts and spatial incoherence" [Section 4 opening].

### Goal: Which task and backbones does it target, and what is it optimising?
Image-to-video: the first frame is the conditioning anchor and is never modified [Section 4.3]. Backbones are CogVideoX-5B (primary), Wan 2.1 (81 frames, 50 steps) and LTX-Video (121 frames, 50 steps), on one H100 [Section 5.1]. The target is physical consistency (Physics-IQ, PhyGenBench), with VBench quality dimensions as guards [Section 5.2]. It does not target motion amount in pure text-to-video and does not report a T2V dynamic-degree result in the sections read.
