# Spatiotemporal Skip Guidance for Enhanced Video Diffusion Sampling

## What this paper does
STG is a training-free guidance term for transformer video diffusion models (Mochi, Open-Sora, SVD). It builds an implicit "aligned weak model" by skipping a spatiotemporal layer (residual skip) or replacing attention with identity (attention skip) and extrapolates away from that weak prediction, ε + w(ε − ε^{s,t}), usually on top of CFG. The goal is better frame quality and temporal consistency without the diversity and dynamics loss attributed to CFG; optional rescaling/restart keep samples on-manifold.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism does it use to change motion amplitude or motion quality relative to standard classifier-free guidance?
STG adds a second guidance term whose negative is a self-perturbed copy of the model: one layer is skipped (STG-R, Eq 15) or its attention matrix is replaced by identity (STG-A, Eq 17), giving ε̃ = ε + w(ε − ε^{s,t}) [full text, Section 4.3 / Algorithm 1]; for factorized models spatial and temporal perturbations get separate scales w1, w2 [Algorithm 2]. The argument is that CFG's unconditional branch differs from the conditional one "not only in quality but also in aspects such as diversity and prompt alignment", whereas a layer-skipped weak model is "aligned" and differs mainly in quality [Section 1, Fig 2, Section 4.1]. It is used together with CFG in all evaluations [Section 5.1], so each step costs three forward passes.

### Goal: What does it report about its effect on dynamic degree / flow magnitude, and about how the guidance scale interacts with motion?
The abstract and Section 1 state that CFG improves quality but reduces diversity and motion. For T2V, STG explicitly aims to keep Dynamic Degree unchanged ("as it may not correlate with video quality") and does so: Mochi 0.87 → 0.86, Open-Sora 0.902 → 0.895 [Section 5.2, Table 1]. Only in I2V (SVD) does dynamic degree rise (0.562 → 0.694), explained as STG counteracting CFG's over-adherence to the conditioning image [Section 5.2, Table 2, Appendix A4 / Fig 22]. Higher CFG scales raise Imaging Quality but worsen FVD (lower diversity), while STG scales do not [Fig 4].

### Goal: What method assumption limits it for controlling motion amplitude under CFG (target signal, per-model or per-prompt tuning, cost, applicability)?
Its weak-model direction is a quality direction by construction, so it has no mechanism, and no stated intent, to raise motion amplitude in T2V [Section 5.2]. Configuration is model-specific and heuristic: skipped layer 8/16 for SVD, 12/28 for Open-Sora, 35/48 for Mochi; STG-R for Mochi but STG-A for the others because residual skips cause OOD activations in factorized models [Appendix A1.3, Section 5.3, A3.5]; the limitation section states that scale and layer "vary across models, requiring users to set these through heuristic tuning" [Section 6]. It adds one extra NFE per step on top of CFG [Algorithm 1].
