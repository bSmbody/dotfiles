# Temporal Specification Guidance for Text-to-Video Generation (TSG)

## What this paper does
A training-free sampler (October 2026) that turns temporal-logic specifications (ordering, timing and persistence of concepts) into a guidance direction for T2V models. Per-latent-frame concept-presence traces come from video-text cross-attention. They are scored with a differentiable MTL semantics (LogSTOP), and the score's gradients become attention-logit biases in an extra conditional pass. That pass's prediction is added to CFG. It is evaluated on five models, including Wan2.1-1.3B and Wan2.2-TI2V-5B in T2V mode, with a 220-prompt benchmark of temporal constraints.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does TSG modify the CFG combination rule by decomposing the guidance difference along time, or address under-animation or subject sticking?
No. The update is d_tsg = d_cfg + λ_tsg(d_φ − d_c), where d_φ comes from an extra, attention-steered conditional forward pass. λ_tsg is set from the norm ratio of the scaled CFG contribution to the TSG contribution [full text, Section 4 / Eq 1]. The CFG term itself is left intact. Steering adds per-latent-frame logit biases on concept tokens, broadcast over all spatial queries of a frame [Section 4, Online attention steering / Eq 3]. The target is the timing of concept appearance (ordered entry, state transitions), not motion amount or screen-space position. It costs a third forward pass, about one extra minute per video on Wan2.1-1.3B [Section 5.3, Result 2]. It runs Wan2.2-TI2V-5B in text-to-video mode [Section 5, Fig. 5 caption].
