# Enhance-A-Video: Better Generated Video for Free

## What this paper does
Training-free enhancement for DiT video models (CogVideoX, HunyuanVideo, Open-Sora, LTX-Video). It computes Cross-Frame Intensity (mean of off-diagonal temporal attention weights), multiplies it by an "enhance temperature" (clipped at ≥1), and uses it to scale the temporal attention output in the residual connection, strengthening cross-frame information flow. Targets temporal consistency and visual quality; VBench gains are small and user studies favour it.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism does it use to change motion amplitude or motion quality relative to standard classifier-free guidance?
It modifies the network, not the guidance rule. CFI = mean of off-diagonal entries of the F×F temporal attention map [full text, Section 3.4 / Eq 6]; CFI_enhanced = clip((τ + F)·CFI, 1) [Eq 7]; the attention output is rescaled, O_final = CFI_enhanced · O_attn + H [Eq 8]. Directly raising softmax temperature instead "can lead to overly smooth motion, loss of visual details" [Section 3.3]. The goal is stronger cross-frame correlation (consistency), not larger motion.

### Goal: What does it report about its effect on dynamic degree / flow magnitude, and about how the guidance scale interacts with motion?
VBench totals change marginally (CogVideoX 77.27 → 77.34, Open-Sora 79.04 → 79.16, LTX-Video 71.93 → 72.04) [Section 4.4 / Table 1]; the user study reports preference on temporal consistency, prompt consistency and visual quality [Fig 10]. Moderate temperature balances consistency and "visual diversity", extremes degrade [Fig 11]. No dynamic-degree or flow-magnitude analysis and no interaction with the CFG scale are reported.

### Goal: What method assumption limits it for controlling motion amplitude under CFG (target signal, per-model or per-prompt tuning, cost, applicability)?
The authors state the improvement is limited because "the temperature parameter require[s] different optimal values for each prompt" and propose future RLHF-based adaptive temperature [Appendix E]. The scalar is open-loop and prompt-agnostic; it only scales cross-frame aggregation and has no notion of a target motion amount.
