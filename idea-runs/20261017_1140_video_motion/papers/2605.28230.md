# Proprio: Latent Self-Scoring and Inference-Time Refinement for Physically Plausible Video Generation

## What this paper does
A training-free framework in which a frozen video generator scores its own outputs. A generated latent z0 is re-perturbed at several scheduler timesteps with several noise draws. The generator's flow-matching residual ||v̂(z_t,t,c) − (ε − z0)||² is measured, optionally masked to motion regions, and aggregated across timesteps with inverse-variance weights. The score drives best-of-N search, gradient-based refinement of a per-channel affine reparameterisation of the initial noise (μ, σ, with a KL term), or both. With TurboWan2.2 it improves Physics-IQ (32.2→37.5) and VideoPhy2-hard physical commonsense.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What self-scoring signal does Proprio compute from the frozen generator, on which sampling state, and does it encode the motion amount a prompt asks for?
The score is the flow residual r = v̂_θ(z_t,t,c) − (ε − z0) evaluated on a finished latent z0 that has been re-perturbed. Means over N noise draws per timestep are combined with inverse-variance weights, plus an optional motion-masked variant S_motion and hybrid S_hybrid [full text, Section 3.1-3.2, Eq. 4-11]. Every candidate must therefore be fully generated before it can be scored. Mid-level noise gives the most informative residuals [full text, Section 6]. The score measures consistency with the learned distribution. The authors stress that motion "magnitude alone is not a proxy for physical correctness" and show gains within motion-magnitude bins [full text, Appendix A.5, Table 9]. No term compares the amount or kind of motion with what the prompt implies.

### Goal: What does Proprio cost, and what limitations does it state?
Search scales linearly with the number of candidates. Search plus refinement is the most expensive T2V setting [full text, Appendix A.3, Table 7]. Refinement experiments "rely on distilled models for computational tractability", because backpropagating through long denoising trajectories of non-distilled generators is costly. Proprio also "cannot recover dynamics that the model has not learned" [full text, Section 7]. In T2V, search beats refinement because candidates differ substantially in scene and motion [full text, Section 6].
