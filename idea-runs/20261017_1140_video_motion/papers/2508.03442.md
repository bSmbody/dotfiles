# MAMBO-G: Magnitude-Aware Mitigation for Boosted Guidance

## What this paper does
Training-free, state-dependent CFG scale for flow models (SD3.5, Lumina, Qwen-Image, Wan2.1 1.3B/14B). It measures r_t = ‖v(c) − v(∅)‖ / ‖v(∅)‖ at each step and damps the guidance scale exponentially in r_t, w(r_t) = 1 + (w_max − 1)exp(−α r_t), which mainly reduces guidance in early high-noise steps where r_t peaks. Motivated by stability and faster convergence (fewer steps for the same quality); evaluated on video only with VBench imaging and aesthetic quality.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism does it use to change motion amplitude or motion quality relative to standard classifier-free guidance?
It replaces the fixed CFG scale with a function of the sampler state: the ratio r_t of guidance-difference norm to unconditional-velocity norm [full text, Section 3.2 / Eq 5] sets w(r_t) = 1 + (w_max − 1)exp(−α r_t) [Section 3.4 / Eq 6], fitted from a greedy search of ImageReward-optimal scales versus r_t [Fig 6]. r_t is a single global number per sample and step; the authors argue that time-only schedules w(t) ignore large per-sample variation of r_t [Section 3.3]. The damping is strongest at initialization where r_t peaks [Section 3.2, Fig 4].

### Goal: What does it report about its effect on dynamic degree / flow magnitude, and about how the guidance scale interacts with motion?
For Wan2.1 it reports VBench Imaging and Aesthetic Quality only, with Wan 1.3B + MAMBO-G exceeding CFG Wan 14B on these [Section 4.2, Fig 8]. The paper contains no motion, temporal or dynamic-degree measurement, although its damping acts in exactly the early steps where coarse layout forms [Section 3.2].

### Goal: What method assumption limits it for controlling motion amplitude under CFG (target signal, per-model or per-prompt tuning, cost, applicability)?
The control signal is a global magnitude ratio designed to flag "unstable" updates [Section 3.2]; it does not distinguish which part of the guidance difference is temporal and which is frame-shared, and has no motion target. α and w_max are calibrated by grid search against an image reward [Section 3.4, Fig 6]. Thus it is a closed-loop CFG scale, but the loop is closed on stability, not on motion.
