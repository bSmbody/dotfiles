# Eliminating Oversaturation and Artifacts of High Guidance Scales in Diffusion Models

## What this paper does
Adaptive Projected Guidance (APG, ICLR 2025): rewrites CFG as D(c) + (w − 1)ΔD on the denoised prediction, decomposes the update ΔD into components parallel and orthogonal to D(c), shows the parallel part mainly causes over-saturation while the orthogonal part improves quality, and down-weights the parallel part (η ≤ 1). Adds norm-clipping (rescaling) and negative momentum on the update, from a gradient-ascent view of CFG. Evaluated on image diffusion models; no extra cost.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism does it use to change motion amplitude or motion quality relative to standard classifier-free guidance?
APG is a decomposition of the CFG update, not a motion method. ΔD_t = D(z_t, y) − D(z_t, y_null) [full text, Section 4 / Eq 3] is split by orthogonal projection onto D(z_t, y) into ΔD^∥ and ΔD^⊥ [Eq 4]; the update uses ΔD^⊥ + η ΔD^∥ with η ≤ 1, because the parallel part acts as a gain that pushes values to extremes [Eq 5]. It further clips ‖ΔD‖ to radius r [Eq 6] and adds reverse momentum [Section 4]. The projection must be done on the denoised prediction, not on ε [Section 5.2].

### Goal: What does it report about its effect on dynamic degree / flow magnitude, and about how the guidance scale interacts with motion?
All experiments are on images; the paper reports FID, recall, precision and saturation, and contains no video or motion evaluation [Section 5]. It shows that the CFG update can be split into components with different perceptual effects: guiding with only ΔD^∥ "barely changes" quality and only adds saturation, while ΔD^⊥ alone gives the quality gain [Fig 2].

### Goal: What method assumption limits it for controlling motion amplitude under CFG (target signal, per-model or per-prompt tuning, cost, applicability)?
The decomposition axis is defined by the current denoised prediction (a saturation/gain direction), which is meaningful for per-pixel intensity but has no temporal structure; applied to a video latent it treats all frames as one vector, so it cannot separate guidance that changes motion from guidance that changes per-frame appearance. η, r and β are fixed hyperparameters [Section 4].
