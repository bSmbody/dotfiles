# How I Warped Your Noise: a Temporally-Correlated Noise Prior for Diffusion Models

## What this paper does
ICLR 2024 (oral). Lifts pretrained image diffusion models (SDEdit, I2SB, PIDM, fluid super-resolution, and a latent-diffusion study) to video by changing only the per-frame noise prior. Independent noise per frame flickers; one fixed noise sample reused for every frame causes texture sticking. The paper proposes ∫-noise, which treats each discrete noise sample as the integral of an infinite-resolution Gaussian field, and a distribution-preserving transport that advects the noise along optical flow, so that frames are correlated the way the content moves while each frame stays white Gaussian. It is not a T2V method and does not touch guidance.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: Does it show, in diffusion, that an input held identical at the same screen coordinates in every frame makes generated content stick to the screen, and how is that diagnosed?
Yes. A "Fixed Noise prior ... reuses a same fixed set of noise samples for all frames", and "the problem of fixed noise signals manifests as texture-sticking artifacts that are oblivious to post-process techniques" [full text, Section 1; Section 3.2 Baselines]. Examples: with fixed noise "the original fold in the corner of the bed stays at the same position in the image even after the bed rotates" [Appendix D.1, Fig. 12]; PIDM gives "obvious cloth texture-sticking artifacts" [Section 3.2, Fig. 2b]; in fluid super-resolution, fixed noise "suffers from sticking artifacts (vertical lines in the slice)" [Section 3.2, Fig. 7]. The diagnosis is visual, via x–t slices in which sticking shows as stripes [Appendix D.2, Fig. 16–17]. A quantitative warp-error metric over the whole frame actually favoured fixed noise, because "most of the background has zero motion" [Appendix D.3], so standard coherence metrics reward sticking.

### Goal: Which spatial band sticks, and does the effect carry over to latent (VAE) diffusion and to subject position rather than texture?
The sticking is mainly fine detail. In the latent-diffusion study, "keeping the noise fixed still induces sticking artifacts of the high-frequency details" (salami slices, crust spots stay put) even with cross-frame attention and feature injection [Appendix E.3, Fig. 20]. In latent models the noise "does not contribute directly to the fine details of the results in image space", so the benefit of noise warping shrinks [Appendix E.1]. The paper does not study the coarse position of whole subjects, text-to-video DiTs, RoPE, or classifier-free guidance; the shared input is the noise, not a guidance term.
