# Self-Refining Video Sampling

## What this paper does
Treats a flow-matching video generator as a time-conditioned denoising autoencoder. At early noise levels (the "motion stage", t<0.2) it inserts up to K_f=3 Predict-and-Perturb (P&P) iterations per step: predict the clean latent, then re-noise it to the same t with fresh Gaussian noise. An uncertainty mask, built by thresholding (τ=0.25) the change between successive clean predictions, limits refinement to uncertain regions, which coincide with moving objects. No verifier or training is used. Evaluated on Wan2.2-A14B and Wan2.1-14B T2V, robotics I2V and VideoPhy2/PhyWorldBench. Humans prefer it over the default ODE sampler (73%) and FlowMo (70%), at about 1.5× NFE.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What is the in-trajectory refinement mechanism, in which denoising window is it applied, and at what NFE cost?
P&P: ẑ1 = D_θ(z_t, t) = z_t + (1−t)u_θ, then z_t ← t·ẑ1 + (1−t)ε, iterated K_f ≤ 3 times at a fixed t [full text, Section 4.2, Eq. 5-8]. The authors motivate it as "larger exploratory moves at early timesteps, thereby mitigating early lock-in ... where temporal dynamics such as motion and physics are largely determined in the first few steps". Applying it only for t<0.2 "suffices" [full text, Section 4.2]. The uncertainty mask is U = mean_c |D(z^{(k−1)}) − D(z^{(k)})| > τ, and confident regions keep the previous latent [full text, Section 4.3, Eq. 10-11, Alg. 1]. Cost is 60 NFE versus 40 (1.5×), "approximately 40%" more time by default [full text, Table 1; Appendix C].

### Goal: What does it report about its effect on temporal variance and motion, and what can it not do?
Iterative P&P is mode-seeking. In video this shows up as "reduced temporal variance", i.e. removing jitter and flicker, which the authors call "temporal mode-seeking" [full text, Section 6.2; Appendix B.5]. Because of cross-frame consistency, repeated P&P on video "act[s] as a local search that refines the latents, rather than a global resampling" [full text, Section 6.1]. Stated limitations include "Local-Search Behavior: ... For tasks such as maze solving, finding a good initial noise may be more effective than iterative refinement" and risk of over-refinement [full text, Appendix C]. More than 3 P&P updates with CFG over-saturate or simplify static regions [full text, Section 4.3, Fig. 9]. The objective contains no term about how much motion the prompt implies.
