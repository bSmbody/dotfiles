# TPD: Temporal Prior Decoupling for Text-to-Video Diffusion Models

## What this paper does
A training-free CFG-space method (July 2026) for prompts in which an early scene persists while a new event should emerge later ("a sandcastle ... a wave washes it away"). It names the failure Temporal Prior Suppression: the early-scene prior dominates the guidance across the whole temporal axis. GPT-4o splits the prompt into early and late segments, a transition point and prior strengths. A third branch conditioned on the early segment alone gives a suppressed direction a = ε_text − ε_counter. A frame- and timestep-selective lower bound on the guidance update's projection onto a is enforced by a closed-form minimal-energy projection. The method costs three branches per step and is evaluated mainly on Mochi.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: Does TPD split or gate the CFG update along the video's time axis, and with what signal, cost and target?
Yes, per frame, but not into frame-shared and inter-frame parts. The corrected update is δ* = δ_ref + μ(τ,i)·a_τ, with μ > 0 only when a_τᵀδ_ref < c(τ,i) [full text, Method, Eq 8–10]. The bound c(τ,i) = λ(τ,i)‖a_τ‖ uses a sigmoid over frame index centred at the LLM's transition point and a timestep factor (τ/T)^q, q = 2. This concentrates the correction "in the early, structure-forming denoising steps" and in late frames [Eq 11–13]. The added direction comes from a counterfactual prompt (a third NFE per step) and an LLM [Implementation Details]. The target is the realisation of a late-segment concept. Motion amplitude is not the target, and the paper reports no dynamic-degree or flow measurement [Quantitative Evaluation].

### Goal: What does TPD report about simply scaling the guidance update, and about the limits of guidance-space corrections?
In the ablation, "CFG Scale-Up: the structure never falls — scaling an update does not redirect it", and a fixed additive push ("Always-Add") also leaves the early scene intact [Fig. 3, Ablation Study]. The limitations state that a guidance-space intervention "mitigates rather than removes the pull of a dominant early scene" and "cannot manufacture a trajectory the backbone is unwilling to follow" [Appendix A]. Controlled results are on Mochi only. The claim that the method transfers across backbones rests on the formulation and on qualitative observations [Implementation Details; Appendix A].
