# TrajCFG: Token-Wise Classifier-Free Guidance via Denoising Trajectory Feedback

## What this paper does
A training-free CFG variant for flow-matching models (October 2026). At each step, every latent token gets its own guidance scale, set from how much that token's guided velocity changed direction over the previous one or two steps (cosine similarity mapped linearly to [s_min, s_max]; larger change, stronger guidance). It reuses the two standard CFG predictions, so it adds no model evaluations. Evaluated on text-to-video (Wan2.1-1.3B, Wan2.2-5B, Wan2.2-14B on VBench and Dynamic-Bench), image-to-video, text-to-audio-video, text-to-image and image-to-3D, against CFG-Zero, CFG-Ctrl, PAG and SRVS.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: How does TrajCFG assign guidance gains across a video latent, and does it decompose the guidance difference along time (frame-shared vs inter-frame)?
The scale for token p at step k is s_{k,p} = g(q_{k,p}), where q is the stabilised cosine between the guided velocities supplied to the solver at steps k−2 and k−1, and g is a clipped linear map from 1−q to [s_min, s_max] [full text, Section 4.2 / Eq 6–9]; a stride-2 comparison is fused with weights 0.8/0.2 [Eq 11]. The guided velocity is v⁻ + s_{k,p}(v⁺ − v⁻), with each scale shared across the channels of its token [Eq 10]. The method "operates on this flattened site axis without assuming a modality-specific latent geometry" [Section 4.2]. Thus each token's whole guidance difference gets one gain; nothing separates a frame-shared component from an inter-frame one, and there is no temporal filtering. The first two steps use the base scale.

### Goal: What does it report on motion / dynamic degree for T2V on Wan2.1-1.3B, and at what cost?
In the full VBench table, Wan2.1-1.3B dynamic degree rises from 65.28 (Base) to 70.83 (TrajCFG), against CFG-Zero 68.90, CFG-Ctrl 68.06, PAG 66.67 and SRVS 66.67; motion smoothness goes 98.00 → 98.12 and VBench Total 80.98 → 82.32 [Appendix F, Table 8]. On Wan2.2-5B dynamic degree goes 55.56 → 68.06 and on Wan2.2-14B 72.22 → 79.17 [Table 8]. Cost: it reuses "standard CFG predictions without additional model evaluations" [Abstract, Section 4.2]. The paper presents these as general quality gains; motion amplitude is not its stated target.
