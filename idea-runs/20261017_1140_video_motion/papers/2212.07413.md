# Towards Smooth Video Composition

## What this paper does
A StyleGAN-V-based video GAN (StyleSV). It first shows that texture sticking, known from image GANs, also occurs in video generation and interrupts the flow of content across frames; it adopts StyleGAN3's alias-free generator plus image pre-training to remove it without losing frame quality. It then adds a temporal-shift module in the discriminator for more realistic dynamics, and a B-spline motion representation with low-rank temporal modulation for smooth, non-repeating long videos. Evaluated on SkyTimelapse, Taichi-HD and YouTube Driving with FID/FVD.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: Does texture sticking occur in video generation, how is it seen, and what does removing the generator's dependence on absolute coordinates change?
Yes. "The texture sticking problem (i.e., some visual concepts are bound to their coordinates) also exists in video generation, interrupting the smooth flow of content across frames" [full text, Section 1]. Tracking pixels at fixed coordinates in StyleGAN-V output gives a "brush effect": "these pixels actually move little. Thus, the texture sticks to fixed coordinates", and "the pixel values are strongly anchored to the coordinates" [Section 2.2, Fig. 2]. Adopting StyleGAN3's alias-free operations, "which reduces the dependency on absolute pixel coordinates", makes the brush effect disappear and gives smoother frame transitions [Section 2.2, Config-B, Fig. 2b]. Direct adoption costs FID/FVD, which image pre-training then recovers [Section 2.2, Config-C, Table 1]. The fix is architectural and requires retraining; the paper does not consider diffusion models or sampling-time changes.
