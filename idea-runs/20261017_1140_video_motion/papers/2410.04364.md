# VideoGuide: Improving Video Diffusion Models without Training Through a Teacher's Guide

## What this paper does
Training-free temporal-consistency guidance: during the first few reverse steps, the current clean estimate is re-noised and denoised for τ further steps by a guiding video model (the model itself or an external one such as VideoCrafter2), and the resulting clean estimate is interpolated with the sampler's own estimate, followed by a low-pass filter that re-injects fresh high-frequency noise. Derived as a gradient step on a teacher-reconstruction loss. Improves subject/background consistency on AnimateDiff and LaVie, with reduced dynamic degree.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism does it use to change motion amplitude or motion quality relative to standard classifier-free guidance?
VideoGuide minimises a teacher-reconstruction loss on the clean estimate, which reduces to interpolating z' = β z_{0|t} + (1 − β) z^ψ_{0|t} [full text, Section 3.1 / Eq 7–10], with z^ψ approximated by τ extra reverse steps of the same or an external model [Eq 12, Section 3.2 / Eq 15], followed by LPF_γ(z_{t−1}) + HPF_{1−γ}(ε) [Eq 13, 16]. Applied for I = 5 early steps with τ = 10 [Section 4]. For CFG, it lowers the guidance scale in early steps (w = 0.8 instead of 7.5) because high early CFG gives off-manifold, saturated estimates that make the interpolation ineffective, and uses CFG++ in the interpolation [Appendix B, Fig 6, Table 4].

### Goal: What does it report about its effect on dynamic degree / flow magnitude, and about how the guidance scale interacts with motion?
On AnimateDiff, dynamic degree (mean of top-5% RAFT flow) drops from 26.67 (base) to 19.28 (FreeInit) and 15.25 (VideoGuide self-guided); the authors call it "an inherent trade-off" between consistency and dynamic degree [Section 4.1 table and text]. With an external guide the drop is smaller, and on LaVie dynamic degree increases [Section 4.1]. UniCtrl likewise improves consistency at "a significant reduction in dynamic degree" [Appendix G]. High early CFG produces color saturation that suppresses the guide's influence [Appendix B].

### Goal: What method assumption limits it for controlling motion amplitude under CFG (target signal, per-model or per-prompt tuning, cost, applicability)?
Its target is a teacher's temporally consistent estimate, not a motion amount, so it trades motion for consistency on the self-guided setting [Section 4.1]. It needs τ extra denoising passes at each of I steps (or an external model sharing the same VAE space [Appendix H]), and it must lower CFG early to work [Appendix B].
