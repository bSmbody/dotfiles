# Foresight: Failure Detection for Long-Horizon Robotic Manipulation with Action-Conditioned World Model Latents

## What this paper does
Foresight monitors long-horizon manipulation by encoding the current observation context with a frozen V-JEPA 2-AC world model and predicting the action-conditioned future latent under the policy's next action chunk; the predicted latents plus positional encodings feed a causal Transformer/LSTM/MLP that outputs per-step failure scores, thresholded by functional conformal prediction. It is policy-external, trained only on trajectory-level success/failure labels, and evaluated on LIBERO-Long, ManiSkill-Long, BEHAVIOR-1K and real robots with pi0.5, SmolVLA, GR00T N1.5, ACT.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What signal/mechanism does it use to decide when to intervene, and what supervision or labels does that signal rely on?
Features z^p_t = Pool(g_psi(z^h_t, A_t)), the world model's predicted latent given the policy's proposed chunk [full text, Section 4.2, Eq 6-7]; a causal sequence model D_theta maps U_<=t to s_t [Section 4.3]. Labels: each timestep inherits the rollout-level label, with early-detection weighting to encourage high scores before or during failure events [Section 4.3]. Alarm when s_t >= delta_t from functional CP [Sections 3, 4.4].

### Goal: How early do failure alarms fire relative to the point where help can still change the outcome?
Evaluated by ROC-AUC and calibrated balanced accuracy (0.94 LIBERO-Long, 0.80 ManiSkill-Long, 0.78 BEHAVIOR-1K) [Section 5.4]; no measurement of alarm time against a recoverability point. The action conditioning only ever uses the nominal policy's chunk A_t, so the score predicts the consequence of continuing, not of handing over.

### Goal: Does good failure-prediction quality translate into intervention value, and what limitation does the paper report?
No intervention is executed; the paper is detection only. Limitations: world-model cost and latency (feature extraction 182.7 ms per replan on an H200, 1.3B params) [Section 14, Table 14; Section 6], and CP guarantees depend on calibration matching deployment [Section 6].
