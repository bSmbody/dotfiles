# Triggering Generalist Reasoning via Predictive Uncertainty for Dual-System VLA

## What this paper does
TUD turns π0 into a dual system (PaliGemma generalist produces a cached KV context; Q-Former bridge + flow-matching specialist re-predict action chunks each step against that cache) and decides when to re-invoke the generalist from the predictive action-plan dispersion u_t: the std across the last E re-predictions of the same upcoming chunk slot. A call is skipped while the running mean of u_t stays below a percentile threshold θ_p and the steps since last call are below Δ_max. Evaluated on VLA-Arena, CALVIN and an SO-101 arm.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism decides when to call the expensive model, and does the decision depend on the remaining call budget or remaining horizon?
Objective is a Lagrangian max E[SR] − λ E[N_G] [full text, Section 2.2, Eq 1]. The rule (Algorithm 1) checks every Δ=3 steps: skip iff ū < θ_p and t − t_k < Δ_max (=20); θ_p is the online empirical p-th percentile of recently observed u_t [Section 3.3, Appendix C]. No remaining-budget or remaining-horizon term; matched budgets are obtained by sweeping p. Conclusion states: "p controls the trade-off but does not directly optimize for a specific target operating point such as a desired latency budget or minimum success rate" [Section 5].

### Goal: What signal estimates the value of a call, and does it model how much the expensive model's answer will help (not just whether the cheap policy is failing)?
u_t measures staleness of the cached plan (specialist changing its mind against the same cache), bounded via a Lipschitz argument [Section 3.2–3.3, Prop 1]. It does not model the gain of a call; every call is the same refresh of h_k. On success/failure separation, "the failure curve rises sharply in the final ~20 steps while the success curve stays flat" [Section 4.3, Fig 4], i.e. separation appears near the end of failed rollouts. At matched budgets TUD reaches 86.7% at 17.8 calls and 82.8% at 9.0 calls on VLA-Arena L0 vs other uncertainty signals [Section 4.3, Table 4]. The "expensive" model here is a co-trained 3B-class VLM at ~7.8× specialist latency [Section 4.2, Table 3], not an API model.

### Goal: What does the paper report as the limitation of its call-allocation mechanism?
No direct targeting of a budget or success constraint (quoted above). The signal requires the dual-system architecture's cached-context re-prediction, so it presumes the expensive model and the cheap policy share one trained architecture [Section 3.1].
