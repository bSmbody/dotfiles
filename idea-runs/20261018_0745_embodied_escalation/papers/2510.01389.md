# INSIGHT: INference-time Sequence Introspection for Generating Help Triggers in Vision-Language-Action Models

## What this paper does
INSIGHT extracts per-token entropy, log-probability and Dirichlet aleatoric/epistemic uncertainty from π0-FAST's action-token distributions and trains compact transformer classifiers over the temporal sequence of these features to output a per-step help/no-help decision, with strong (per-step "no progress" annotations) or weak (episode success) labels. Evaluated on real-robot rollouts (in-distribution and shifted) and LIBERO-10 sim OOD, against conformal prediction on entropy/perplexity.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism decides when to call the expensive model, and does the decision depend on the remaining call budget or remaining horizon?
A binary classifier P(help_t | P_t^1..P_t^n) run in parallel with the policy [full text, Section IV–V]; help is requested when the classifier fires. There is no budget variable at all; trigger frequency is an outcome reported post hoc (TTFH, trigger count, trigger rate) [Section VII-B5].

### Goal: What signal estimates the value of a call, and does it model how much the expensive model's answer will help (not just whether the cheap policy is failing)?
Only failure/no-progress likelihood of the VLA; labels are "if the action does not contribute to task progress" (strong) or episode success (weak) [Section VI-C]. The helper's effect is not modeled. Timing trade-off: strongly supervised model "triggers help earliest and most frequently, maximizing failure coverage but often over-intervening"; weakly supervised is "conservative, rarely interrupting successes but risking late or missed interventions" [Section VIII]. Temporal modeling beats static sequence-level CP scores.

### Goal: What does the paper report as the limitation of its call-allocation mechanism?
All models degrade under distribution shift; CP has many false positives when exchangeability fails; strong labels are subjective and costly [Section VIII]. No closed-loop intervention is executed; evaluation is offline classification.
