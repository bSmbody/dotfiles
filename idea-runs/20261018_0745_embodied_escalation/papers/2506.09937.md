# SAFE: Multitask Failure Detection for Vision-Language-Action Models

## What this paper does
SAFE detects VLA failures on unseen tasks by probing the VLA's last-layer hidden states with a 1-2 layer MLP (cumulative per-step sigmoid scores) or LSTM, trained on successful and failed rollouts from seen tasks, and calibrates a time-varying alarm threshold with one-sided functional conformal prediction on successful rollouts. It is evaluated on OpenVLA, pi0, pi0-FAST over LIBERO, SimplerEnv and real robots, against token-uncertainty, sample-consistency and embedding-OOD baselines.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What signal/mechanism does it use to decide when to intervene, and what supervision or labels does that signal rely on?
Signal: VLA final-layer embedding e_t (before logits or velocity field) fed to f_MLP(e_0:t)=sum sigma(g(e_tau)) or an LSTM [full text, Section 4.2]. Supervision: only the trajectory-level failure label y_i; every step of a failed rollout inherits it, and exact failure time is not used [Section 3]. Alarm when s_t exceeds the functional-CP band upper_t built from successful calibration rollouts, bounding false positives on successes at alpha [Section 4.3].

### Goal: How early do failure alarms fire relative to the point where help can still change the outcome?
The paper trades bal-acc against detection time T-det by varying alpha and compares to human-annotated failure times ("when a human thinks failure happens or intervention is needed"), claiming detection can precede those times [Section 6.2]. Its real-robot flags fire after the policy has frozen or the object has slipped [Section 6.3], i.e. reference is the visible failure, not a recoverability point.

### Goal: Does good failure-prediction quality translate into intervention value, and what limitation does the paper report?
SAFE treats detection as the end goal: on a flag "either the task execution is aborted or a human monitor will step in" [Section 3]; recovery and fallback policies are explicitly future work [Appendix F.3]. It requires collecting successful and failed rollouts of the deployed policy before use [Section 6.4]. Listed limitations: unclear transfer across embodiments/sim2real, last-layer features only, offline (not online) CP [Section 7, Appendix F.1-F.2].
