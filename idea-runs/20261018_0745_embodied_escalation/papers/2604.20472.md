# Temporal Difference Calibration in Sequential Tasks: Application to Vision-Language-Action Models

## What this paper does
The paper formalises sequential calibration of task-success confidence along an episode with a sequential Brier score and proves its minimiser is the policy's value function (expected return given history, under the deployed policy). It proposes TDQC, which trains a success predictor with a TD loss (bootstrap between consecutive steps, terminal step matched to the outcome, target network) on hidden states or black-box action/token probabilities. Applied to OpenVLA, pi0, pi0-FAST, UniVLA on LIBERO and a Franka dataset; also used for conformal early stopping and value-guided action selection.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What signal/mechanism does it use to decide when to intervene, and what supervision or labels does that signal rely on?
The predictor f_theta(h_t) approximates E^pi[R(h_T)|h_t] = V/Q of the nominal policy pi (Theorem 5.1) [full text, Section 5]. Training data are trajectories generated by pi with terminal success labels; TD loss (f(h_t)-f_target(h_{t+1}))^2 for t<T and (f(h_T)-y)^2 at T [Eq 6, Algorithm 1]. Early stopping flags when 1-f exceeds a functional-CP threshold from successful validation trajectories, following SAFE [Section 5.1].

### Goal: How early do failure alarms fire relative to the point where help can still change the outcome?
Early in the rollout TD predictors are only comparable to BCE baselines; Brier improves as time advances [Section 6.4, Fig 1]. Detection is scored by ROC-AUC up to the task's minimum rollout length; there is no measurement against a recoverability point or helper.

### Goal: Does good failure-prediction quality translate into intervention value, and what limitation does the paper report?
The only intervention studied is one-step look-ahead value-guided selection among the policy's own samples, which needs simulator access at inference (+13% on 3 LIBERO-10 tasks) [Sections 6.6, 7]. The value estimated is strictly on-policy for pi: bootstrapping follows pi's own transitions, so it says nothing about the value of switching to another controller. Limitations: no transfer across environments/embodiments (OpenVLA LIBERO-10 predictor to WidowX: 53.69 ROC-AUC), binary episodic success, look-ahead not deployable without a world model [Section 7]. The authors name allocating search effort only to difficult decisions as future work [Section 7].
