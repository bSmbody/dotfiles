# Self-Cross Diffusion Guidance for Text-to-Image Synthesis of Similar Subjects

## What this paper does
CVPR 2025. Training-free latent guidance against subject mixing. For each subject it aggregates the self-attention maps of patches selected by Otsu thresholding of the subject's cross-attention, weighted by cross-attention. It penalises overlap between this aggregated self-attention map and the cross-attention maps of the other subjects, plus a cross-attention response term, at early steps and intermediate layers. Evaluated on SD1.4, SD2.1 and SD3-medium, with a similar-subjects dataset and GPT-4o TIFA scoring.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism does it use against attribute leakage or object omission, and which part of MM-DiT joint attention (image-to-text, text-to-image, text-to-text, image-to-image quadrant, or token states) does it read or modify?
Aggregated self-attention A^s_i (Eq 5); pairwise guidance g(i,j) = sum min(A^s_i, A^c_j) + sum min(A^c_i, A^s_j) (Eq 6), averaged over subject pairs and added to a cross-attention response score (Eq 7-8), minimised by latent updates [full text, Section 4]. On SD3, cross-attention is the image-text block of joint attention (max over CLIP and T5) and self-attention is the image-image block [full text, Appendix A].

### Goal: Does it account for text tokens being rewritten by image tokens across MM-DiT blocks, or does it treat text tokens as fixed keys or apply a uniform text-side transform?
Text tokens are fixed keys; only subject tokens are used ("other tokens such as verbs and adjectives ... are not involved in our guidance"), so attribute tokens and the text-token update are not addressed [full text, Section 4].

### Goal: What limitation, failure case, quality or runtime cost, or external dependency (layout, LLM/VLM, fine-tuning) does it report on this axis?
Failure cases: blurry or cartoonish images and object-centric problems [full text, Appendix G]. FOCUS [2510.02315] reports segmentation artifacts and degradation under fine-tuning for this guidance.
