# When Numbers Speak: Aligning Textual Numerals and Visual Instances in Text-to-Video Diffusion Models

## What this paper does
NUMINA (CVPR 2026) is a training-free identify-then-guide method for count accuracy in DiT text-to-video models (Wan2.1/2.2, plus CogVideoX-5B MMDiT in the appendix). A truncated pre-generation runs to step t*=20 of 50. One self-attention head is chosen by an instance-separability score and one cross-attention head per noun by peak response; fusing them gives a countable layout. The layout is edited (remove the smallest instance; add a copied or circular template at a heuristic-cost location), and the video is regenerated with cross-attention bias or score overwrite under a decaying schedule. CountAcc on Wan2.1-1.3B rises from 42.3% to 49.7%.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does it identify the instance layout in a DiT and at which step/layer?
Self-attention heads at reference step t* and layer l* are PCA-projected and scored by contrast, block variance and Sobel edge clarity; the best head gives the spatial scaffold. Per noun, the cross-attention head with maximal peak gives a focus mask. Clustering of the self-attention map gives proposals, which are kept if their overlap with the focus mask is at least tau; the number of kept regions is the count [full text, Section 4.1, Eq 2-4]. Defaults are t*=20 and l*=15 of 50 steps [full text, Section 5.1]. Accuracy rises until t*=20; t*=40 gives only a 3.2% relative gain at double the pre-generation cost, and t*>40 declines. "Early denoising steps are needed to form instance-separable attention", and separable layouts "emerge most distinctly at middle denoising steps" [full text, Section 5.3; Appendix S1].

### Goal: How does it correct the count, and does correction consider spatial relations?
Removal erases the smallest region. Addition copies the smallest existing region, or uses a circle if none exists, and places it at the grid center minimizing overlap + distance-to-current-layout-center + temporal cost [full text, Section 4.2, Eq 5-6]. Regeneration adds a large negative bias to suppress removed regions, and a k*delta(t) bias or overwritten pre-softmax scores to boost added ones, with delta(t) decreasing over steps [full text, Section 4.2]. Placement uses only overlap and centrality; no prompt-specified relation enters the cost, and each category is handled independently [full text, Section 4.2].

### Goal: What does it report about cost, failure modes and MM-DiT applicability?
The method is two-pass (pre-generation, then full regeneration); EasyCache is used to cut pre-generation cost [full text, Section 5.3]. The characteristic failure is a head that attends to salient parts (an animal's head), so one instance is over-segmented into several and the error is "irrecoverable" [full text, Appendix S2]. Dense counts (tens or more) are unexplored [full text, Section 6]. On CogVideoX-5B (MMDiT) CountAcc goes from 40.2% to 44.4% [full text, Appendix S1].
