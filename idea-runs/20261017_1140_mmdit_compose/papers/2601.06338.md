# Circuit Mechanisms for Spatial Relation Generation in Diffusion Transformers

## What this paper does
A mechanistic-interpretability study (CVPR 2026). PixArt-style DiTs are trained from scratch on a synthetic two-shape, eight-relation task with random token embeddings (RTE), T5 or CLIP text encoders, and relation circuits are traced by attention synopsis, ablation and causal edits. RTE-DiTs use a two-stage circuit: a relation head reads the relation word and writes a spatial gradient, then an object head places the shapes. T5-DiTs instead decode the relation from the contextual embedding of the second object token. Relation words matter little there, and the circuit is brittle to filler words. Head screening transfers to pretrained PixArt-Sigma.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Where is spatial-relation information carried in a T5-conditioned DiT?
In the contextual embedding of the second object token. Masking relation, filler or color words barely changes accuracy. Masking shape2 drops shape, binding and relation accuracy by ~50%, and masking shape1 drops relation accuracy by 15% [full text, Section 5]. Variance partitioning of the shape2 embedding gives relation ~12% partial R^2 in T5 space, rising to ~21% after the DiT MLP projection (Table 1). Vector arithmetic on that embedding (subtract V_lowerleft, add 3 V_lowerright) causally moves the generated objects. One or two cross-attention heads turn the relation factor into a spatial gradient over image positions (L3H7) [full text, Section 5].

### Goal: What implication does this have for guidance operating on relation tokens in pretrained DiTs?
With a T5 encoder, relation geometry is entangled with object identity in object tokens, not localised in the relation word. Inserting "the" or reordering the sentence collapses T5-DiT relation accuracy, while RTE-DiT stays stable [full text, Section 5; Appendix A.8]. Pretrained PixArt-Sigma has weak relation ability (8 of 30 pairs non-trivial), but token ablation shows spatial information in both relation and object words, and a few spatial heads are found [full text, Section 5; Appendix A.10]. All experiments are on cross-attention PixArt-style DiTs, not joint-attention MM-DiT, and the paper proposes no guidance method [full text, Section 3, 6].
