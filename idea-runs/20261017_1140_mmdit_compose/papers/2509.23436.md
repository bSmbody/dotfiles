# LOTFormer: Doubly Stochastic Linear Attention via Low Rank Optimal Transport

## What this paper does
Proposes a linear-time doubly stochastic attention: two entropic optimal-transport problems (queries to a small learnable pivot measure, pivot to keys) are composed into a low-rank coupling that is provably doubly stochastic. Evaluated on ImageNet-1K, Long Range Arena, GLUE conversion of fine-tuned BERT, and a plug-and-play protocol on IWSLT'14 translation, against Sinkformer and ESPFormer.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: Can a trained softmax Transformer have its attention swapped for a doubly stochastic (query- and key-normalised) operator without retraining, and with what loss?
Yes, for the encoder of translation models. The plug-and-play protocol "starts from a trained model and performs a surgical conversion": only the encoder self-attention operator is replaced, with "the rest of the network bitwise unchanged", and the model is evaluated "with no additional training" [full text, Section 4.4]. On IWSLT'14 De-En with a softmax Transformer base (33.40 BLEU), swapping in Sinkformer gives 33.36, ESPFormer 33.38, LOTFormer 33.29; on a DiffTransformer base (33.85), Sinkformer gives 33.67 [full text, Section 4.4, Table 5]. So adding query-axis (column) normalisation to a frozen softmax model cost at most about 0.2 BLEU. The GLUE/BERT conversion, by contrast, uses a distillation phase plus fine-tuning, so it is not training-free [full text, Section 4.4; Appendix D.3]. The evidence is for encoder self-attention in text models only; no generative image model is tested.
