### C1 verdict

- **Verdict**: `revise(focus=novelty)`
- **Evidence**:
  - [2610.13941] (SFG/iSFG, 2026-10-09, concurrent) already controls the lever C1 edits: the image-to-text message in the text-query rows (O_t = A_{t->t}V_t + B_{i->t}). It needs no training or layout, and iSFG adds zero extra NFE by scaling the image values that text queries read by 0.80 in every joint block of SD3.5M and FLUX. Over CFG, iSFG raises GenEval colour attribution 0.5767 to 0.5950 (SD3.5M) and 0.5233 to 0.5417 (FLUX), raises counting and position, and adds +1.33 HPSv2.1. Its transplantation study already shows that I2T messages causally rescue or disrupt outcomes, mostly in steps 1-8 of 40. This falsifies the gap as written ("no layout-free method controls ... which image regions ... a token may absorb"). It also pre-empts C1's secondary contribution of showing how much leakage travels through text-token updates. What survives is the delta of per-phrase, ownership-gated suppression against one uniform multiplier. C1 never names this work, and iSFG at matched I2T mass must become the primary baseline. SFG itself supplies the argument C1 needs: its uniform suppression rescues 72.0% of failures but disrupts 16.7% of successes (Appendix G), and selective, ownership-based gating could plausibly cut that collateral damage.
  - [2610.13941] also complicates C1's premise. It finds that I2T feedback preserves what the image already shows, including errors in a phrase's own region. C1 deliberately keeps each phrase's absorption of the region it wins, and if the gated row is renormalised over the won keys (as Slot Attention's weighted mean is), it concentrates that absorption. A cup region already reddened through the image-query path would then be fed back into "cup" at full strength. The candidate must say whether the gated rows are renormalised, and why own-region feedback should be kept when the measured evidence favours suppressing it.
  - [2606.15796] is overstated as grounding. The cup turns red only when text-layer-3 red features, whose attributions are "one to two orders of magnitude weaker than the dominant ones", are amplified with alpha = +100; the unsteered cup is blue. The paper does not tie those features to the cup tokens or to text-query absorption of image tokens. The 89.9% text-stream share is measured at step 0 of 4-step schnell, when image tokens are still mostly noise. That is the step at which text queries can absorb the least object content, so the figure does not support "leakage written by phrase tokens absorbing other objects' regions".
  - [2503.12885] does not isolate C1's path. DreamRenderer encodes each instance separately with T5, and its text mask also blocks cross-instance text-text attention. Its regions are known from step 0 through boxes plus depth/canny. Its 3.5-6.2% ISR gain therefore cannot be credited to text-query-to-image routing, and it says nothing about ownership inferred from noisy early affinities.
  - [2006.15055] The source mechanism is stated correctly: softmax over slots, harm when normalisation moves to the input axis, soft k-means at 75.5 vs 98.8 ARI, and a rough upper bound on slots being enough. Two small faults: the background slot is suggested as future work, not recommended, and one mapping row is wrong. Slot iterations share weights and refine persistent slots, but the MM-DiT text stream is reset from E(p) at every denoiser call [2610.13941]. "Early steps" therefore cannot be slot iterations. Competition iterates only across blocks within one forward pass, and each block has its own projections.
  - [2110.11773], [2509.23436] are mis-targeted as tolerance evidence. Sinkformers' near-unit column sums are for square self-attention trained from scratch. The MM-DiT text-query x image-key block is rectangular (tens of phrase tokens against about 4k image keys) and sits inside a joint softmax, so "near what trained attention already does" does not follow. LOTFormer's frozen swap is NMT encoder self-attention. Stronger and more direct evidence that a frozen MM-DiT tolerates I2T edits is iSFG's in-place scaling, which also gained preference scores [2610.13941].
  - [2510.15015] The stated difference from DeLeaker holds: every Eq-11 edit has an image query and the masks are frozen from early steps. Its text-text ablation (9-20% worse) supports leaving text-text alone. Its early-separation evidence concerns image-query-to-text scores, so the second half of C1's key assumption remains untested: that text-query-to-image affinities rank the right phrase early. The first half, that I2T messages are causally potent early, is now supported by [2610.13941].
  - Other checks pass. The contribution is methodological: a zero-NFE attention rule that fits SD3.5-M in 24 GB and stays well under 40 GPU-days. Phrases, inputs and the competition axis are mapped concretely. Novelty queries run: "slot attention text-to-image", "slot competition", "query competition attention", "softmax over queries", "doubly stochastic attention diffusion", "Sinkhorn attention diffusion", "text tokens joint attention", "text-to-image attention MMDiT", "MMDiT training-free", "joint attention masking training-free", "regional prompting FLUX", "text token isolation", "entity attention mask text-to-image", plus recency passes since 2025-10-17 ("image-to-text feedback", "attribute binding training-free", "compositional text-to-image training-free", "semantic leakage text-to-image"). None found phrase-level competition for image tokens in MM-DiT text-query rows. The closest is the uniform [2610.13941].
- **Strongest objection in 1 sentence**: Concurrent SFG/iSFG [2610.13941] already suppresses what MM-DiT text tokens absorb from image tokens, layout-free at zero extra NFE and with GenEval colour/count gains, so C1's gap is false as stated. Its contribution shrinks to per-phrase competitive gating over a uniform I2T multiplier, and C1 has neither argued why that gating should help nor positioned it against SFG.
