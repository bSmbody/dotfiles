# Calibrate What You SHIP: Post-Selection Risk Control for Verifier-Guided Text-to-Image Generation

## What this paper does
SHIP addresses the mismatch between calibrating a verifier threshold on individual candidates and the risk of the image a full test-time search policy actually releases. It replays the complete deployed policy (best-of-N, sequential first-pass, adaptive rewriting/editing) on held-out prompts, scores the released image with an independent target VLM judge, and picks the most permissive threshold whose released-output risk upper bound meets a budget, with finite-sample validity for replayable policies. On GenEval2 with FLUX at N=16, released risk drops from 0.310 (pooled-candidate threshold) to 0.162.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does SHIP use any per-candidate second measurement at inference time, or only offline held-out calibration?
Only offline calibration. The online selector is Qwen2.5-VL Soft-TIFA atom scores; an independent target judge (Qwen3-VL) is "used only offline" to define the loss, and the deployed policy "never observes" it [full text, Section 3.1-3.2, Eq 1-2]. At inference the policy releases the first or best candidate whose selector risk is below the calibrated threshold τ*, a single global scalar [Section 3.1, Figure 2]. No per-candidate reliability signal or re-measurement of a candidate is used.

### Goal: How large is the within-prompt selection effect (winner's curse of the selector) relative to other sources of mismatch?
In the mechanism decomposition at N=16 for sequential release, changing prompt weights contributes +0.108 risk (bootstrap 0.083–0.133), while replacing the mean passing candidate's loss by the released candidate's contributes −0.009 (−0.035 to 0.017); "best-of-N selection has a more negative within-prompt component" [full text, Appendix C "Mechanism decomposition"]. So with a VLM selector and an independent VLM target judge, within-prompt selection did not inflate held-out risk in this setting; the mismatch came mostly from which prompts get released.
