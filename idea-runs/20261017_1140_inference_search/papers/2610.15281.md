# Imperfect Verifiers, Exact Targets: Inference-Time Scaling for Continuous Generation (CS-VGB)

## What this paper does
CS-VGB is an outer MCMC over noise-level checkpoints for continuous generators (diffusion, flow matching, one-step posterior maps) that alternates denoising and renoising moves, guided by process verifiers V_hat_j at interior checkpoints via edge conductances, with reverse insertion and lazy pool evaluation for finite-candidate implementations. It proves the stationary clean distribution equals the reward-tilted target r(x) pi_0(x) regardless of process-verifier accuracy, with exponential convergence under verifier error. Experiments: ImageNet prompt-guided generation with Meta Flow Maps (optimising HPSv2, held-out VQAScore), CelebA attributes with Posterior Diamond Maps, multi-robot planning.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What does "imperfect verifier" mean here, and what is assumed about the terminal reward?
Imperfection refers only to process (intermediate) verifiers: V_hat_j may differ from V_j(x)=E[r(X0)|X_j=x] at noisy checkpoints, "we assume that V_hat_0 = r is evaluated exactly" [full text, Section 2 "Process verifier and sampling goal"]. The target is pi_0*(dx) ∝ r(x) pi_0(dx) with r the given terminal reward [full text, Section 1]. Hence the guarantee is exactness with respect to the proxy-tilted target; misspecification of the terminal verifier itself is outside the framework.

### Goal: How does held-out quality behave as compute grows?
On ImageNet/MFM, CS-VGB-G reaches HPSv2 52.01 vs 45.66 for MFM-G at ~2^16 NFE, with held-out VQAScore 0.778 vs 0.745 [full text, Section 3.2 "Prompt-guided generation with posterior maps", Fig 5]. The optimised reward rises ~14% between those methods while the held-out score rises ~4%; the paper does not analyse whether the held-out metric saturates while the optimised one keeps climbing. Stated limitations: verifier error can slow mixing, evaluation costs offset low-budget gains, and sampling guarantees do not extend to the search heuristics [full text, Section 4].
