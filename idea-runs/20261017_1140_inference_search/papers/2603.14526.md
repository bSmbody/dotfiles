# LatSearch: Latent Reward-Guided Search for Faster Inference-Time Scaling in Video Diffusion

## What this paper does
LatSearch trains a latent reward model (Qwen2-VL-3B backbone with a 3D-conv latent patchifier, step embedding and VQ/MQ/TA query tokens) that scores partially denoised video latents of Wan2.1-1.3B at arbitrary timesteps. Training labels assign the final video's VideoAlign-style rewards to intermediate latents scaled by the cosine similarity between z_t and z_0. At inference, Reward-Guided Resampling and Pruning (RGRP) scores N candidates at steps {10,15,20}, resamples unique seeds by softmax(tau*r), accumulates the weights, and keeps the single highest-cumulative-weight seed for the rest of denoising. It reports +2.44% VBench-2.0 fidelity at equal compute and up to 79% less runtime than prior search.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What intermediate reward/value estimator does it use for partial trajectories, and how is it computed?
A learned latent reward R_psi(z_t,p,t) [full text, Section 3.2]. Targets are "similarity-grounded credit assignment": r~_t = s_t * r with s_t=(1+cos(z_t,z_0))/2 and r the final video reward vector [Eq 7-9]; trained with MSE regression plus a pairwise preference loss [Eq 11-14] on 5,000 videos from 1,000 prompts [Section 4.1]. Pairwise accuracy at steps 10/15 is ~73-75% average across VQ/MQ/TA [full text, Appendix 0.C.1, Table 0.C.1].

### Goal: How are intermediate estimates turned into prune/resample decisions, and is that rule adapted per prompt or per step from measured reliability?
At fixed scoring steps S={10,15,20}, weights pi=softmax(tau*r), multinomial draw of N with replacement, keep unique seeds; cumulative weights c_i += pi_i; at max(S) keep argmax c_i [full text, Section 3.3, Eq 16-18]. tau is a single global constant (0.5-2.0 give similar results); S is chosen by ablation: early steps are avoided as "unstable and non-informative", and adding more scoring points "accumulate[s] uncertainty from similarity-derived targets and degrade[s] performance" [Appendix 0.C.2]. Nothing is adapted per prompt and no uncertainty is predicted.

### Goal: What limitation does it state or show that bears on intermediate reward estimation for pruning/resampling?
No convergence guarantee because the latent reward is learned and approximate; the cosine-similarity credit assignment "remains an approximation of true semantic contribution" and is named the "approximation bottleneck" [full text, Section 6]. Candidate diversity comes only from perturbing the initial noise z_T (Eq 15); resampling keeps unique seeds, so no new children are created mid-trajectory [Section 3.3].
