# VASR: Variance-Aware Systematic Resampling for Reward-Guided Diffusion

## What this paper does
VASR traces SMC lineage collapse in reward-guided diffusion to multinomial resampling. It decomposes per-step estimator variance into continuation variance (from propagating particles) and residual variance (from random offspring counts), allocates offspring mass m ∝ w*exp(r~) to minimise the former under a plug-in proxy, and uses systematic resampling with an ordering heuristic to bound the latter. For latent T2I diffusion, where Tweedie intermediate rewards are noisy, it proposes VASR-Max, which redirects all extra offspring to the argmax particle. It reports better FID/lineage diversity on MNIST/CIFAR-10 and higher DrawBench ImageReward than FKD at matched NFE, ~4x faster than DTS*.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What intermediate reward/value estimator does it use for partial trajectories, and how is it computed?
The Tweedie proxy r~_i = r(x̂0(x_t^(i),t)), optionally z-scored across particles per step [full text, Algorithm 1 line 4; Section 4 "Variance-Optimal Mass Allocation"].

### Goal: Does the method represent the spread/uncertainty of the final reward given x_t?
Proposition 1 shows the expected continuation variance is sum_i (w_i^2/m_i) sigma_t^2(x_t^(i)), where sigma_t^2 is "the conditional variance of the terminal reward score obtained by continuing particle i" — the optimal allocation therefore depends on that spread. VASR does not estimate it: it substitutes the "plug-in scale proxy sigma_t(x_t) ∝ e^{r~}", which yields m ∝ w e^{r~} [full text, Section 4, Proposition 1 / Eq 5, Eq 8]. An appendix measurement with 10 stochastic rollouts per particle on SD1.5 shows within-particle ImageReward variance ~0.02-0.04, roughly constant across particles, so "score differences between particles at intermediate timesteps are comparable in magnitude to the inherent rollout noise, making a soft proportional allocation unreliable" [full text, Appendix A "Stochastic Reward Noise", Fig 10].

### Goal: How are intermediate estimates turned into resample decisions, and what limitation bears on pruning/resampling?
Fixed schedules (e.g. every 5 steps from t=10 to 40), lambda=1, set per task [full text, Appendix A]. For T2I it switches to VASR-Max, putting all extra offspring on argmax_j w_j e^{r~_j} [Eq 9], "trading unbiasedness for robustness" and sacrificing theoretical guarantees [Section 7]. Higher lambda reduces lineage diversity and worsens FID [Section 6, Fig 4]. FKD over-optimises the aesthetic reward with artifact-prone samples [Section 5, Fig 7].
