# Navigating the Exploration-Exploitation Tradeoff in Inference-Time Scaling of Diffusion Models (Funnel-SMC / SMC-A)

## What this paper does
The paper argues that SMC-based inference-time scaling faces a dilemma: early noisy states are malleable but their Tweedie-based rewards are unreliable, while late states are scoreable but no longer tiltable. It proposes F-SMC, a funnel-shaped particle schedule (more particles early, fewer late, matched NFE), and SMC-A, a linearly increasing temperature lambda_t=(T-t)/T*lambda that down-weights early rewards. On SD1.5/2.0/2.1/SDXL with GenEval and DrawBench it beats best-of-N, top-k, SVDD and FK-SMC at matched NFE.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What intermediate reward/value estimator does it use for partial trajectories, and how is it computed?
All methods, including theirs, score r(x_{0|t}) where x_{0|t} is the Tweedie denoised estimate from the score network [full text, Section 4.2, Definition 1 / Eq 9; Algorithm 1 line 12].

### Goal: How are intermediate estimates turned into prune/resample decisions, and is that rule adapted per prompt or per step from measured reliability?
Resampling at fixed steps [10,15,20,25,29] with a fixed funnel particle schedule (e.g. [12,10,6,4,4]) chosen by sweeping five schedules on DrawBench at NFE=240 [full text, Appendix A.4], and a temperature that rises linearly with t, identical for every prompt [full text, Section 4.2, "lambda_t=(T-t)/T lambda_0"]. Neither the schedule nor lambda_t is set from any per-prompt or per-particle reliability measurement; the final output is argmax of final reward among survivors [Algorithm 1, lines 15-17].

### Goal: What does it report about the reliability of intermediate estimates?
Spearman correlation between intermediate and final ImageReward over 16 noises per prompt is weak in early stages [full text, Section 5.1, Fig 5], and single-step resampling early has "negligible or even negative impact" while mid-stage resampling helps most. The correlation differs by GenEval category: "the correlation for the position category remains consistently low" [full text, Appendix A.3, Fig 9]. The conclusion lists "low reliability of reward estimators during early timesteps" as an open challenge and plans better early-stage reward models [full text, Section 6].
