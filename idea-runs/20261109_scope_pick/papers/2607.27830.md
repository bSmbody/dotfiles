# Thinking Once Is Enough: Intermediate-Layer Evidence Routing for High-Resolution VQA

## What this paper does
Thinking-Once is a training-free, single-visual-pass method for high-resolution VQA: run the full multimodal sequence (one full-image encoding) to an intermediate routing layer, rebuild question-conditioned attention there, keep a minimum-coverage set of evidence tokens at full resolution plus coarse per-cell background summaries, and continue the remaining layers on the shortened sequence. Evaluated on Qwen2.5-VL-7B, Qwen3-VL-8B and ZwZ models on V*, HR-Bench 4K/8K (max_pixel=16384 tokens setting).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How well do base models do at (capped) native resolution on V* / HR-Bench, and how do re-look methods compare?
Qwen2.5-VL-7B base: V* 76.4, HR-4K 68.1, HR-8K 65.5 (mean 72.5) [full text, Appendix Table 2; Section 5.2]. Thinking-Once on it: mean 79.1 (+9.9/+4.6/+5.5), peak memory ~20 GB → ~16 GB [Section 5.2]. Re-look methods in Table 1: DeepEyes V* 84.3 / HR-4K 72.8 / HR-8K 70.8 mean 76.0, ~37 min; ViCrop mean 75.2; DeepScan mean 77.8 at ~180 min (Thinking-Once cuts V* time by 97.2% vs DeepScan) [Section 5.3, Table 1].

### Goal: Are the interleaved crops in thinking-with-images models the source of their gains?
Mostly not on these benchmarks. Removing interleaved images (results adapted from another paper): DeepEyes-7B V* 84.3 → 84.3, HR-4K 72.8 → 72.3, HR-8K 69.3 → 69.5; Thyme-7B changes ≤0.6; Pixel-Reasoner-7B drops 1.5 on V* and 1.0 on HR-8K [Appendix, Table 2]. The authors conclude extra images "are not always necessary", while noting some settings degrade [Appendix].

### Goal: What does this imply about whether new pixels are needed?
The paper argues failures are often utilization-limited (evidence encoded but diluted by many irrelevant high-res tokens), and explicitly does not claim to replace tools when the encoding genuinely lacks the information [Section 3.1]. Several compression baselines ran out of memory at max_pixel=16384 on an A800 [Section 5.3], i.e., at this cap HR-Bench images produce up to ~16k visual tokens.
