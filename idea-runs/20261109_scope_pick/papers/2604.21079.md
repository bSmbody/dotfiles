# Foveated Reasoning: Stateful, Action-based Visual Focusing for Vision-Language Models

## What this paper does
FoveateR (ECCV 2026) starts a Qwen2.5-VL (3B/7B) from a low-resolution global view (224² or 336²). During one autoregressive decode it may emit a special `<fov>` token; a small continuous box head on a layer-27 hidden state predicts a box, the high-resolution crop is tokenized by the vision encoder and injected into the same decoding stream as a delimited `<fov> V_t </fov>` block. Trained with cold-start SFT on pseudo boxes then GRPO with a foveated-area regularizer. Evaluated on Visual CoT (12 sets) and V*.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Is the high-resolution evidence appended to the context or does it replace the low-resolution tokens of the region?
Appended. The memory is M_t = M_{t-1} ⊕ o_t, "implemented by the Transformer's KV-cache", and the crop tokens are injected "back into the same autoregressive stream as a delimited block" [full text, Section 3.1, Eq 5]. Appendix 0.C (Limitations) states explicitly: "each newly acquired high-res evidence is accumulated in the Transformer key–value cache ... it causes a linear growth in memory & compute with the amount of acquired evidence", and lists as future work replacing this cache-growing mechanism with a more compact one and policies "which decide which evidence to retain, update, or discard over time" [full text, Appendix 0.C]. So in-place replacement is named as an open direction, not done.

### Goal: What are the token budgets, number of foveations, and efficiency results?
At 336² the 3B model uses ~307 ± 156 visual tokens on average (vs 1,152 for multi-pass VisCoT) [full text, Section 4.2]. Max 4 foveations per answer, max 2048 generated tokens [Appendix 0.A.3]. Table 3: Qwen2.5-VL-7B oracle at original resolution 90.4 / 87.8 / 48.2 (DocVQA / TextVQA / V7W); 336² baseline 55.1 / 77.2 / 46.5; cold-start FoveateR revealing only 3-8% of high-res area recovers to 64.2 / 82.2 / ~ (V7W exceeds oracle) [Section 4.2, Table 3]. Versus VisCoT-7B: ~7x fewer visual tokens and ~9x lower latency on one H100 [Section 4.3]. No comparison against simply running Qwen at a moderately reduced whole-image resolution at matched tokens beyond the 336² baseline.

### Goal: How are positions of injected tokens handled?
Injected tokens are appended at the end of the sequence like normal new tokens; for batching they left-pad and pad attention mask, hidden states, KV cache and position ids [Appendix 0.A.3]. No sub-grid or image-frame positions are used; the paper does not discuss positional alignment of crops with the global view.

### Goal: On which benchmarks relevant to the run is it evaluated?
V* (zero-shot, MCQ): FoveateR-3B at 336²: Attr 56.5, GPT4 76.5, OCR 80.0 [Table 2]; no HR-Bench, MathVista or thinking-model (long CoT) experiments; not memory-saturated serving.
