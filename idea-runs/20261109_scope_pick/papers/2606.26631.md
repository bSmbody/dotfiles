# Position Rebinding Cache Reuse: Replay-Free Visual Revisiting for Interleaved Multimodal Reasoning

## What this paper does
PRCR targets interleaved multimodal CoT (ICoT) where attention-selected visual tokens are re-inserted during decoding. Instead of re-forwarding (replaying) them, it stores pre-RoPE visual K/V per layer from prefill with their 2D coordinates, assigns new positions in a short window just left of the current text position (Position-Consistent Reinsertion, L=2), re-applies RoPE to the keys, reuses the values, and appends the entries to the active cache. Evaluated on Qwen3-VL-8B/32B and InternVL3.5-8B/14B (M3CoT, MathVista, etc.).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What exactly is the failure mode of reusing stale visual KV?
Direct KV reuse (copying prefill visual KV, keys still rotated to original image coordinates, appended to the cache at decode time) collapses into repeated-token loops: 23.50% accuracy with an 81.04% stuck rate [full text, Section 2.2, Section 4.4 Table 2]. Their diagnosis: keys bound to original coordinates give a stale relative offset to the current text query (Eq 5), and since they are normalized together with all active entries, they reshape attention over the whole context, which autoregressively amplifies into low-entropy loops [Section 2.3]. Note: in Direct reuse the entries are duplicates of tokens already in the prefix, so the perturbation combines stale offset and duplicated attention mass; the paper attributes it to the positional binding.

### Goal: Which position assignments work, and what does this imply for writing visual KV at early (image-region) positions late in decoding?
Linear appending after the current token (shifts later text positions): 63.03%, 7.93% stuck. Uniform squeeze between two text positions: 67.45%, 0% stuck. PCR (re-anchor into (p_txt−L, p_txt+1), keep 2D relative layout): 68.68%, 0% stuck [Section 4.4]. Performance peaks at L=2 and falls as the inserted entries move farther left from the decode position; leftward re-anchoring beats rightward [Section 4.4]. So the evidence is that reinserted visual KV works best placed adjacent to the current decode position; placing it at distant, early positions (the original image coordinates) is the failing configuration.

### Goal: Are deeper-layer KV of reinserted tokens recomputed under the new context?
No. Per-layer raw K/V from prefill are reused; only key rotation changes; values are reused unchanged [Section 3.3, Eq 11-12]. Hidden states are not recomputed.

### Goal: Does PRCR handle zoomed/re-encoded regions?
No. Limitations: PRCR relies on prefill visual KV; zoomed or rescaled region features have no prefill KV, so "in scenarios involving dynamic resolution adjustments or local zooming, the cache reuse mechanism fails" [Section 7]. Suggested extensions: multi-scale KV prefill or learned scale-aware rebinding.
