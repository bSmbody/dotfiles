# Foveated Compression: Selective High-Resolution Preservation for Token-Efficient VLMs

## What this paper does
With a frozen Qwen2.5-VL-7B, the image is encoded once at full resolution; a learned Foveated Merger (32.8M params, behaviorally self-distilled from the native model) compresses each 3x3 block of native tokens into one; a Foveated Selector (10.8M) picks one of nine spatial cells to keep at native resolution by in-place replacement of its compressed tokens, giving exactly 20.99% of native visual tokens. Evaluated on 37,610 examples of eight short-answer benchmarks.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does it construct a mixed-resolution image and assign M-RoPE positions?
Compressed units in the chosen cell are replaced by their 3x3 native blocks at prefill; both share the 3584-d interface so no re-encoding [full text, Section 3.4]. "Native and compressed tokens share the native visual-grid coordinate system for M-RoPE. Native tokens use unit-stride coordinates, while compressed tokens use the top-left coordinate of their corresponding 3x3 native block", keeping later text positions invariant [Section 3.4]. The compressed tokens required a trained merger (self-distillation) to stay compatible with native ones [Section 3.2]; there is no test of untrained mixed resolution.

### Goal: Does localized high resolution beat whole-image resizing at matched tokens?
No, on average. Macro accuracy: Native 78.85 (100%); Foveated Selector 69.61 (20.99%); matched-budget downsample 70.99 (19.82%); 2x downsample 72.43 (24.57%); uniform compression 64.62 vs iso-token downsample 64.86 (11.11%) [Section 4.3, Table 1]. Selector wins only on ChartQA (57.84 vs 53.48); resizing wins strongly on OCRBench (67.00 vs 58.40) and MME. A K=1 region oracle reaches 82.73, i.e., large headroom from region choice [Table 1].

### Goal: How often does region choice matter at all?
All nine cells give the same score on 78.5% of examples; only 21.5% are spatially informative [Section 4.2]. Uniform 9:1 compression keeps 86.1% of native-correct predictions (38.8% on ChartQA to 94.3% on POPE) [Section 4.2].

### Goal: What is outside its scope?
One-shot at prefill, short answers (≤16 tokens), no in-reasoning refinement, no wall-clock/FLOPs, full-resolution encoder still runs, no comparison with crop-and-reprocess methods [Section 5].
