# PIVOT: Pivot-Aware On Policy Self Distillation for Multi-Turn VLM Agents

## What this paper does
PIVOT studies why on-policy (self-)distillation helps multi-turn VLM agents (Sokoban, FrozenLake, AI2-THOR navigation, ManiSkill PrimitiveSkill, SVG). Counterfactual rollback probes show the gain comes mostly from restoring the environment at the pivot step, the first action that makes the goal infeasible within the remaining budget, not from teacher hints. PIVOT then trains an analyzer to localize the pivot from visual collages and action logs, a privileged teacher to re-score failed tokens, and a student with GRPO plus confidence-gated OPD, all without environment rollback during RL; Qwen2.5-VL-3B reaches 0.90 overall accuracy.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How is the pivot step defined, and what does it assume about the environment?
t* is "the first action that renders the task unfeasible under the remaining budget", computed from a domain feasibility certificate Feas(s,k) [full text, Sec 2.2, Eq 2, App B]. This requires a solver-style certificate; FrozenLake's slippery setting is disabled "for determinism" [App E]. Limitations: the definition "relies on remaining-budget goal reachability"; continuous control "may use dynamic, probabilistic, or entropy-guided reachability" [Sec 6].
### Goal: What do the rollback probes say about the value of teacher hints versus state rollback at the pivot?
Rollback at t* with no hint lifts suffix return in 3.3-56.6% of failures; rolling back one step later sharply reduces it (PrimitiveSkill 17.7% to 8.3%); self hints differ from no-hint by at most 2.6 points and 8B-teacher hints add only 0.3-3.5 points [Sec 2.2, Empirical Findings]. Takeaway: rollback drives recovery, hints are marginal, temporal precision is critical [Sec 2.2, Takeaway].
### Goal: Does it separate agent errors from stochastic low-level execution failures?
No. Its environments use discrete or primitive actions executed deterministically (pick(x,y,z) primitives in PrimitiveSkill) [App E], and pivot labels come from feasibility heuristics [Sec 6]; there is no learned stochastic executor below the agent and no rule for budgeting external teacher calls.
