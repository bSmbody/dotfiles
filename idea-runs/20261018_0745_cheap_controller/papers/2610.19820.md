# ActionOPD: Distilling Decisions, Not Tokens

## What this paper does
ActionOPD distils black-box LLM agents into Qwen3-4B/8B by maximizing expected teacher-action likelihood at student-encountered states. At each state the student samples N=4 responses, the teacher samples K=3, and each student response is rewarded by semantic agreement of its parsed action (tool call or message) with the teacher samples via a programmatic plus LLM matcher; GRPO-style clipped updates train the student; the highest-reward candidate is executed. Gains of 3.3-6.9 pp on four out-of-distribution tool-use benchmarks.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What is the unit of supervision and how is it obtained at student states?
Supervision is the semantic action a = phi_s(y), not tokens or reasoning; objective Eq 1 maximizes E_{s~d_S, a^T~pi_T}[pi_theta(a^T|s)], which reduces to REINFORCE with agreement reward (Eq 3-4) [full text, Sec 2, 2.1]. The teacher is queried for K=3 responses at every student-encountered state, and N=4 student candidates per state are scored against them (Eq 5-6) [Sec 2.2, 2.3].
### Goal: What does it state as limitations relevant to long-horizon decisions?
"Agreement does not guarantee correctness: teacher errors or suboptimal decisions can become learning targets"; matcher false positives/negatives misassign rewards; and fixed K, N "may contain few semantically matching actions when plausible decisions are dispersed, as may occur in long-horizon interactions" [Sec 5]. Committing the best-matching candidate introduces "a degree of off-policy state visitation" [Sec 2.3]. Collection budgets do not equalize "teacher queries, or compute" across methods [App B.4], so teacher-call cost is not controlled.
