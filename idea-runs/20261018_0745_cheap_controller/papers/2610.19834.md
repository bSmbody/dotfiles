# Teaching Agents in their Own Style: Blackbox Teacher Distillation for Web Agents

## What this paper does
WebKD distils a closed frontier teacher into Qwen-3.5 4B/9B and Gemma-4 E4B web agents. After behaviour cloning on teacher rollouts (P1), the student is rolled out (P2); for failed episodes the teacher localizes the first fatal step (P3) and rewrites every step from there on in the student's style using in-context exemplars of the student's own successes (P4). Training uses action-informative token weighting and a KL anchor on rationale tokens. Gains of 5.5-10.7 pp over BC, m-GRPO and teacher-editing baselines on TimeWarp, transferring to Online-Mind2Web.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What deficiency of offline imitation of teacher traces does it identify?
Offline imitation "trains the student on states it has never visited, and in a style it does not write in" [full text, Abstract, Sec 1]. Teacher edits carry a high "perplexity premium" for the student (Def 1, Eq 2), and under uniform SFT the loss concentrates on teacher-style rationale tokens while "the action tokens that fix the failure carry only a few percent of the loss" [Sec 3.2].
### Goal: What assumptions does its failure localization and correction rely on?
Transitions are "effectively deterministic" and the browser action is deterministically parsed [Sec 3.1]. The teacher sees the student's state because it is "re-rendered by replaying the student's original actions in the deterministic container" [Sec 3.3, P4]. Proposition 1 (unique first fatal step) assumes "deterministic transitions and parsing, a terminal binary reward" [App B.2]. Localization vs humans: under the budget reading the teacher is never late (early 93%); under loop-onset it is late ~10% [App B.2]. Each edited step is one independent teacher call [Sec 3.3, Eq 3].
### Goal: Where do its gains come from, and what fails without the external teacher?
Largest gains from "verifying the steps and answer +14.6pp and exiting loops +9.4pp" [Sec 4.2 Discussion]. Student self-edits fall below BC on every backbone: "without an external signal, the student cannot reliably tell which of its own decisions were wrong" [Sec 4.2]. Off-policy teacher takeover underperforms because it "drifts too far from the states the student has visited" [RQ3].
