# ASCT: Attentive Search over Counterfactual Trees for Credit Assignment in Agentic Reinforcement Learning

## What this paper does
ASCT trains an LLM workflow actor (agentic RAG on HotpotQA) with PPO whose per-decision credit comes from a training-time counterfactual search: at each actor-visited state it restores a recoverable copy of the prefix, executes every legal alternative action (Uniform, UCT, or cost-aware AgentUCT allocation) to terminal utility, builds a local Q table, centres it by the frozen actor's probabilities, and uses Q(s,a_t) - V(s) as the advantage of the actor's sampled action. It beats trajectory-return PPO and workflow-adapted VinePPO (0.6187 vs 0.5939 utility) with fewer auxiliary tokens.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does it evaluate alternative decisions at the actor's own states?
"Recoverable prefixes make this possible. From the same observations, an evaluator can execute alternative legal actions through later workflow decisions to terminal outcomes" [full text, Sec 1]. Each Q value averages terminal utilities of trials that start with action a [Sec 4, Eq 2]; the advantage is Q(s,a_t) minus the actor-weighted average [Eq 3-4], which equals a local policy-gradient signal [Sec 4]. Deployment uses the actor alone [Sec 4].
### Goal: What does it assume about determinism and randomness of the environment?
It requires recoverable execution and a finite legal action set ("Recoverable execution and finite legal actions delimit applicability") [Sec 7]. Alternatives are evaluated with "an independent random stream" [Sec 4]; there is no coupling of randomness across compared actions, and its environment's query refinement is deterministic by design [Sec 5.1, App A.3]. It reports fixed-state credit-sign agreement across repeats rising with budget (5 to 48 trials) [Sec 6, Fig 6].
### Goal: What does evaluating alternative actions buy over state-value MC credit?
All three ASCT evaluators beat VinePPO at every seed (paired gains 0.0293, 0.0354, 0.0098), and AgentUCT uses 50.3% fewer auxiliary tokens than VinePPO, chiefly by avoiding actor-continuation scoring [Sec 5.2-5.3, Table 3].
