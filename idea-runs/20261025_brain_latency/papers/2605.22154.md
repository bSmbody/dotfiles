# IdleSpec: Exploiting Idle Time via Speculative Planning for LLM Agents

## What this paper does
While an LLM agent waits for slow tool or environment responses, it iteratively drafts plan candidates. Progressive drafts assume the observation succeeds; recovery drafts assume it fails. Thompson sampling with posterior updates chooses which kind to draft. When the observation arrives, all drafts are fed to the next reasoning step as soft guidance. GAIA/FRAMES gain +5.1% with Gemini-2.5-Flash, and MLE-Bench gains up to +9.1% Any Medal.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it act in the environment during idle time, or only compute?
Only compute. The drafts are text plans aggregated into the prompt after the observation arrives [full text, Sec 4.1-4.2]. The agent is not forced to follow any draft. Nothing is executed during idle time and there are no irreversible side effects, so physical feasibility and hedging do not arise.

### Goal: How are candidate futures weighted?
Strategy choice (progressive vs recovery) is weighted by a Thompson-sampled posterior updated from forecast signals [Sec 4.3]. There is no calibrated model of which answer the slow component will return. Relevance here: digital "use the idle time on contingencies conditioned on the pending answer" is established. The proposal's physical, feasibility-valued version is a different setting, not an overlap.
