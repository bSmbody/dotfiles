# Reinforcement Learning with Random Delays

## What this paper does
Defines Random-Delay MDPs (RDMDP) with random observation delay ω and action delay α, shows the delayed observation must be augmented with a buffer of the last K sent actions (plus the delays) to restore the Markov property, and derives Delay-Correcting Actor-Critic (DCAC), which uses the known delays to resample trajectory fragments in hindsight for off-policy multi-step value estimation. MuJoCo with delays; ICLR 2021.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism maps a delayed high-level decision/observation onto the current state?
State augmentation: (s, u, ω, α) where u ∈ A^K is the action buffer; "the oldest actions along with the delayed observation describe the current state of the undelayed environment, whereas the most recent actions are yet to be applied" [full text, Section 2.1]. The learned policy maps this augmented state to an action; no explicit forward prediction. Timestamps on actions/observations are used to measure delays and discard superseded ones [Appendix A.1-A.2].

### Goal: What does the method assume about the slow decision-maker (trainable, white-box features, output type, delay scale)?
The delayed agent is the policy being trained by RL, acting every time-step on a continuous action space; the buffer length K equals the max total delay in steps [Section 2.1].

### Goal: What does it report as a limitation relevant to this axis?
Long maximum delays "would require a long action buffer"; observations beyond K should be discarded; run-length encoding suggested for long delays [Appendix A.3]. Time-steps smaller than the inference time "will require a few tricks" [Section 7].
