# DSDyn-VLA: A Dual-Stream Dynamic Manipulation Framework with Motion Perception, Future Awareness, and Realtime Correction

## What this paper does
Slow-fast dynamic manipulation: a slow Flow-Planner VLA with optical-flow input and future-state awareness produces action chunks; a fast 30 Hz RL residual policy (Res-Refiner) corrects them from current observations. Introduces DynBench (MuJoCo, nine moving-object tasks); reports >76% failure-rate reduction in high-latency Kinetix settings.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What latency does it address, and does it involve a high-level (frontier/VLM planner) brain?
VLA inference latency T_lat for the planner's chunks (execution horizon 0.5–1 s) [full text, Sec 3.3–3.4]. The "slow" stream is a VLA, not a frontier reasoner.

### Goal: What mechanism predicts the future state or reconciles a stale decision with the current state, and what does it assume?
Kinematics rollout: q̂_future = f_kin(q_query, Σ A_buffer) over T_lat is injected as proprioception, trained with random time-shifts pairing vision at t with state/actions at t+δ [Sec 3.3.2, Eq 5–6]. Object motion is handled only implicitly via optical-flow input, not predicted; the residual RL policy reconciles the chunk with the current observation at 30 Hz [Sec 3.4, Eq 7]. Removing future-state awareness drops real-world success to 36.5% [Sec 4.3]. Requires training the VLA and an RL residual.
