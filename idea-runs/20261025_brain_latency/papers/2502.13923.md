# Qwen2.5-VL Technical Report

## What this paper does
Technical report of the open-weight Qwen2.5-VL family (3B, 7B, 72B): a native-dynamic-resolution ViT trained from scratch (window attention in most layers, 2D-RoPE, 14×14 patches), an MLP merger that groups four adjacent patch features, and a Qwen2.5 LLM decoder with time-aligned MRoPE. Pre-training includes grounding data with absolute pixel coordinates (boxes and points, >10,000 categories, referring expressions). It reports results on VQA, documents, spatial grounding, video and agent benchmarks.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Which sizes are released and what is the 7B model made of (usable as a local, inference-only speculator)?
Three sizes, 3B, 7B and 72B [full text, Sec 2.1, Table 1]. All three share the redesigned ViT (hidden size 1280) and an MLP vision-language merger; the LLM is initialised from Qwen2.5 [Sec 2.1]. Images are resized to multiples of 28 and processed at native resolution [Sec 2.1, 2.1.1]. The report states nothing about GPU memory, so fit on a 24 GB card is an assumption of the proposal, not a claim of this paper.

### Goal: Can the model ground referring expressions to specific objects in an image, and how well does the 7B do on standard referring-expression benchmarks?
Yes: it is trained on box and point grounding with referring expressions and outputs coordinates in the input image's pixel frame [Sec 2.2.1, "Grounding Data with Absolute Position Coordinates"]. On RefCOCO/+/g the 7B scores 90.0 (RefCOCO val), 84.2 (RefCOCO+ val) and 87.2 (RefCOCOg val), against 92.7, 88.9 and 89.9 for the 72B and 73.2, 62.5, 75.2 for Gemini 1.5 Pro [Sec 3.3.3, Table 6]. These are appearance/location expressions; they do not test knowledge-laden or commonsense referents.
