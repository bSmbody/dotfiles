# Becoming a Fruit Ninja: Real-Time Probabilistic Kinodynamic Planning for Manipulator Projectile Interception

## What this paper does
FRUITNINJA is an anytime GPU-batched kinodynamic sampling-based planner that slices thrown objects with a blade on a Franka FR3. It grows a tree toward a nine-dimensional interception manifold (arrival time, contact point, blade orientation, speed) with exact cubic edges timed against the arm's dynamics limits. Plans are ranked by a risk-aware objective over object-position and arrival-time uncertainty. Reported cut rates: 96.7% of tosses in the open and 68.3% among five obstacles, vs 68.3% and 35.0% for the best of six baselines.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it involve uncertainty over which target to intercept, or a slow external decision-maker whose answer arrives late?
No. There is one known projectile. The uncertainty is over its future position, which grows with arrival time T, so plans are ranked by contact probability [full text, Sec III, Def III.2–III.3, Sec IV-B]. The planner replans at 50 Hz with a 1 kHz controller [Sec I]. There is no candidate set and no oracle latency. Its relevance to C3 is as an example of a reachability-window computation (minimum feasible time against actuator limits [Sec IV-D]) of the kind C3's certifier needs, at sub-second scale.
