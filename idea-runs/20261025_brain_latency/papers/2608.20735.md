# ForeTime-VLA: Causal Future-Token Distillation from a World Action Model for Conveyor-Belt Manipulation

## What this paper does
Distills a frozen Fast-WAM teacher's future-aware 64-D latent into π0.5: an eight-frame causal history encoder predicts the compressed future code, manipulation phase and normalized time-to-transition; four future tokens plus one phase token enter the VLM prefix and a residual conditions the action expert. Offline MAE improves 2.63%; real conveyor grasps 44/90 vs 23/90 for π0.5 (11/30 vs 2/30 at fast speed).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What latency does it address, and does it involve a high-level (frontier/VLM planner) brain?
Not a latency method per se: it targets reaction-time-limited grasping of moving objects by the low-level VLA [full text, Sec VI]. No high-level brain; the prediction horizon is not tied to any measured inference delay (it is tied to phase and time-to-transition) [Sec III-C].

### Goal: What mechanism predicts the future state or reconciles a stale decision with the current state, and what does it assume?
Learned future code from history, trained by distillation from a world-action-model teacher; requires fine-tuning π0.5 with added objectives [Sec III-C–E]. Evidence "limited to one conveyor-belt embodiment and a fixed offline video backbone" [Sec VI].
