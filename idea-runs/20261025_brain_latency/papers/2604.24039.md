# AgenticCache: Cache-Driven Asynchronous Planning for Embodied AI Agents

## What this paper does
Exploits "plan locality" in LLM-planned embodied agents: a runtime cache of frequent plan transitions proposes the next plan instantly, while a background updater asynchronously queries the LLM (GPT-5 family) to confirm or correct cached entries. On four multi-agent embodied benchmarks (TDW, graph environments) it raises success by 22% on average, cuts simulation latency by 65% and tokens by 50%.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does the system measure, ignore or compensate the brain's latency?
It measures latency and hides it by non-blocking execution of cached plans; it reviews prior strategies: synchronous plan-act stalls, and "Parallelized Planning-Acting ... queries the next plan while executing the current one ... the generated plan may become invalid when the environment changes" [full text, Sec 2.2].

### Goal: What mechanism reconciles a delayed LLM decision with the state reached in the meantime?
"After an asynchronous delay of k steps, it receives the LLM's response p'_{t+k} and compares this prediction against the executed plan trajectory {p_{t+1},…,p_{t+k}}": if p' already appears it is a confirmation; otherwise the cache is corrected and "the ongoing plan [is replaced] with p'_{t+k} if it is executable" [Sec 4.2]. Reconciliation operates on discrete symbolic plans only (no geometric content, no prediction of physical state); on a cache miss, high-level planning pauses [Sec 4.2].

### Goal: Relevance to continuous manipulation?
Benchmarks are symbolic/high-level multi-agent household tasks; no continuous control, moving objects, or spatial targets.
