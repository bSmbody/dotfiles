# FlashVLA: Streaming Action Decoding for Fast and Asynchronous VLA Inference

## What this paper does
Maintains a streaming buffer of action chunks at staggered noise levels decoded with chunk-wise causal attention, emitting one executable chunk per inference step (≥30 Hz on one GPU); continuity across asynchronous chunks becomes a structural property of the decoder.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What latency does it address, and does it involve a high-level (frontier/VLM planner) brain?
Millisecond-scale VLA decoding latency; asynchronous delay d=1–4 steps on LIBERO / RoboTwin 2.0 (97.5–98.3% on LIBERO) [full text, Sec 4.2]. No high-level brain.

### Goal: What mechanism predicts the future state or reconciles a stale decision with the current state, and what does it assume?
No explicit future-state prediction: future chunks "attend to the near-execution chunks already in flight", removing the need for "future-state conditioning" [Sec 2, Asynchronous VLA execution]. Assumes the stale part is a few control steps and that continuity of the executor's own trajectory is the main issue; requires multi-buffer fine-tuning [Sec 6].
