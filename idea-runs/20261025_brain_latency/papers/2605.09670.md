# Towards Generative Predictive Display for Vision-Based Teleoperation: A Zero-Shot Benchmark of Off-the-Shelf Video Models

## What this paper does
Zero-shot benchmark of five off-the-shelf generative video models (LTX family, SVD, Wan, etc.) as predictive displays: given frames up to I_{t-Δ}, roll out K future frames and display the one aligned with the current time. CARLA driving data at 15 FPS, short horizons (tens to hundreds of ms), metrics MAD and inference time.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism maps a delayed high-level decision/observation onto the current state?
Predictive display: forecast the current frame \tilde I_t = \hat I_{(t-Δ)+Δ} from delayed history with a video generator, so that the (human) decision-maker views the present rather than the past [full text, Sections II-A, II-B, Eqs. 1-2].

### Goal: What does the method assume about the slow decision-maker (trainable, white-box features, output type, delay scale)?
Decision-maker is a human operator viewing pixels; delay is "tens to hundreds of milliseconds" [Section II-C]; no closed-loop decision is evaluated.

### Goal: What does it report as a limitation relevant to this axis?
"None of the evaluated models, in their off-the-shelf zero-shot configuration ... satisfy the combined requirements of accuracy, temporal consistency, and real-time inference at the 15 FPS source rate"; diffusion models (SVD, Wan) show drift and non-monotonic error; no closed-loop or task-success study; no last-frame or flow-warping baselines [Sections VII, VII-A].
