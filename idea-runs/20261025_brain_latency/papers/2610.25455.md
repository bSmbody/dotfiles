# Self-Crystallizing Behavior Trees: Amortizing Foundation-Model Reasoning into Deterministic Control for Autonomous Drones (SCBT)

## What this paper does
SCBT keeps a deterministic BT in the drone's tick path. When a MissionCritical node fails during a learning phase, an LLM (Gemini 3.1 Pro, two schema-constrained calls) proposes one minimal subtree patch in a constrained grammar (WrapFallback, GuardWith, WrapRetry, ...), CMA-ES tunes its numeric ports in simulated replays, a three-stage admission pipeline (static, invariant monitors, empirical margin) gates a patch library, and a contextual bandit learns when to reuse stored patches vs. call the LLM. Once LLM-call rate is low, patches are crystallized into a frozen BT with no LLM. Evaluated in Project AirSim on wind-gust and battery failures.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does the brain pre-specify monitors and local recoveries that fire without re-calling the brain, and how are they parameterized?
Recoveries are LLM-authored but reactive-then-amortized, not pre-armed per stage: the LLM is called only after a node fails, and the resulting patch is reused for later episodes via the library/bandit [full text, Sec. IV-A, IV-D]. Patch parameters are tuned by CMA-ES rather than given by the LLM [Sec. IV-E]. After crystallization "no neural component is in the tick path" [Sec. I-A].

### Goal: Does it measure wall-clock latency/time saved by avoiding brain re-calls, and does the world keep evolving (no pause) during brain calls?
It quantifies brain latency — "a single structured proposal from a frontier model takes 31–105 s (two schema-constrained calls); a vehicle in a wind gust cannot wait for it" [Sec. I] — but during learning "the runner pauses the simulator" for each LLM call [Sec. IV-A, Fig. 1 caption: "with the live episode paused during each call"]. Savings are reported as LLM calls per episode (amortization curve), not as wall-clock or success under real-time latency [Sec. VI-C].

### Goal: What limitation does it report?
Does not generalize beyond failures met in the learning phase [Sec. VIII]; two failure types, one vehicle, headless simulator (no perception failures) [Sec. VIII].
