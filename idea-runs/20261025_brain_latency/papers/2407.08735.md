# Real-Time Anomaly Detection and Reactive Planning with Large Language Models

## What this paper does
AESOP (RSS 2024) pairs a fast embedding-based anomaly detector with a slow generative LLM (GPT-3.5/4 class) that, once an anomaly is flagged, chooses one of d pre-specified recovery interventions (or "continue"). A receding-horizon MPC keeps a tree of trajectories: a nominal plan plus one recovery trajectory per intervention, all sharing the first input, and the recovery branches sharing inputs for K steps, where K upper-bounds the slow reasoner's latency. So whatever the LLM picks is still feasible when its answer arrives. Evaluated on quadrotor sim/hardware and CARLA recreations of AV failures.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does AESOP hide a slow LLM reasoner's latency, and what plays the role of the contingency branches?
The MPC (Eq 3) optimizes |Y|+1 trajectories: nominal x^0 plus one per recovery set X_R^i, with consensus u^i_{t|t}=u^0_{t|t} and u^i_{t:t+K|t}=u^j_{t:t+K|t}, "in effect generating a branching tree of recovery trajectories" so the options "remain feasible throughout the K time steps it takes the monitor to decide" [full text, Sec IV-B, Eq 3, Alg 1]. The branches are over the slow reasoner's possible answers (which intervention), not over futures of the world. K is set from measured latency: GPT-3.5 mean 3.1 s, sd 0.85 s, K = 4.3 s (95% quantile) [App I-3].

### Goal: What does the LLM output, and is it conditioned on predicted future states or contingent on events not yet observed?
The LLM gets a VLM text description of the current observation and outputs one classification y in {0..d} from a fixed, executor-certified intervention set Y_t chosen so Eq 3 is feasible [Sec IV-A, Alg 1 line 5]. It does not see any prediction of the world at answer time and does not return a conditional plan; the contingency lives entirely in the executor's MPC. Robot dynamics are known; recovery sets are hand-defined or from reachability analysis [Sec III]. Future work: "whether fallbacks can be programmatically determined upon latency timeout" [Sec VI].
