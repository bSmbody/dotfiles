# EgoExo-Next: Benchmarking Vision-Language Models on Visual-Option Next-State and Cross-View Reasoning

## What this paper does
A 2,503-question four-choice benchmark where a VLM, given egocentric or exocentric observation sequences of a human action trajectory, must pick the image showing the subsequent visual state (or the cross-view match). Distractors include earlier and later states of the same trajectory. Evaluates proprietary, open and spatial VLMs against humans.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How well do frontier VLMs infer future visual states from observed motion on their own?
Poorly relative to humans: the best overall model (Gemini-3.1-Pro) reaches 43.81% average accuracy vs 98.55% for humans [full text, Sec 4.2]. GPT-6, evaluated only on the composed Ego-to-Exo next-state subtask, reaches 62.23% / 58.14% (indoor/outdoor) vs ~97% for humans [Sec 4.2]. Failure types include "Temporal Progression Failure" (choosing an earlier intermediate state) and confusing candidates "that differ only in subtle motion or state changes" [Sec 4.5]. Caveat for transfer: the trajectories are human hand-object actions, not simple kinematic object motion, so this bounds but does not directly measure a VLM's ability to extrapolate a rolling object from a motion trail.
