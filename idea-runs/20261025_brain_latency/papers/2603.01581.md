# KERV: Kinematic-Rectified Speculative Decoding for Embodied VLA Models

## What this paper does
Speculative decoding for token-based VLAs (DAC 2026). A draft model proposes action tokens that the VLA verifies. Instead of re-inferring on token errors, a kinematics-based Kalman filter predicts the action and compensates. A kinematic signal also adjusts the acceptance threshold dynamically. On LIBERO and other environments: 27–37% speedup with nearly no loss in success.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does "speculative" here mean guessing a slow high-level decision and executing physical motion on that guess before it is confirmed?
No. Speculation is at the level of decoding the VLA's own action tokens: draft, verify in parallel, accept or compensate [full text, Abstract, Sec 1, Sec 4]. It cuts the VLA's millisecond-scale inference cost. There is no separate slow semantic brain, no candidate set of targets, and no hedging of motion over possible answers. No overlap with C3's mechanism.
