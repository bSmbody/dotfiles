# Speculative Policy Orchestration: A Latency-Resilient Framework for Cloud-Robotic Manipulation

## What this paper does
SPO masks cloud network latency (tens to hundreds of ms) by having a cloud world model + policy autoregressively roll out K future kinematic waypoints that the edge buffers; an edge ε-tube verifier compares the measured state to the predicted state each control step and, on a miss, holds the robot and requests synchronous replanning; additive-increase/multiplicative-decrease adapts K. Evaluated on RLBench under emulated delays (>60% less idle time than blocking inference).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism predicts the future state or reconciles a stale decision with the current state, and what does it assume?
Prediction: ŝ_{k+1} = M_φ(ŝ_k, â_k), â_k = π_θ(ŝ_k), initialized from the latest physical state [full text, Sec IV-A]. Reconciliation: normalized distance e_t between measured and cached predicted state; inside ε the cached action runs, outside it the trajectory is invalidated and a zero-velocity hold is issued [Sec IV-C]. Assumes a low-dimensional state-space world model and a continuous policy in the cloud, and network delay of 30–100 ms [Sec III-B]; it does not deal with a semantic, seconds-scale decision from a black-box VLM.

### Goal: What limitation does it report?
Simulation-only with emulated delays; future work to "integrate larger-scale vision-language-action models within the SPO framework" [Sec VI].
