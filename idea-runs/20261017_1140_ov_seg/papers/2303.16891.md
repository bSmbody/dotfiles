# Mask-free OVIS: Open-Vocabulary Instance Segmentation without Manual Mask Annotations

## What this paper does
Mask-free OVIS (CVPR 2023) trains an open-vocabulary instance segmenter (Mask R-CNN) only on pseudo-masks generated from image-caption pairs. A pre-trained vision-language model (ALBEF) gives Grad-CAM activation maps for the object word from its cross-attention; an iterative masking step replaces the most activated region with the image mean and recomputes the map, so activation spreads to less discriminative parts; a weakly supervised proposal network and a small CNN turn the merged map into pseudo boxes and masks.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: (reviewer R1, C3) Is the model that takes the "second look" after erasing frozen or retrained, and what signal does the re-query produce?
Frozen: "we use pre-trained ALBEF as our vision-language model ... we directly employ the original setting of ALBEF and no additional modification is performed" [full text, Sec 4 implementation]. At each of G iterations "the most activated part of the object is replaced with image mean and the new activation map is computed", and the final map is the union of the thresholded maps [Sec 3.1, Eq 4, Fig 4]. The re-query asks the frozen VLM where else the object's evidence is (a localisation map), not whether the concept is still present, and it is used offline to build pseudo-labels for a trained segmenter, not at inference and not to choose crops or zoom. It therefore shows that erase-and-re-ask with a frozen recogniser predates the proposal, while leaving the residual presence test as a zoom gate open.

### Goal: (reviewer R1, C3) What happens when too much of the object is erased?
The ablation over G shows gains for the first few masking steps, but "performing too many mask iterations could completely mask out the object of interest and unrelated background regions might get activated", giving a poor guidance map [Sec 4.3, Fig 6]. This is the frozen-model analogue of Adversarial Erasing's context failure: once the object's own evidence is hidden, the frozen VLM's response moves to background rather than vanishing.
