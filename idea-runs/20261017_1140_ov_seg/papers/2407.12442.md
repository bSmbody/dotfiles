# ClearCLIP: Decomposing CLIP Representations for Dense Vision-Language Inference

## What this paper does
Training-free CLIP OVSS. Decomposes the final-block output of CLIP's ViT into the residual stream X_res and the attention output X_attn and shows that X_res alone gives ~0 mIoU while X_attn alone beats the sum (COCO-Stuff, B/16: X_sum 4.4, X_res 0.01, X_attn 11.6). Attributes noise to a few dominant channels in the residual stream that all tokens share. ClearCLIP modifies only the last block: removes the residual connection, removes the FFN, and uses query-query self-self attention, outputting Proj(Attn_qq · v).

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism does it use to suppress spatial noise in CLIP patch tokens, at which layer(s), and on which quantity (attention weights, value content, whole pathways)?
Only the final transformer block is changed [full text, Section 3.3 "Our solution", Eq. 6]. Three edits: (1) the residual connection of the last block is discarded; (2) the last FFN is discarded; (3) q-k attention is replaced by self-self attention (q-q by default). The dense feature is X_attn = Proj(Attn_qq · v), where v is computed from the unmodified penultimate state. So the content that is classified is still the penultimate-layer value vectors; the method changes the mixing weights and deletes whole pathways. An alpha-scaling study (X_sum = X_res + alpha·X_attn) shows mIoU rises monotonically with alpha, i.e. the gain comes from suppressing the residual [full text, Section 3.3 "Residual connection"; Section 4.2 "Effect of amplifying the norm"]. Appendix B: removing the top-beta high-value residual channels helps, best at beta >= 70%, and normalising X_res is comparable; they then drop all residual channels [full text, Appendix 0.B, Table 4].

### Goal: What does it identify as the source of the noise in CLIP patch tokens?
The residual stream. Its normalised entropy collapses to near zero from the middle layers on in CLIP-B/16 (not in DINO-B/16), maximum activations grow ~90x with depth, and a few channels dominate the peak values; because most token vectors share these peak dimensions, cosine similarity cannot separate them [full text, Section 3.3, Fig. 4]. They attribute this to image-level supervision emphasising a global feature direction in the residual latent space [full text, Section 1; Section 5].

### Goal: What limitation of attention-only modification does it report, and does it scale to larger backbones?
Prior self-self attention fixes (MaskCLIP, CLIPSurgery, SCLIP) "fail to obtain reasonable results" on ViT-L/14: Attn_qq and Attn_kk are even worse than vanilla q-k there, because the residual norm is much larger in L/14; attention modification is effective "only when the influence (or norm) of X_res is minimal" [full text, Section 3.2; Section 3.3, Fig. 2]. The paper notes the residual-domination phenomenon "becomes more pronounced in larger vision transformer models with deeper layers" [full text, Section 3.3]. Its own remedy discards the penultimate state's residual entirely rather than separating its local from its global content.
