# SCI-CLIP: Segment-Centric Inference with Reference Memory for Training-Free Open-Vocabulary Segmentation

## What this paper does
SCI-CLIP makes region masks from an auxiliary mask generator (SAM2, Mask2Former, EoMT or SegFormer) the unit of inference: DINO patch affinity is restricted to within-region pairs after DBSCAN merging of similar regions, a Gaussian spatial prior is added, CLIP values are propagated by this affinity, and cross-window key-value retrieval blends in long-range context. Segment embeddings are also matched against an offline reference memory of labelled segments and late-fused with the dense scores. Evaluated on eight benchmarks.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: (axis: small objects and stuff classes) What mechanism does it use that affects small or thin objects, and at what resolution/scale is the class decision made?
SCI-CLIP restricts DINO patch affinity to pairs inside the same region of an external mask generator (SAM2, Mask2Former, EoMT or SegFormer), after DBSCAN-merging regions with similar mean DINO features (Eq 4-5, "Mask merging") [full text, Sec 3.3, 4]. A Gaussian spatial prior is added (Eq 6-8), then CLIP values are propagated by this affinity; cross-window KV aggregation retrieves values from a global bank and blends them with fixed λ_kv (Eq 10-11) [Sec 3.4]. Small objects therefore depend on the mask generator isolating them and on the merge step not fusing them.

### Goal: (axis: small objects and stuff classes) What mechanism does it use for stuff classes / large regions / many-class benchmarks, and what global context or class-competition handling does it apply?
The Gaussian prior "is particularly useful for contiguous stuff regions" [Sec 3.3]. A reference memory of segment embeddings from reference images with image-level label incidence is retrieved at segment level and late-fused with dense scores (Eq 13-17) [Sec 3.5-3.6]; removing it costs −2.1 average, largest on C60, V21 and Stuff [App. C].

### Goal: (axis: small objects and stuff classes) What does it report as its limitation or failure on small objects or stuff classes, and which knobs are set per dataset?
The Gaussian (locality) prior "improves C60 and VOC20, but slightly degrades City and VOC21 when applied in isolation, suggesting that locality bias alone can become overly restrictive when long-range evidence is needed"; cross-window KV restores it [Sec 4.4]. Removing cross-window KV causes "a pronounced drop on City" [App. C]. Late-fusion and retrieval calibration values are dataset-specific [Sec 4, Table 1(b)]. Qualitatively claims better thin structures (poles, signs) and small-object recall [App. B]; remaining errors in highly ambiguous/occluded regions.
