# Self-Calibrated CLIP for Training-Free Open-Vocabulary Segmentation

## What this paper does
SC-CLIP, a training-free CLIP OVSS method (IEEE TIP). Identifies "anomaly tokens" that attract disproportionate attention from all normal patches and appear as PCA outliers. (1) Detects ~10 anomaly tokens in the penultimate-layer state with Local Outlier Factor and replaces them by the mean of their non-anomalous 3x3 neighbours; (2) self-adjusting: uses CLIP mid-layer patch similarity to aggregate deep features and to augment k-k attention; (3) two-pass multi-level fusion L(X^penul) + L(sum of mid-layer features) through the last block. No extra backbone.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism does it use to suppress spatial noise in CLIP patch tokens, at which layer(s), and on which quantity (attention weights, value content, tokens)?
Anomaly resolution acts on token content, but only on the ~10 detected tokens (~5% of ViT-B/16 tokens) and only in X^penul, the input to the last block: LOF flags outliers and each is replaced by the mean of its 3x3 neighbours excluding other anomalies [full text, Section III-B, Eq. 3; Section IV-C(a)]. The self-adjusting step builds Simi^mid (cosine similarity of mid-layer features) and uses it to re-aggregate deep features, X^deep_p = sum_q Norm(Simi^mid_pq) X^deep_q, and to augment attention: softmax(KK^T) + softmax(Simi^mid) [full text, Section III-C, Eqs. 4-5]. The two-pass strategy passes summed mid-layer features (layers 4-9) through the last block, which realigns them to the last-layer space (similarity 0.094 -> 0.983) [full text, Section III-D].

### Goal: What does it identify as the source of the noise, and does it say the damage is confined to the anomaly tokens?
Anomaly tokens draw excessive attention from normal tokens in deep layers, causing uniform attention and "feature homogenization" [full text, Section I; Section III-B]. It states explicitly that after resolving them "a challenge still remains: anomaly tokens have already caused substantial disruption to other normal tokens in the previous layers, diminishing their local awareness" [full text, Section III-C]. ROC analysis on 2000 ADE20K images: mid-layer patch-similarity AUC 0.76 (DINO 0.77) vs last layer 0.66 [full text, Section III-C, Fig. 4].

### Goal: Can the anomaly tokens be removed earlier in the network, and what limitation does the paper report?
No. Table V (VOC21): resolving anomaly tokens starting from layer 1..11 gives 33.5, 36.3, 40.4, 43.5, 44.6, 45.6, 44.7, 38.0, 8.1, 12.4, 22.1 mIoU vs 60.3 when applied only at the last layer; self-self attention applied to non-last layers likewise collapses (0.3-13.8). The authors explain that "the training-free setting relies on the feature modeling of earlier CLIP layers", so modifications only work at the last layer [full text, Section IV-C, Table V]. Failure cases: distant traffic sign missed, coarse car boundaries, attributed to CLIP resolution and training paradigm [full text, Section IV-F]; they conclude fundamental fixes need architecture/training changes [full text, Section V].
