# Plug-in Feedback Self-adaptive Attention in CLIP for Training-free Open-Vocabulary Segmentation

## What this paper does
FSA (ICCV 2025), a training-free plug-in for CLIP OVSS methods (MaskCLIP, SCLIP, ClearCLIP, ProxyCLIP). Argues that improved intermediate attention may not survive later operations and never sees text. It computes the output logits, isolates the effect of the learned attention by subtracting logits from a parallel uniform-attention branch, builds patch-pair similarity from KL divergence of the isolated predictions, sparsifies it by a cumulative-confidence cutoff, and feeds it back as attention (ensemble of three ways of combining with the initial attention) over the same value vectors.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism does it use to suppress spatial noise in CLIP patch tokens, at which layer(s), and on which quantity?
Attention weights of the final attention computation only. All three adaptations, A^f·(Attn^init·V), Attn^init·(A^f·V) and A^f·V, and their ensemble ((A^f·Attn^init + Attn^init·A^f + A^f)/3)·V, change how V is mixed; V itself is unchanged [full text, Section 4.3, Eqs. 11-14]. A^f comes from softmax of a sparsified similarity S = 1/(KL+1) between patch predictions, keeping entries until cumulative confidence p = 0.45, with lambda = 2 [full text, Section 4.2, Eqs. 9-10].

### Goal: How does it separate image-global from attention-specific evidence in the logits?
Attention isolation: a parallel branch with uniform attention Attn^uni = (1/L)·1 goes through the same sub-modules and text alignment; its logits Y_uni (the image-global readout) are subtracted, Y_iso = Softmax(Y - Y_uni), to keep only what the selective attention contributes [full text, Section 4.1, Eq. 6]. This subtraction is used only to build the feedback similarity; the final segmentation is the argmax of the adapted logits from Eqs. 11-14, without subtracting the global term [full text, Section 4.3].

### Goal: What limitation of prior intermediate-attention fixes does it identify?
"Enhanced intermediate coherence may deteriorate due to subsequent operations and hence might not translate into improved segmentation", and "intermediate attention does not interface directly with class information" [full text, Section 1]. Its remedy remains a re-weighting of the same last-layer values by self-predicted affinities.
