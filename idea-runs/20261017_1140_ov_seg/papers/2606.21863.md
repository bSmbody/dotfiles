# Prompt-Calibrated SAM 3 for Open-Vocabulary Remote Sensing Semantic Segmentation

## What this paper does
ProC-SAM3 (IEEE GRSL 2026) adapts frozen SAM 3 to remote-sensing OVSS through its prompt interface: an offline MLLM-built and constrained prompt pool per category, cached text embeddings for batched inference, and Presence-Guided Residual Fusion that gates each prompt's decoder outputs by presence and instance confidence and aggregates prompts by a pixelwise maximum to keep small-object activations. It reports 56.1 average mIoU on eight remote-sensing benchmarks, +3.9 over SegEarth-OV3.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: (innovator C2) How does it treat small objects, at what resolution, and on what hardware?
Its small-object fix is on the aggregation side: averaging several prompts' responses "tends to suppress local activations of small or narrow targets", so it takes a pixelwise max over presence-gated prompt responses and then an argmax over categories [full text, Sec II-C]. Inputs "are resized to 1008×1008 by the SAM3 processor"; no crop, window or zoom stage is used [Sec III-A]. All experiments run training-free "on a single RTX 4090 GPU (24GB)", with the image encoder in half precision [Sec III-A]. So the small-object remedy redistributes the evidence of one 1008-px pass rather than re-acquiring evidence.
