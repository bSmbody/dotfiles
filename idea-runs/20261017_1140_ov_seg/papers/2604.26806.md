# ViCrop-Det: Spatial Attention Entropy Guided Cropping for Training-Free Small-Object Detection

## What this paper does
ViCrop-Det (2026-04) is a training-free, plug-in refinement for DETR-style closed-set detectors. From one full-image pass it builds a class-agnostic heatmap from aggregated decoder cross-attention, scores multi-scale sliding windows by mean attention times normalised attention entropy, keeps at most K = 5 windows that are both salient and ambiguous, re-runs the detector on each resized crop, and merges crop detections with the original ones by class-wise NMS and score blending. It improves small-object AP on VisDrone, DOTA-v1.5 and COCO at a 20–25% throughput cost.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: (innovator C2) What decides where it crops, how many crops, and how are crop results fused?
Windows at relative scales 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 are scored by σ(w) = m(w)·Ĥ_s(w), where m is the mean of the class-agnostic attention heatmap and Ĥ_s its normalised entropy in the window; windows must pass m ≥ 0.3 and Ĥ_s ≥ 0.7, then class-agnostic NMS and top-K = 5 [full text, Sec 3.2–3.4]. A global entropy gate skips easy images [Sec 3.1]. Thresholds come from a small grid search on validation splits of DOTA-v1.5 and VisDrone and are then frozen [Sec 3.1]. Crop detections are mapped back, score-boosted by entropy, and merged by class-wise NMS with s_f = 0.7 s_o + 0.3 s_r [Sec 3.5]. Selection is a single stage and class-agnostic; nothing is recursively zoomed.

### Goal: (innovator C2) What evidence does it give for selective versus uniform cropping, and for the risks of adding crops?
Against uniform slicing, SAHI and ASAHI give smaller APS gains at lower speed: ViCrop-Det is 1.47–1.60× faster than SAHI with +2.7–4.8 APS [Sec 4.2]. At an equal crop budget, entropy-guided selection beats random windows by +1.4 (VisDrone) and +0.9 (DOTA) [Sec 4.3]. But more crops can hurt: K = 7 drops mAP50 to 34.1 from 38.9 at K = 5, attributed to redundant hypotheses, fusion conflicts and budget spent on lower-entropy regions "that amplify false positives" [Sec 4.3].
