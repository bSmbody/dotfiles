# SAM 3: Segment Anything with Concepts

## What this paper does
SAM 3 is a unified model that detects, segments and tracks all instances of a concept given a short noun phrase or image exemplars (Promptable Concept Segmentation). Its image detector is a DETR-style encoder–decoder on a Perception Encoder backbone: a fusion encoder conditions image tokens on the prompt, a decoder with 200 object queries predicts boxes and masks, a MaskFormer-style head gives instance and semantic masks, and a learned presence token decides whether the concept is in the image at all. It is trained on a large data engine (SA-Co, 4M unique concepts with hard negatives) and doubles prior systems' PCS accuracy; it also reports open-vocabulary semantic segmentation on ADE-847, PC-59 and Cityscapes.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: (innovator C2) What does the presence head compute, at what granularity, and how does it combine with the dense outputs?
The presence token is "solely responsible for predicting whether the target concept ... is present in the image/frame", i.e. p(NP is present in input); each object query then only solves localisation conditioned on presence, and the final query score is the product of the two [full text, Sec 3 "Presence Token"; App C.2 "Presence Head"]. The motivation is that recognition needs "contextual cues from the entire image", while forcing each query to be globally aware "conflicts with the inherently local nature of the localization objective" [Sec 3; App C.2]. The same presence score gates the semantic segmentation head, whose binary mask head is trained only on positives [App C.2]. Presence is supervised with image-level presence/absence by binary cross-entropy, and decoupling it brings significant gains, particularly on IL_MCC [App A.1]. The presence decision is therefore made once per concept per input image: run on a crop, it is a pooled "is this concept anywhere in this view?" test.

### Goal: (innovator C2) At what input resolution does SAM 3 run, and how are non-square images handled at evaluation?
The vision encoder processes a 1008-pixel image split into 3×3 windows of 336 px for windowed attention, with global attention in 4 of 32 layers [App C.2, "Image and Text Encoders"]. "The input resolution of our model is always a fixed square (usually 1008×1008). During evaluation, the images are resized to this size, without respecting their aspect ratio" [App C.4, geometric augmentation paragraph]. For semantic segmentation evaluation, instance masks are predicted per category and filtered by presence scores, mask scores and mask areas [App, semantic segmentation evaluation]. The model has about 850M parameters (≈450M vision encoder, ≈300M text encoder, ≈100M detector/tracker) [App C.1]. The data engine explicitly mines "images with very small objects" as a hard case [Sec 4, Phase 3 / App].
