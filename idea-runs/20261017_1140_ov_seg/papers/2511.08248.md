# NERVE: Neighbourhood & Entropy-guided Random-walk for training free open-Vocabulary sEgmentation

## What this paper does
NERVE refines CLIP cross-attention label probabilities with a random walk with restart on Stable Diffusion self-attention tokens. Its transition matrix is a fixed mix of a global (query-key cosine) and an 8-neighbour local affinity, and attention heads are weighted by the entropy of the label distribution they induce, averaged over the image. A low-rank truncated walk keeps it efficient. No CRF/PAMR post-processing; evaluated on seven benchmarks and as a WSSS pseudo-label generator.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: (axis: small objects and stuff classes) What mechanism does it use that affects small or thin objects, and at what resolution/scale is the class decision made?
NERVE diffuses CLIP cross-attention label probabilities G (query tokens vs class-prompt keys, Eq 11) with a random walk with restart, P = (1−α)Σ α^t S^t G (Eq 2), over Stable Diffusion self-attention tokens [full text, Sec 3.1-3.3]. The transition matrix mixes a global affinity (cosine of SD queries/keys, Eq 8) and an 8-neighbour local affinity (Eq 9) as S = βS_global + (1−β)S_local (Eq 10) — one β for the whole image [Sec 3.2]. Inputs resized to 336×336 with CLIP ViT-L/14 [Sec 4.1].

### Goal: (axis: small objects and stuff classes) What mechanism does it use for stuff classes / large regions / many-class benchmarks, and what global context or class-competition handling does it apply?
Head weighting by uncertainty: each attention head's weight is exp(−c·H^(h)), where H^(h) is the entropy of the one-step label distribution averaged over all N tokens of the image (Eq 12-15) [Sec 3.4] — an image-level score, so heads are chosen by how confident they make the image overall, dominated by large regions.

### Goal: (axis: small objects and stuff classes) What does it report as its limitation or failure on small objects or stuff classes, and which knobs are set per dataset?
Motivation criticises fixed-size Gaussian kernels and isotropic neighbourhoods [Abstract, Sec 1]. Qualitatively the walk sharpens boundaries and fills object parts over steps [Sec 4.4]. No analysis of small objects or stuff classes and no limitation section on scale.
