# CoCo-SAM3: Harnessing Concept Conflict in Open-Vocabulary Semantic Segmentation

## What this paper does
CoCo-SAM3 (April 2026) is a training-free OVSS pipeline on frozen SAM 3. It splits inference into intra-class enhancement and inter-class competition. Synonym prompts generated offline by an LLM are aggregated by a LogSumExp over Perception Encoder similarity maps (Synonym Aggregation). The cross-class softmax of this semantic evidence is then injected as a log prior into SAM 3's per-pixel mask logit, plus SAM 3's image-level presence logit, so that all classes compete on one comparable scale (Semantic Evidence Calibration). It reports 64.3 mean mIoU over the eight standard benchmarks against 57.5 for vanilla SAM 3, at almost the same cost.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: (reviewer R3, C3) What mIoU does CoCo-SAM3 report on the eight benchmarks, and how does it compare with ActiveSAM's numbers?
Table 1 [full text, Sec 5.2]: CoCo-SAM3 V21 86.8, PC60 50.5, COCO-O 67.9, V20 95.2, PC59 61.2, COCO-S 43.6, City 70.7, ADE 38.3, average 64.3. Vanilla SAM 3 in the same table: 81.9, 46.1, 65.4, 88.9, 50.0, 33.3, 62.3, 31.8, average 57.5. Against the ActiveSAM table (average 66.0, City 73.0, ADE 40.0, COCO-S 44.9; see papers/2606.16996.md), CoCo-SAM3 is about 1.7 mIoU lower on average and 2.3 lower on Cityscapes, but higher than SegEarth-OV3's reported 63.9 average. CoCo-SAM3 does not compare against SegEarth-OV3 or ActiveSAM; ActiveSAM's paper does not list CoCo-SAM3 either (no mention in its text). It is therefore a second, independent SAM 3 training-free base of near-SOTA strength.

### Goal: (reviewer R3, C3) At what input resolution or tiling does it run SAM 3, and does it do anything for small or missed objects?
"The input image is resized to 1008×1008 pixels to match SAM3's native resolution", with no data augmentation or post-processing [Sec 5.1, Experimental Details]. There is no tiling, crop or zoom stage. Its mechanism re-weights the evidence of one pass (prior from PE similarity, presence logit added as a global bias) [Sec 4.1, Sec 4.2], so like ActiveSAM it cannot create evidence for an instance that the single squeezed pass did not see. Small objects are not analysed specifically.

### Goal: (reviewer R3, C3) What is its compute per image, and on what hardware?
Table 5: on RTX 4090s with prompt chunks of 16, vanilla SAM 3 takes 902 ms/img and 10,563 MB; CoCo-SAM3 934 ms/img and 10,722 MB, averaged over the eight benchmarks [Sec 5.3, Table 5]. This is a useful reference point for a 24 GB consumer card: one full-vocabulary SAM 3 pass at 1008 px costs about 0.9 s on a 4090 when every prompt is decoded.

# CoCo-SAM3: Harnessing Concept Conflict in Open-Vocabulary Semantic Segmentation

## What this paper does
CoCo-SAM3 (2026-04) is a training-free OVSS method on frozen SAM 3. It argues that per-prompt SAM 3 masks lack a common evidence scale across classes, so classes overwrite each other, and that synonyms activate inconsistent evidence. It computes a cross-class softmax prior from intermediate Perception Encoder features matched to (LLM-expanded, LogSumExp-aggregated) synonym text embeddings, and adds it in logit space to SAM 3's mask logit and image-level presence logit before a per-pixel argmax. It reports 64.3 mean mIoU on the eight standard benchmarks, with one 1008x1008 pass per image.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: (reviewer R1, C3) Is it training-free, at what input resolution does it run, and does it use any tiling, multi-scale or second look at small objects?
Training-free with the official SAM 3 (PE-L+) kept frozen; "the input image is resized to 1008x1008 pixels to match SAM3's native resolution", with no augmentation or post-processing, on 4x RTX 4090 [full text, Sec 5.1 "Experimental Details"]. The score is S_c(x) = logit(P_c^sam(x)) + λ log π_c(x) + z_c, where z_c is the image-level presence logit broadcast to every pixel [Sec 4.1, Eq 4]. Synonym aggregation works only on the PE-feature branch and adds no SAM 3 decoding calls [Sec 4.2]. There is no crop, tile, zoom or re-encoding of any region: like ActiveSAM and SegEarth-OV3 it redistributes the evidence of one squeezed pass.

### Goal: (reviewer R1, C3) What mIoU does it report on the eight benchmarks, and how does it compare with vanilla SAM 3 and the CLIP-based training-free methods?
Table 1 [Sec 5.2]: CoCo-SAM3 V21 86.8, PC60 50.5, COCO-O 67.9, V20 95.2, PC59 61.2, COCO-S 43.6, City 70.7, ADE 38.3, average 64.3. Vanilla SAM 3 in the same table: 81.9 / 46.1 / 65.4 / 88.9 / 50.0 / 33.3 / 62.3 / 31.8, average 57.5. Best CLIP-VFM training-free row ReME: City 59.0, ADE 28.2, average 55.2. Compared with ActiveSAM's reported 66.0 average (City 73.0, ADE 40.0) it is lower on average, but higher on V21 (86.8 vs 83.4) and PC59 (61.2 vs 60.4). It is a further frozen-SAM 3 single-pass base pipeline that a plug-in like C3 could wrap.
