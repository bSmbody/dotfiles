# Training-Free Class Purification for Open-Vocabulary Semantic Segmentation

## What this paper does
FreeCP (ICCV 2025) argues that over-complete vocabularies cause class redundancy (absent classes activated) and visual-language ambiguity (similar classes on the same region). It refines CLIP CAMs with CLIP self-attention affinity, removes classes whose CAM changes too much under refinement (IoU below a threshold), groups classes whose refined maps overlap, and resolves each group by cropping the ambiguous region, re-encoding it with CLIP and matching LLM fine-grained descriptions. A plug-in for MaskCLIP, GEM, ClearCLIP and SCLIP on eight benchmarks.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: (axis: small objects and stuff classes) What mechanism does it use that affects small or thin objects, and at what resolution/scale is the class decision made?
FreeCP refines class activation maps M with a class-agnostic self-affinity SA (average of CLIP self-attention maps over layers, at H/16×W/16) [full text, Sec 3.2, Eq 2-3]. Redundancy Purification removes class i from the image's vocabulary when the IoU between M_i and its refined version falls below T_rp (Eq 4-5). Ambiguity Purification groups surviving classes whose refined maps overlap (IoU > T_ap, DFS), crops the bounding box of their averaged high-response region, resizes it to 112×112, re-encodes it with CLIP and assigns the crop to argmax over LLM fine-grained descriptions; losers are zeroed only in that region (Eq 6-8) [Sec 3.3.2]. This crop re-encoding is triggered only for ambiguity groups among classes that already survived the global redundancy test.

### Goal: (axis: small objects and stuff classes) What mechanism does it use for stuff classes / large regions / many-class benchmarks, and what global context or class-competition handling does it apply?
The paper locates the many-class failure in class competition: restricting refinement to ground-truth classes lifts SCLIP from ADE 15.6→37.5 and Stuff 22.1→42.5, whereas refinement over the full vocabulary collapses (ADE 7.6, Stuff 13.4) because class-agnostic refinement "unintentionally reinforces activation for irrelevant categories" [Table 1, Sec 3.2]. Examples of stuff ambiguity: leaves/bush/tree, river/water [Sec 1].

### Goal: (axis: small objects and stuff classes) What does it report as its limitation or failure on small objects or stuff classes, and which knobs are set per dataset?
Image-level multi-label filtering "often misidentifies small or less prominent object classes as redundant" [Sec 1]. FreeCP's thresholds (T_rp, T_ap) are set per dataset from prior knowledge: low for ADE/Stuff "to retain small or rare regions", higher for VOC/Object "to suppress noise" [Sec 4.2]. Gains on stuff-heavy sets are small relative to the GT-vocabulary oracle: SCLIP+RP-AP reaches ADE 18.4 (baseline 17.2) and Stuff 24.9 (baseline 23.2) [Table 3]; ambiguity purification helps less on complex-class datasets [Sec 4.4].
