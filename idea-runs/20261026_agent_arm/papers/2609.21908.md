# CommitFlow: Semantic Commitment Verification and Local Correction for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation VLA Execution

## What this paper does
Wraps a frozen VLA for long-horizon (incl. bimanual handover) tasks with (i) a Semantic Commitment Monitor that checks stage-specific physical conditions (holding, support, contact, release) from a GRU state estimator over RGB-D features, metric relations and joint state, holding back dependent actions until conditions are verified; (ii) BoundaryFlow, a trainable flow branch that adds corrective action chunks to the base action; (iii) relation/gain calibration from successful data. Re-verification precedes resuming.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does it represent and verify the physical conditions a step must establish?
An adapter defines templates for pre-alignment, reaching, grasp closure and lifting, "specifying objects, arm roles, physical conditions, geometric relations, and subsequent actions"; SCM checks "the adapter's required conditions C_e against state estimates, metric relations, and history"; conditions are pending/blocking/violated; "Stage predictions cannot override unresolved commitments" [full text, Sec III-A, III-B, Eq 3–4]. Observer trained on annotated causal windows with "Rule-generated labels" [Sec III-B]. SCM Macro-F1 88.98% stage, 94.89% event over 1,971 frames [Sec III-B].

### Goal: What limitation does it state?
"Runtime verification remains dependent on RGB-D perception and temporal state estimation"; "Task-dependent event and precondition definitions also limit extension to open-ended tasks and unfamiliar interaction relations"; future work: adapters that "infer event structures, manipulation roles, and physical preconditions from observations and language instructions" [Sec V].
