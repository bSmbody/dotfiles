# SPARK: Strategic Policy-Aware Exploration via Dynamic Branching for Long-Horizon Agentic Learning

## What this paper does
RL for long-horizon LLM agents (ACL 2026). Rollouts start from M roots; at each step the agent's reasoning may emit an <explore> tag, which branches B continuations from that history under a global leaf budget N; completed leaves get binary terminal rewards and are used in a GRPO-style update. Cold-start SFT (300 trajectories) teaches the tag. Evaluated with Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B on ALFWorld, ScienceWorld and WebShop.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does it allocate exploration and assign credit in long-horizon agent RL?
Branching criterion B(z_t) = B if "<explore>" appears in the reasoning trace, else 1 [full text, Sec 2.2 Eqs 4-5]; budget enforcement caps leaves at N=8 (M=4 roots, B=2) [Sec 2.2 Stage 3, Sec 3.1]. Credit: "Completed leaf trajectories from the same task are grouped and assigned binary terminal rewards, which are propagated to all steps. Shared prefixes induce comparable alternatives" [Stage 4]. Branching from a mid-episode history means continuing several copies of the environment from the same state; its environments are text simulators, and the paper does not discuss the cost of doing so in slow or physical environments.

### Goal: What limitation does it report?
The branching signal relies on the agent's self-awareness; it "may not fully exploit exploration opportunities in extremely low-capability base models where the agent's self-awareness is limited" [Limitations].
