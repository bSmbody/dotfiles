# MADRA: Multi-Agent Debate for Risk-Aware Embodied Planning

## What this paper does
Training-free risk assessment for embodied task planning: several LLM agents give initial safe/unsafe assessments of an instruction, a critical evaluator scores them (logic, risk identification, evidence, clarity), agents debate, and consensus voting decides; plus a hierarchical planning framework with memory and self-evolution. New SafeAware-VH dataset (VirtualHome); also AI2-THOR.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: At what point and on what input is the safety decision taken?
Once, on the instruction text before planning, as a plug-in module; baselines (LLM-Planner, ProgPrompt, CoELA, etc.) reject <10% of unsafe tasks while MADRA reaches ~91% rejection with low over-rejection [full text, Sec 3, Sec 6.3.1].

### Goal: Limitations?
"Our approach focuses on semantic planning without visual integration, creating a simulation-to-reality gap" [Sec 7]. It classifies instructions; it does not monitor the physical consequences of executed actions.
