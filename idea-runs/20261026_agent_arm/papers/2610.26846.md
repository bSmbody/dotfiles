# GPT-6 Astra Is a Native Visuo-Tactile Policy for Contact-Rich Manipulation

## What this paper does
Feeds GelSight-style tactile RGB images to GPT-6 Astra (xhigh reasoning) through its ordinary image interface, together with scene cameras, robot state, demonstration blocks and RoboICL-style interaction memory; Astra outputs end-effector delta and gripper commands. On 19 UniVTAC/OpenViTac tasks (10% stratified sample, 10 scenes per task) it reaches 93.8%/81.8% mean success, compares vision-only vs visuo-tactile on the same scenes, and analyzes contact inference, probing, fine adjustment and recovery.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does the agent judge contact state and step success during execution?
No separate verifier: Astra reads tactile and scene images each round and writes short action notes; "Execution feedback distinguishes a completed command from achieved task progress: an insertion command can still leave an object blocked at the rim" [full text, Sec 3.3]. Successful cases include active probing — relaxing the grasp after lifting to get a discriminative response, detecting slip and re-establishing contact — and a "retention test" in Grasp Chip [Sec 5.2.2–5.2.3].

### Goal: What verification-related failures does it report?
Weak tactile cues are misread (Weight Classify: commits to a light-object interpretation "without resolving that ambiguity") [Sec 6.1]; Insert HDMI: obstruction recognized but correction direction not resolved over 600 actions — "Effective diagnosis requires probes that discriminate between competing explanations and subsequent decisions that preserve what those probes established" [Sec 6.2]; Empty-Can Selection: repeated probing leaves 30 of 400 actions to finish — "Information gathering must be coordinated with a stopping rule" [Sec 6.3, Fig 16]. Touch decreases 4 tasks [Sec 6].

### Goal: What study limitations does it state?
Simulation only; ten episodes per task per setting; task-specific demonstrations [Sec 8].
