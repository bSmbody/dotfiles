# Do What? Teaching Vision-Language-Action Models to Reject the Impossible

## What this paper does
Findings EMNLP 2025. Instruct-Verify-and-Act (IVA) builds on LLARVA and instruction-tunes a VLA on a semi-synthetic RLBench dataset of paired valid and false-premise instructions (referring to absent objects/conditions) so the model detects infeasible requests, answers with clarification/correction in language, and grounds plausible alternatives in action.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What does "reject" mean here and what mechanism produces it?
Rejection is of infeasible (false-premise) instructions, not harmful ones; learned via supervised instruction tuning on paired positive/false-premise prompts with the structured LLARVA template [full text, Sec 3]. It is a perception-grounded feasibility judgment made from the instruction and the current frame.

### Goal: Limitations?
RLBench-generated simulated data, artificially balanced in/out-of-domain premises; real-world robustness not validated; responses limited to premise types seen in training; short structured instructions, no multi-turn clarification; overhead of LMM backbone [Limitations].
