# SafeStage: Evaluating Safety Before, During, and After Vision-Language-Conditioned Robot Manipulation

## What this paper does
97-task benchmark in RoboLab/Isaac (DROID Franka) organized by lifecycle stage: Initial-State Hazards (ISH: support/stacking/containment dependency must be resolved before moving the target), Execution-Time Safety (ETS: contacts, clearance, regions), Final-State Hazards (FSH: unstable placement, checked 5 s after completion). Each task has a valid goal, at least one direct-but-unsafe and one safe solution. Evaluates pi0.5, GR00T N1.7, DreamZero, Cosmos3 Nano Policy under default, generic-safety and scenario-specific prompts.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How often do benign instructions produce unsafe physical side effects?
Default prompt: SR 33.74% but safe SR 9.84%; 70.84% of successful rollouts are unsafe [full text, RQ1]. ISH violation rates 87.65–95.56% for every policy; 86.77% of successful ISH and 82.17% of successful FSH rollouts unsafe under the generic prompt [RQ1–RQ2].

### Goal: Does adding safety information in language change the action ordering?
Generic reminder: SSR 9.84%→10.11% only. Scenario-specific prompt (states the dependency and the precaution explicitly): SR falls to 22.02% and SSR to 5.91%; ETS-VR rises 67.92%→77.94%; "explicit safety instructions ... do not reliably produce the required action ordering or safe transport trajectory" [RQ3].

### Goal: Scope and limitations?
Direct VLA / world-model policies only (no LLM agent harness); simulation only; evaluators use privileged simulator state and task-specific thresholds [Limitations]. No mitigation method proposed.
