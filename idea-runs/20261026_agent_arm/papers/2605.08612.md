# ATAAT: Adaptive Threat-Aware Adversarial Tuning Framework against Backdoor Attacks on Vision-Language-Action Models

## What this paper does
Despite the title, a backdoor ATTACK framework (Findings ACL 2026) on OpenVLA's visual pathway. Identifies "gradient interference" between benign alignment and backdoor objectives; resolves it by implicit de-confliction (orthogonal perturbations in data poisoning) or explicit de-confliction (anchoring backdoor logic in dormant neurons under fine-tuning access). TASR >80% at 5% poisoning; semantic-concept triggers robust to instruction rewrites.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How do existing defenses fare against VLA backdoors?
Input pre-processing (JPEG, Gaussian noise) fails because triggers are physical/semantic; STRIP and Activation Clustering adapted to VLAs give high false-positive rates "when applied to continuous robotic trajectories" because benign task variance is misclassified; Circuit Breakers (representation-space intervention) is the only partial defense, lowering explicit-attack TASR to 45.2% [full text, Sec 4.5, App F.1]. Related work notes defenses (safety alignment, runtime monitoring, internal intervention) "target known unsafe behaviors" [Sec 2].

### Goal: Limitations?
OpenVLA only; proxy-victim alignment needed for implicit attack; dormant-neuron anchoring leaves an activation footprint Circuit Breakers can detect; static triggers only [Sec 6].
