# TreeHCA: Hindsight-Guided Exploration and Credit Assignment for Multi-Turn LLM Agents

## What this paper does
For multi-turn LLM agents with terminal verification, TreeHCA scores each realized turn by how it changes the policy's teacher-forced likelihood of the reference answer, revisits the parents of low-scoring turns to sample and execute alternative turns, continues one selected alternative, and computes turn-level advantages by a tree backup with inverse hindsight importance weights. Evaluated on search-based QA with Qwen2.5-3B, Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What signal drives turn-level exploration and credit, and what does it require?
The hindsight score is "the mean token log probability" of the reference answer y* appended after a history; "We use the reference answer y* during training to score these alternatives" [full text, Sec 3, Sec 4.2]. Every candidate turn is executed against the environment before scoring; one exploration step costs N turn executions and one full rollout [App B.3/D, Algorithm 1 notes]. So the method requires a reference answer whose likelihood can be teacher-forced, which tasks judged only by a success check do not provide.

### Goal: What does it cost and how large are the gains?
8 H200 GPUs: 34.4 h (4B) and 36.2 h (8B), 9-11% more than AT2PO; aggregate EM gains over the strongest baseline 0.90 and 1.63 points [Sec 6.4].
