# CaP-X: A Framework for Benchmarking and Improving Coding Agents for Robot Manipulation

## What this paper does
CaP-Gym lets coding agents control robots by writing programs over perception and control primitives; CaP-Bench evaluates 12 frontier and open models across tiers varying primitive abstraction (S1-S4), multi-turn feedback (M1-M4), and grounding. CaP-Agent0 adds visual differencing, an auto-synthesized skill library and parallel multi-model reasoning (training-free). CaP-RL applies GRPO to Qwen2.5-Coder-7B on three tasks and transfers to a real Franka.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How is the small coder trained with RL and what reward/state does it use?
GRPO with verifiable rewards on Cube Lift, Cube Stack, Spill Wipe for 50 iterations per task, single-turn program generation [full text, Sec 5]. Training uses the privileged state-based S1 APIs "to avoid the noisy reward signals present in tier S2, where compounding perception and control errors can cause otherwise correct programs to fail during execution, introducing credit assignment ambiguity" [Sec 5]. Evaluated on S2 (noisy perception): Cube Lift 25 -> 80%, Cube Stack 4 -> 44%, Spill Wipe 30 -> 93%; real Franka Cube Lift 84%, Cube Stack 76% (25 trials) [Table 4].

### Goal: What does the benchmark show about small/open versus frontier models and harness dependence?
"Closed-source models consistently outperform open-source alternatives" and no model matches human-expert single-turn programs (88.5%) [Sec 3.3 Takeaway 1]. Success rises monotonically with human-designed primitive abstraction, which "imposes a generality ceiling that masks failures in low-level reasoning" [Takeaway 2]. Raw RGB interleaving (M2) hurts versus text feedback; a VLM-based visual differencing module helps [Takeaway 3]. CaP-Agent0 uses 9 parallel queries per turn or 3 each to GPT-5.2, Opus 4.5, Gemini-3-Pro [Sec 4].

### Goal: What limitations are stated?
Programmatic control "remains brittle for contact-rich behaviors that require tight visual servoing and continuous feedback (e.g., insertion or pouring)"; proposed direction is hybrid CaP-VLA policies [App A]. CaP-RL covers only three short tasks and single-turn generation.
