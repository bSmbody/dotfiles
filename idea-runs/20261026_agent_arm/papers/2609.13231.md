# ShieldVLA: Feasibility-Aware Safety Alignment for Vision-Language-Action Models

## What this paper does
Safety-aligned RL fine-tuning of VLAs using a model-free Hamilton-Jacobi reachability safety critic learned from visual observations; the critic gates optimization (pure task reward inside the estimated safe set, recovery near unsafe states) instead of Lagrangian cost penalties. Critic targets come from rubric-based VLM safety scores (per-frame axes such as collision risk, hazardous proximity, manoeuvring room). Five benchmarks (Dubins-VL, TurtleBot-Nav, Safety-CHORES Nav/Fetch, Franka-Reach); -57% cumulative safety cost and +0.13 success vs SafeVLA.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What safety signal does it learn from and what kind of hazards can it represent?
Per-frame rubric scores from a VLM decomposed into geometric/proximity axes, converted into critic targets; the HJ value captures worst-case future constraint violation via a TD (safety Bellman) backup [full text, Sec 1, Sec 4.1–4.2]. Constraints in all environments are collision / hazardous-zone / proximity style [App D].

### Goal: What limitations does it report?
Safety claims are empirical (HJ contraction does not carry over to function approximation); the learned safe set is the one induced by the rubric, a second error source; sub-8B VLM scorers mode-collapse on tail frames; the Bellman update only learns the safe-set boundary on regions the rollout policy visits, so with rare unsafe events the gate threshold becomes hand-tuned; single published baseline; simulation only [App B].
