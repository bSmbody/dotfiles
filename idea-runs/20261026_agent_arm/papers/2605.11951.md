# From Reaction to Anticipation: Proactive Failure Recovery through Agentic Task Graph for Robotic Manipulation (AgentChord)

## What this paper does
GPT-5-backed agents turn an instruction and initial observation o_0 into a directed task graph (nodes = sub-goals with constraints c(z)<=0, edges = constrained motion transitions); a recovery-orchestration agent predicts likely failure modes per edge, writes each as a scalar monitor over geometric/gripper features and attaches forward-moving recovery branches that merge back into the graph; a compilation agent turns nodes and edges into constrained solver programs (sub-goal pose solver, receding-horizon path solver). Evaluated on 3 EmbodiChain sim tasks and 6 real bimanual tasks with injected disturbances (RSS 2026).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does the method handle an action that succeeds locally but makes a later stage fail, and is the dependency anticipated before commitment?
Failures are anticipated per edge before execution, but each sub-goal is solved locally. The sub-goal solver computes the node's target end-effector pose e^i by minimizing "a task-specific cost lambda^i_sub(e; x^i)" subject to that node's constraints C^i_sub, initialized "using the robot's current (or last attained) pose", with terms for collision, reachability/IK residual, pose regularization toward the current pose, consistency and bimanual spacing [full text, App C-E]. No term scores the feasibility of later nodes/edges from the chosen pose (e.g. a grasp chosen for later insertion). Anticipated failures are disturbance-type events on an edge (object shift, tilt, gripper width, attachment, visibility) [App C-G].
### Goal: What does it do when a failure is unanticipated or the recovery is infeasible, and what limitation does it report?
"they may still miss rare, compound, or non-hand-induced failures, especially in long-horizon tasks. If such a failure is not covered by the pre-compiled monitors or recovery branches, the system may not trigger an appropriate recovery behavior"; suggested fallback is re-invoking the MLLM pipeline at sub-goal boundaries or timeout [Sec V]. Failure analysis also reports wrong monitor signals chosen by the compilation agent [Sec IV-D]. The graph is synthesized once from o_0.
