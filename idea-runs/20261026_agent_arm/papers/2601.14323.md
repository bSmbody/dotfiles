# SilentDrift: Exploiting Action Chunking for Stealthy Backdoor Attacks on Vision-Language-Action Models

## What this paper does
Black-box data-poisoning backdoor (Findings ACL 2026) against chunked, delta-pose VLAs (pi0, VLA-Adapter on LIBERO). Exploits the intra-chunk visual open loop: per-step perturbations integrate over a K-step chunk (E_accum = sum of delta_i). Perturbations are Smootherstep-modulated (C2-continuous, zero velocity/acceleration at boundaries) and injected only at keyframes in the approach phase. 93.2% ASR at <2% poisoning with 95.3% clean success.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Which defenses does it evade and why?
Kinematic anomaly filters on velocity/acceleration/jerk (C2 continuity removes their signature), human replay inspection (near-miss failures look natural), and statistical/distribution auditing (keyframe sparsity) [full text, Sec 3.2–3.4, App A.5]. Temporal ensembling acts as a low-pass filter that preserves the low-frequency drift [App A.3].

### Goal: What defense does it discuss and what limits it?
Chunk size 1 reduces ASR only to 56.1% and sacrifices real-time control; proposed "Adaptive Chunk Truncation" triggered by a safety monitor near critical interaction zones is conceptual; hurdles: monitor needs near-miss vs crash data, adds latency, and threshold tuning trades false-positive truncations against missing smooth drifts [App A.4].
