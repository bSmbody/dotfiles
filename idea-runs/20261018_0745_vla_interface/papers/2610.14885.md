# Verify, Act, Distill: Turning Test-Time Compute into Test-Time Adaptation for Vision-Language-Action Models

## What this paper does
Samples N=8 action chunks from SmolVLA, scores them with a fixed RoboMonkey-style action verifier (trained by Bradley–Terry on RMSE to recorded successful actions), executes the softmax-weighted aggregate, and distils the aggregated chunks from successful adaptation episodes (K=10 per task) into a per-task rank-8 LoRA of the action expert. The distilled single-candidate policy retains ~74% of the test-time-compute gain on LIBERO (54.1 → 70.6).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What is amortized, into which model, and from what signal?
The verifier-selected action is amortized into the VLA's own action expert (LoRA), not into a controller or into the language command; the instruction is fixed [full text, Sec. 3.1–3.3]. The verifier scores closeness to recorded successful actions, not a referent [full text, Sec. 3.2, App. A.5]. Distillation needs successful episodes: on LIBERO-plus 66 of 140 tasks had no success in 10 episodes and could not be updated; LoRAs are per task and not transferred; 5 of 40 tasks degrade [full text, Sec. 5 Limitations].
