# Bring My Cup! Personalizing Vision-Language-Action Models with Visual Attentive Prompting (VAP)

## What this paper does
A training-free input-space adapter for frozen VLAs (π0/OpenVLA-class, Personalized-SIMPLER, Personalized-VLABench, real robot) to act on a specific personal instance among lookalikes. Grounding DINO proposes category boxes. Embedding matching against a few reference images votes for the instance, then SAM2 segments and tracks it. The mask is shown as a semi-transparent tint (α=0.5), and the instruction is rewritten by template ("my cup" → "the <tint-colour> cup"). No VLA weights change.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What non-language representation is used and how is it injected into the VLA?
A per-view instance mask M_t rendered as a semi-transparent colour tint on the RGB observation, paired with a language rewrite naming the tint colour [full text, Sec. 3.2–3.3]. The mask is re-anchored every frame by tracking.
### Goal: How much VLA training does reading the cue require, and what happens without it?
None: the VLA stays frozen. The frozen VLA reads the cue only through the coupled colour word, though. Mask-only (no rewrite) collapses to 7.3% SR (Google Robot) and 5.2% (WidowX), against 62.7% and 83.5% for mask+rewrite. Rewrite-only gives 15.8% and 18.8% [App. E.1, Table 14]. On the same frozen VLA, the point, circle, trajectory-arrow (TraceVLA-style) and numbered-marker (PIVOT/MOKA-style) prompts stay at 28–36% SR on WidowX, against 83.5% for the tint. Bounding boxes trail by 28–36 points [Sec. 5.7, App. E.4, Table 17]. Learned concept tokens transferred from the VLM drift in attention during closed-loop control [Sec. 3.3, App. B].
### Goal: What limitation or failure mode does it report for cue adherence or OOD generalization?
Single-view failures are dominated by control after correct grounding: 86.9% (Fractal) and 78.6% (Bridge) of failures are Case 3 [App. D.3, Table 13]. Multi-view per-camera tracking can lock onto different instances [Sec. 5.5]. The cue specifies only "which instance", not where to place or how to move: spatial and affordance-style prompts are explicitly said to fail on the frozen VLA [Sec. 5.7]. Tint colour and α are fixed by hand [App. E.4].
