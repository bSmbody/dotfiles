# DeicticVLA: Unifying Instruction Modes Based on Language and Deictic Gestures in a Single VLA

## What this paper does
Canonicalises language-only (LI), language+gesture (VLI) and gesture-only (VI) instructions into a text prompt plus SAM2 deictic masks (target, goal, reference) for a single π0 VLA. It compares, under matched backbone, demos and training steps, two RGB visual-prompt renderings (VP-BBox, VP-Fade) and two separate-channel mask fusions (MP-Early before the vision encoder, MP-Late after it, Alpha-CLIP-style additive patch embedding). Single-stage and two-stage full fine-tuning are both tested, in simulation zero-shot suites (Object-ZS, Spatial-ZS) and on three real tasks.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What non-language representation is used and how is it injected into the VLA?
Deictic masks, either rendered on the RGB image (red bounding boxes at 1% line width; or fading everything except the masks and robot at opacity 0.8) or patch-embedded with no bias and added to the image tokens before or after the vision encoder. A zero mask leaves the input unchanged [full text, Sec. III-C, Eq. 7–9].
### Goal: How much VLA training does reading the cue require, and what happens without it?
Full-parameter fine-tuning is required: first on LI data, then jointly on LI+VLI+VI [Sec. III-D]. With single-stage training at matched steps, the Object-ZS ΔSR of VP-Fade and VP-BBox "fall to nearly zero", and VP-BBox's Spatial-ZS gain "nearly disappears" [Sec. IV-B2]. Dropping LI data in stage two causes forgetting of language following: LI SR falls by 21.9–28.4 points [Sec. IV-B3].
### Goal: What limitation or failure mode does it report for cue adherence or OOD generalization?
In-distribution SR saturates at 94–96% for all methods. On Spatial-ZS, every method except VP-BBox stays in single-digit ΔSR, because the policies "more often select a visually identical distractor bowl despite valid masks" [Sec. IV-B1]. No prompting method is consistently best, so the paper treats the method as a per-VLA implementation choice [Sec. VI-B1]. The LI sampling ratio needs re-tuning on small real datasets [Sec. VI-A3].
