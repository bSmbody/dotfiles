# Cue the Flow: Steering Flow-Matching Policies for Open-World Delivery Manipulation

## What this paper does
Keeps a (demo-fine-tuned) π0.5 frozen and steers it with a 2D spatial cue: a Gaussian heatmap at the target centroid from Qwen3-VL-2B + SAM2.1. The cue is not drawn on the image. A ~0.02%-parameter adapter predicts a diagonal affine modulation (γ, β) of the flow-matching velocity at every integration step: ṽ = v⊙(1+γ)+β. It is trained with the flow loss, a spatial-shift contrastive loss, a residual (cue minus zero-cue) contrastive loss and a magnitude regulariser. Evaluated on real tabletop and mobile-base delivery, plus LIBERO-PRO Object.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What non-language representation is used and how is it injected into the VLA?
A target-centroid Gaussian heatmap h_t, encoded by a cue encoder. It acts on the action expert's output velocity, not on the VLA input. γ and β are vectors in R^{d_a}, broadcast across all H chunk positions [full text, Sec. 3.2, Eq. 1]. System 2 is invoked once per rollout [Sec. 3.4].
### Goal: How much VLA training does reading the cue require, and what happens without it?
The base π0.5 is first fine-tuned on 80 task demos. The adapter is trained on the same demos with ground-truth cues. LoRA on the action expert overfit and did not steer [Sec. 3.4, 4.1]. VP-VLA-style input prompting with a grounding loss on π0.5 reaches only 4.2% (tabletop) and 8.3% (mobile) in Setting III (unseen object + novel language), against 66.7% and 41.7% for the adapter [Sec. 4.2, Table 2]. The adapter alone gives 16.7% in Setting III. The contrastive losses lift it to 33.3% and then 41.7% [Sec. 4.3, Fig. 5].
### Goal: What limitation or failure mode does it report for cue adherence or OOD generalization?
"The steering strength is applied consistently throughout the rollout, which can over-modulate the policy output when precise actions are required … overshooting or unstable grasping" [App. D]. The regulariser trades off over-correction against under-steering: LIBERO-PRO SR is 11.5/15.5/12.5/10.5 for λ_reg ∈ {0, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1}, and the absolute LIBERO-PRO SR is only 15.5% against 10.5% for the base [Sec. 4.3]. The cue specifies a single target location only. Sensitivity to OOD affordance regions and positions is inherited from the base policy [Sec. 6].
