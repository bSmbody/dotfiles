# What Frozen VLAs Already Know About Success: A Probing Study of Value-Like Structure in Foundation Robot Policies

## What this paper does
Fits linear ridge probes on frozen OpenVLA, π0.5, DINOv2 and CLIP features to Monte-Carlo outcome-derived targets from mixed successful/failed LIBERO-Goal trajectories, validates them under same-task, same-timestep matched pairs (~92% ordering accuracy for π0.5), and uses the probe inside a simulator-backed best-of-16 selector over π0.5 action prefixes (push-plate 26.7 → 44.3%).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What non-rollout signal of success does it read from a frozen VLA, and what does obtaining it cost?
The probe target is v_t = γ^{T−1−t} for successful trajectories and 0 for failed ones, i.e. it is trained on complete-episode outcomes [full text, Sec. 3.1]. At test time the selector restores the simulator, rolls out each of K=16 five-action prefixes, and picks among those that succeed, else by normalised reward plus probe score; the authors call it "a diagnostic instrument ... rather than a runtime controller", about 2× random selection's cost and over an order of magnitude slower than greedy [full text, Sec. 3.4, Sec. 5].

### Goal: Does the probe score respond correctly when the language command is changed at a fixed observation (could it score candidate commands)?
No in the calibrated sense. Under goal swaps π0.5 VLM features move (L2 1.96, cosine 0.9985), but the probe score moves by +0.12, −0.08 or −0.02 depending on seed/layer, with the original goal preferred on only 53.7% of pairs; drawer tasks score the original goal lower, rack/container tasks higher [full text, Sec. 4.4, App. A "Per-task goal-swap direction"]. The authors conclude the probe "does not behave as a calibrated reward model that always prefers the matched goal" [full text, Sec. 4.4].

### Goal: What limits does it report?
Gains are task-dependent (drawer near unchanged) and need simulator access; a teacher-to-student amortised scorer gives +16 pp on one LIBERO task but is near chance elsewhere (RoboTwin pairwise 0.52) [full text, Sec. 5, App. A Table 11].
