# Paraphrase Consensus Is Not Self-Consistency: Instruction Reliance and Robustness in VLA Policies

## What this paper does
Tests whether test-time paraphrase ensembling (query a frozen VLA under K paraphrases and execute the medoid action chunk, "LCC") recovers what rewording costs OpenVLA / OpenVLA-OFT on LIBERO; adds a wrong-instruction probe measuring instruction reliance across OpenVLA, OFT, π0.5 and four suites; and shows LoRA post-training on paraphrased instructions removes the cost. Consensus never helps; one held paraphrase costs base OpenVLA −38 points.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism maps the controller/user instruction to phrasing the frozen VLA executes, and at what granularity (per episode / per sub-task / per step)?
Per replanning step, the medoid over K action chunks from K paraphrases, with distance scaled by per-dimension action statistics [full text, Sec. 3, Eq. 1]. Mapping back to the nearest training string "presupposes the closed instruction set that deployment removes" and fails for a quarter of Spatial's structural rewrites [full text, Sec. 5 "Mapping back to the training string"].

### Goal: What signal tells the method whether a phrasing is executable, and what does acquiring it cost?
Action-chunk disagreement u_t across paraphrases at a state. Aggregation fails because paraphrases are not exchangeable: each is a different conditional with its own bias b_k, so averaging reduces variance but not shared bias [full text, Sec. 3, Eq. 2; Sec. 4.3]. As a failure detector under length-controlled risk-set evaluation, paraphrase disagreement is the only source significant from the first replan (AUROC 0.598–0.620) on shifted LIBERO-Spatial, while visual TTA with 4× the magnitude is at chance [full text, App. E–F]. K=4 costs 2.98× one query [full text, Sec. 3 Cost].

### Goal: What limitation does it report regarding state/context-dependence or cases where no phrasing works?
Paraphrase cost appears only where the suite's success depends on the instruction (high on LIBERO-Goal, low on Spatial/Object/Long for OFT and π0.5) [full text, Sec. 4.4]. Disagreement is measured but not used to choose among phrasings; confidence-based selection (−28.0) also fails [full text, Abstract, Sec. 4.3]. Paraphrases are LLM-generated; human phrasings not collected [full text, Limitations].
