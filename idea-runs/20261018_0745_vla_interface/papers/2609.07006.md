# GIFT: Goal-Injected Fine-Tuning for Efficient Manipulation Policy Adaptation

## What this paper does
Generates one goal image per episode with a refined instruction-conditioned image editor (DiT with inversion and in-context edit prompts, ~4.2 s). It injects the goal into pretrained VLAs (OpenVLA, CogACT, π0.5) by adding ZeroConv(Proj_g(F_g − F_t)) to the observation embedding. Only the goal projector, the zero-conv and the action head are trained, for a single epoch. Evaluated on SIMPLER (Visual Matching / Variant Aggregation), LIBERO and a real robot.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What non-language representation is used and how is it injected into the VLA?
A generated goal image ĝ, encoded by the shared image encoder. Its feature difference to the current observation (the "semantic contrast" F_Δ = F_g − F_t) is added through a zero-initialised convolution [full text, Sec. 3.2, Eq. 4–5]. The goal is generated once from o_0 and reused throughout execution [Sec. 3.1, Fig. 1].
### Goal: How much VLA training does reading the cue require, and what happens without it?
Light fine-tuning (one epoch, most parameters frozen). Cross-attention injection "directly disrupts the pretrained policy … near-zero success rates", and FiLM underperforms ZeroConv [Sec. 4.3]. Removing semantic contrast lowers SIMPLER-VM from 67.7 to 57.5 [Sec. 4.3]. Removing language while keeping the goal image gives 0.0% on Grasp and Stack, with unstable fine-tuning [App. C.1, Table 6].
### Goal: What limitation or failure mode does it report for cue adherence or OOD generalization?
"GIFT enhances rather than creates capabilities": OpenVLA stays at 0% on PutInDrawer after goal injection [Sec. 4.2]. Performance tracks goal quality closely: degraded goals drop LIBERO-Long to 24.0 against 56.0 [Sec. 4.3, Table 4]. Stated limitations are that frozen parameters cannot extend the model beyond tasks already supported, and the two-stage generation adds overhead and distribution mismatch [Limitations].
