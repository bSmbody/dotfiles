# Correcting WHERE, Preserving HOW: Compositional Generalization for Vision-Language-Action Models via Referential Guidance

## What this paper does
ReGuide is a training-free wrapper around a frozen VLA (π0.5, GR00T N1.7, OpenVLA-OFT) for compositional shifts on LIBERO. Given referent identity and poses from a grounding module (oracle simulator state in the main study; GroundingDINO + depth in an appendix), it integrates the unexecuted action chunk into a predicted end-effector endpoint. It tests which object's approach box that endpoint commits to and, on a mismatch with the instructed referent, transports the end-effector with scripted, demo-bounded motion into a demonstration-derived pre-contact pose, then hands back to the frozen policy. Compositional success rises by 55.5–56.8 points across backbones in simulation and from 15% to 90% on an xArm6.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does ReGuide read the frozen VLA's predicted action chunk against an externally given referent, and what does it do with that signal?
Yes. The predicted endpoint q̂_t = q_t + Σ over the unexecuted chunk of scaled translation commands (Eq. 2) is tested against each candidate referent's approach box with a demo-calibrated commitment radius (Eq. 3); the nearest qualifying box is "the policy's referent", and a mismatch with the instructed referent triggers guidance, "an anticipatory mismatch signal without an external scorer" [full text, Sec. 3.2]. The signal is used only as a trigger for geometric transport (Eq. 6) toward a demo-median grasp pose; on arrival the pending chunk is discarded and the frozen policy resumes [full text, Sec. 3.3]. The language instruction is never changed: "The instruction text is used only by the policy and by the retrieval of habitual referents" [full text, App. B.3]. No speaker or controller is learned.

### Goal: What does ReGuide require, and what are its stated limits?
It requires the training demonstrations with end-effector and object poses (per-object-type median grasp offsets, rotation medoids, step quantiles, commitment radii), "which not every released policy provides", plus an external grounding module [full text, App. E, Sec. 3.1]. Objects needing a grasp absent from the demonstrations are not covered; "relational or ambiguous instructions are not evaluated"; remaining failures concentrate after hand-back (moka pot: 48/50 failures for GR00T N1.7 and OpenVLA-OFT vs 1/50 for π0.5) [full text, App. E, Sec. 4.2]. Perception-based grounding costs 7.0 points against oracle on π0.5 (87.5% compositional success) [full text, Sec. 4.2, App. C.6].

### Goal: How many public VLA backbones are evaluated on the same LIBERO setting?
Five VLA backbones are evaluated on the same cells, and three (π0.5, GR00T N1.7, OpenVLA-OFT) are wrapped with ReGuide using the same demonstration statistics; only chunk length and executed actions differ (10/5, 16/8, 8/8) [full text, Sec. 4.1, App. B.3].
