# PRPO: Perception-Reinforced Policy Optimization via Token-Level Dynamic Advantage Reshaping

## What this paper does
PRPO reshapes the GRPO/DAPO sequence advantage token by token using "Robust Visual Dependency": a token's KL shift under a strong image corruption (black mask), discounted by its KL shift under an imperceptible pixel jitter, so that tokens that are image-dependent but numerically brittle are filtered out. The filtered score passes through a bounded S-shaped (generalized logistic) curve and multiplies the sequence advantage. It reports that most MathVerse failures of Qwen2.5-VL-7B are perception errors inside long trajectories.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What signal does it use to assign token-level credit for visual reasoning, and how is it combined with the outcome reward?
Visual Dependency S_t = KL(pi(.|I) || pi(.|P_str(I))) with a strong black-mask perturbation; Visual Inconsistency S'_t = KL(pi(.|I) || pi(.|P_wk(I))) with Gaussian pixel jitter [full text, Section 4.1, Def 4.1-4.2 / Eq 4-5]. RVD_t = S_t * ReLU(1 - S'_t/(S_t+eta)) (Eq 6). PAR: A_hat_t = A_hat^i * T(RVD_t) with T a logistic curve bounded in [m, M], m > 0 (Section 4.2, Eq 7-8). The sign of the token update therefore always equals the sign of the trajectory-level advantage.

### Goal: Does the credit signal check whether a token's visual content is correct, or only how much it depends on the image?
Only dependence and robustness to noise. The paper itself diagnoses that failures come from "ground on incorrect visual regions, misread local evidence, or extract erroneous numerical cues at intermediate perception steps" [full text, Section 1, Fig 1], yet RVD contains no comparison of the emitted value with the image: a misread number that is strongly image-dependent and stable under jitter scores as a "pivotal perceptual token" and is amplified with whatever sign the final answer earned. Finding 4.2 shows high-dependency tokens split into brittle and stable clusters, but both clusters are defined by sensitivity, not correctness [Section 4.1].

### Goal: What limitations does it report?
Additional forward passes under perturbed inputs raise training cost [full text, Appendix E].
