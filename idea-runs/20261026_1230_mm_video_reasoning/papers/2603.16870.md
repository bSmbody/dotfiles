# Demystifying Video Reasoning

## What this paper does
Mechanistic study of reasoning in video diffusion models (LTX2.3, Wan2.1, Wan2.2 and VBVR-LoRA variants). Decoding x0-hat at each step, it argues reasoning happens along denoising steps ("Chain-of-Steps") rather than across frames: early steps hold multiple candidate solutions (multi-path / superposition) that are pruned as denoising proceeds. Proposes a training-free ensemble of latent trajectories across seeds.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Along which axis does reasoning unfold in a video diffusion model, frames or denoising steps?
72% of 200 inspected examples show multi-path or superposition exploration across denoising steps [full text, Section 3.1.3]. Injecting noise into all frames at one step collapses VBVR score from 0.685 to below 0.3, while noising one frame across all steps barely hurts, because bidirectional attention repairs the frame from neighbors [full text, Section 3.2, Fig 3]. Self-corrections "take place globally across all frames simultaneously within a single diffusion step" [full text, Section 4.2]. Sensitivity peaks around steps 20-30 [full text, Section 3.2].
### Goal: What does this imply for method design?
Reasoning state is distributed over the whole clip and the step axis; there is no explicit per-frame reasoning step. The paper's proof-of-concept intervention is a training-free seed ensemble [full text, Section 6]; it does not propose training changes.
