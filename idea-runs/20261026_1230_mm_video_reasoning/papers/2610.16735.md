# VAttnPO: Visual Attention-Guided Policy Optimization

## What this paper does
VAttnPO adds a zero-sum token-level correction to the GRPO sequence advantage. The frozen old policy teacher-forces the correct answer to obtain a reference distribution of attention over visual patches; each rollout token's patch-attention distribution is compared to it by JSD, centered within the trajectory, modulated by a smooth relative-position weight, and balanced so positive and negative corrections cancel. The final token advantage is A_hat_i + alpha * d_it. Main backbone Qwen3-VL-8B.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What signal does it use to assign token-level credit for visual reasoning, and how is it combined with the outcome reward?
P_gt: attention from correct-answer tokens (the answer string <answer>X</answer>, no reasoning) to visual patches at layer 20, pooled over heads and normalized [full text, Section 4.2 / Eq 2; Appendix C.1]. s_it = 1 - JSD(Q_it || P_gt)/ln 2 (Eq 3), centered within trajectory (Eq 4), position-weighted (Eq 5), balanced to zero sum (Eq 6-7); A_tilde_it = A_hat_i + alpha d_it (Eq 8). Because the correction is additive and zero-sum, individual tokens can receive credit of opposite sign to the trajectory, but only according to where they attend.

### Goal: Does the credit signal check whether a token's visual content is correct, or only where it looks?
Only where it looks. The paper states it treats attention "as a proxy ... rather than as a causal attribution or certificate of correctness" [full text, Section 1]. The reference is the policy's own attention when conditioned on the answer letter, so a token attending to the right region while misreading its value receives positive correction; the pilot study shows correct responses are closer to P_gt on average, a population-level separation [Section 3, Fig 1].
