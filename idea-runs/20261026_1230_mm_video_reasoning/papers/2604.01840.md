# Not All Tokens See Equally: Perception-Grounded Policy Optimization for Large Vision-Language Models

## What this paper does
PGPO is a token-level credit-assignment variant of GRPO for LVLMs. It scores each generated token by its "visual dependency", the KL divergence between the policy's next-token distribution with and without the image (image tokens masked out in the attention mask), log-compresses and min-max normalizes this per sequence, maps it through a threshold-gated piecewise weight, renormalizes so the weights sum to the sequence length, and multiplies the sequence-level GRPO advantage by that weight. Trained on Qwen2.5-VL 3B/7B, evaluated on seven multimodal reasoning benchmarks.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What signal does it use to assign token-level credit for visual reasoning, and how is it combined with the outcome reward?
Signal: S(s_t, I) = KL(pi(.|s_t, I) || pi(.|s_t)), with the no-image branch obtained by masking visual tokens in attention [full text, Section 3.1 / Eq 1; Appendix A.1], estimated with the single-sample (r-1)-log r estimator. It is transformed by log(1+S), per-sequence min-max (Eq 4-5), a threshold-gated weight omega(I_t) that shrinks tokens below tau and boosts those above (Eq 6), and a sum-preserving normalization (Eq 7). Final advantage is A_tilde_{i,t} = A_i * omega_tilde_t (Eq 8): the sign of every token's update is the sign of the sequence-level outcome advantage; the weight only rescales its magnitude.

### Goal: Does the credit signal check whether a token's visual content is correct, or only how much it depends on the image?
Only dependence. The score measures how much the image shifts the predictive distribution, not whether the emitted value matches the image. The paper's supporting evidence is distributional: GPT-5-annotated "visual anchor" tokens have higher average S than non-anchors, and on CHAIR captions truthful tokens have higher average S than hallucinated ones [full text, Section 3.2, Fig 2(c,d)]; this is a mean difference, not a per-token correctness test. Because Eq 8 multiplies the outcome advantage, a high-dependency token in a correct-answer trajectory is amplified positively and a high-dependency token in a wrong-answer trajectory is amplified negatively, regardless of whether that token's visual reading is correct.

### Goal: What limitations does it report?
Hyperparameters tau and beta are tuned to the training distribution and may need re-tuning; validated only up to 7B [full text, Limitations]. No limitation is stated about the outcome sign being applied to visually correct tokens in failed trajectories.
