# Counterfactual Tests for Measuring Chain-of-Thought Faithfulness in Visual Language Models

## What this paper does
Adapts the Counterfactual Test (CT) and Correlational Counterfactual Test (CCT) to images (vCT, vCCT; EMNLP 2026 Findings). A diffusion pipeline removes one grounded object per image (Counter-A-OKVQA, Counter-SNLI-VE), with quality filtering and no-op reconstruction controls. For eight open VLMs it measures whether the explanation mentions the removed object in proportion to the induced shift in answer probabilities, comparing pre-answer CoT with predict-then-explain (PE).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism does it use to make the reasoning trace visually grounded/faithful (or measure it)?
Measurement only. vCCT correlates "edited concept mentioned" with the total-variation-style shift of answer probabilities between original and edited image; vCT checks mention when the top prediction flips [full text, Sec 3.5-3.6].
### Goal: Does its signal test whether the final answer causally depends on the trace's visual claims, or only claim truth / image sensitivity / attention?
It tests explanation-prediction alignment under image interventions. Finding: pre-answer CoTs track behaviorally influential evidence poorly (mean vCCT 0.200 on A-OKVQA and 0.129 on SNLI-VE) and worse than PE (0.303 / 0.264), for every model, robust to accuracy-matched and directional controls [Sec 4.1]. Binary vCT saturates (~0.9-0.95) because flips are rare [Sec 4.2].
### Goal: What counterfactual/intervention operator does it use, and what limitation does it report?
Object removal by diffusion inpainting; a preliminary color-change extension on 122 pairs. Limitations: correct attribute attribution, relations, counting, actions and compositional edits are left to future work; edits may carry artifacts; mention detection is model-based (92.2% human agreement) [Limitations, Sec 4.1].
