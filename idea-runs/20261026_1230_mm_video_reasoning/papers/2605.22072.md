# Faithful-MR1: Faithful Multimodal Reasoning via Anchoring and Reinforcing Visual Attention

## What this paper does
Separates two failure modes of multimodal RLVR: perception failure and the perception-reasoning disconnect (PRD), where a correctly perceived fact is later dropped or contradicted in the derivation. Anchoring stage: a <Focus> token's attention is supervised against Gemini-annotated question-relevant boxes before reasoning. Reinforcing stage: tokens whose next-token distribution shifts (KL) when the annotated regions are masked are marked vision-dependent, and answer-correct trajectories whose attention to image tokens is concentrated at those positions (ratio > 2) get a binary auxiliary reward, with split GRPO advantages. Qwen2.5-VL 3B/7B, 19.2K boxed examples.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism does it use to make the reasoning trace visually grounded/faithful?
D_t = KL(p_t || p~_t) with p~ under region-masked image [full text, Sec 3.3 / Eq 7]; g = a^vd / (a^nonvd + eps), r_attn = 1[g > 2], gated on answer correctness [Eq 8]; A = A^acc + lambda A^attn [Eq 9-10].
### Goal: Does its signal test whether the final answer causally depends on the trace's visual claims, or only claim truth / image sensitivity / attention?
It targets "faithful use" but operationalizes it as image sensitivity of trace tokens plus attention ratios; it does not intervene on the trace and check whether the answer changes. Its own behavioral analysis on MathVerse Vision-Only (Gemini-judged gold facts) gives Faithful Use = Pr(fact used as load-bearing premise | verbalized correctly) of 66.7% for Faithful-MR1 vs 62.9% VPPO and 49.4% Vision-SR1 [Sec 4.4, Table 3], so a third of correctly verbalized facts remain unused even after training on this signal.
### Goal: What counterfactual/intervention operator does it use, and what limitation does it report?
Masking the annotated evidence regions (removal). Limitations: needs region annotations and one extra masked forward pass per rollout [Sec 5].
