# Faithful GRPO: Improving Visual Spatial Reasoning in Multimodal Language Models via Constrained Policy Optimization

## What this paper does
FGRPO shows that GRPO with task reward raises spatial-reasoning accuracy while CoT quality decays (inconsistency 7.4% -> 26.1% over training; semantic grounding 72.7%). It defines three reasoning-quality signals: a text-only LLM consistency judge (does the CoT entail the answer), a sentence-level VLM-judge semantic grounding score, and a CIoU spatial grounding score for generated boxes, and enforces them as constraints via Lagrangian dual ascent with decoupled per-signal group normalization (GDPO-style). Seven spatial benchmarks; FGRPO reaches 67.2% accuracy with 1.7% inconsistency and 86.0% grounding.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism does it use to make the reasoning trace visually grounded/faithful?
R_C: binary text-only LLM judge of whether the <answer> follows from the <think> text, "ignoring visual correctness" [full text, Sec 3.2]. R_S: trace split into sentences, trivial ones filtered by pattern matching, each visual sentence scored CORRECT/INCORRECT by a VLM judge given image, question and prior context; R_S is the mean over scored sentences [Sec 3.2, App A.1]. R_G: Hungarian-matched CIoU of <bbox> outputs to GT boxes [Eq 1]. Constrained objective with dual ascent on lambda [Eq 2-6]. R_C and R_S are masked to answer-correct rollouts to prevent hacking.
### Goal: Does its signal test whether the final answer causally depends on the trace's visual claims, or only claim truth / image sensitivity / attention?
Claim truth (R_S, via external VLM judge) and textual entailment (R_C) only. R_C explicitly ignores the image; a trace that entails the answer textually is scored consistent even if the answer was not computed from it. R_S is a precision-style mean over sentences that make visual claims; it does not require the decisive fact to be present nor test answer dependence on it [Sec 3.2]. No intervention on image or trace.
### Goal: What counterfactual/intervention operator does it use, and what limitation does it report?
None. It reports that naive additive/multiplicative shaping trades off accuracy (Table 2) and that within-group normalization cancels constant constraint signals, motivating decoupled advantages [Sec 3.3, 4.5]. Judge reliability validated against humans in App B.3.
