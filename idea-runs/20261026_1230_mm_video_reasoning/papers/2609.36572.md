# Visual Sensitivity Is Not Claim Retractability: Persistence-Aware Credit Assignment for Multimodal Reinforcement Learning

## What this paper does
Shows that 27.81% of answer-correct Qwen2.5-VL-7B trajectories contain at least one unsupported direct visual claim (count, OCR value, chart reading, geometric property), which inherits positive outcome credit. A fixed-rollout counterfactual diagnostic teacher-forces the same response under the original and a degraded image and separates Evidence-Function Sensitivity (unsigned KL at claim tokens) from claim persistence (drift-corrected log-likelihood change). Under DAPO and VPPO sensitivity rises while unsupported claims become more persistent (Sensitivity-Persistence Decoupling). PACG attenuates positive credit of unusually persistent claims; +1.8 (DAPO) and +1.1 (VPPO) on a nine-benchmark average.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism does it use to make the reasoning trace visually grounded/faithful?
A frozen Qwen3.6-35B-A3B router marks direct-visual-claim token spans [full text, Sec 4.1]. Persistence Delta~(s) = mean claim-token log-prob change under I^cf minus the mean change of residual (non-visual) tokens [Eq 4-5]. Gate g_s = max(g_min, exp(-alpha [Delta~ + m]_+)) multiplies only positive token advantages on routed claims; stop-gradient through the gate [Eq 6-7, Sec 4.4].
### Goal: Does its signal test whether the final answer causally depends on the trace's visual claims, or only claim truth / image sensitivity / attention?
It tests the image -> claim link (does the claim lose support when the image is degraded), not the claim -> answer link. Answer tokens and non-routed reasoning tokens keep the parent credit; a true claim that the answer ignores is never penalized [Eq 7, "positive-only gating"]. It explicitly argues that unsigned sensitivity (EFS) cannot tell retraction from prior-driven reinforcement [Sec 4.2].
### Goal: What counterfactual/intervention operator does it use, and what limitation does it report?
Image-level degradations: random 14x14 cell blackening (p=0.5), "coarse ink" dark-stroke blackening, and a padded foreground-box blackout; the paper states these are "image-level controls, not verified claim-localized evidence removals" [App, Fig 7]. A residual-token correction is needed because corruption shifts the whole response likelihood [Eq 5]. Limitation: "image corruption measures a claim's response to the chosen intervention, rather than certifying factual correctness or guaranteeing selective removal of that claim's evidence" [App J]. PACG fixes SPD only partly for DAPO [Sec 5.4 Q7].
