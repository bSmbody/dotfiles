# Deep But Reliable: Advancing Multi-turn Reasoning for Thinking with Images (DRIM)

## What this paper does
DRIM builds a high-difficulty, verifiable high-resolution VQA set that needs several zoom-in calls, cold-starts a VLM by SFT on synthesized multi-turn tool trajectories, and then runs agentic RL (DeepEyes-style rollouts) with "redundancy-penalized policy optimization": when an answer is wrong and more than one zoom was made, the reward is penalized by the pairwise IoU overlap between zoom boxes, pushing the policy toward multi-scale exploration rather than repeated near-identical crops. Evaluated on V*, HR-Bench and VisualProbe.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What does DRIM identify as the failure of outcome-reward RL for multi-turn visual operations, and what reward does it add?
It argues DeepEyes-style RL "only encourages rollout trajectories that yield correct answers without any mechanism to evaluate the reasoning process itself", so "the model tends to guess an answer with fewer reasoning turns" and fails to self-reflect [full text, Section 1]. Failure cases show "oscillatory micro-adjustments around the same target" because the model "heavily relies on grounding-based initialization" [Section 3.3, Redundancy-Penalized PO]. Added term: Γ_rdn(τ) = −λ/C(T,2) Σ_{t<t'} max(0, IoU(b_t,b_t') − ε), applied only when R_acc=0 and T>1 [same section]. The signal is purely geometric (box overlap among the policy's own boxes); it never measures what the crops contained or whether a crop changed the answer.

### Goal: What does it report as remaining limitations of the visual-operation loop?
Future work lists overthinking (tools invoked "excessively even when it has already obtained sufficient information"), single-crop-tool restriction, and visual hallucination: "even after performing multi-scale exploration and correctly localizing the target region, the model may still produce incorrect answers" [Section 9]. Ablation: without cold-start SFT the RL-only model makes few turns and drops on VisualProbe/V* [Section 4.3].
