# Video Models Reason Early: Exploiting Plan Commitment for Maze Solving

## What this paper does
Analyzes Wan2.2-14B and HunyuanVideo-1.5 on 2D mazes (Frozen Lake, VR-Bench). Finds early plan commitment (route decided within the first few denoising steps) and a sharp horizon limit (~12 path steps). Proposes EPBS (screen many seeds from early x0-hat predictions with a lightweight verifier) and ChEaP (chain several generations), raising long-maze accuracy from 7% to 67%.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: When during denoising is the plan decided, and can refinement change it?
On 4x4 mazes the step-5 trajectory already has 93% convergence to the final one (of T=40), near-perfect by step 10 [full text, Section 4.3]. Re-noising/refinement branches from the same seed give at most 25% trajectory diversity vs 68% across seeds: the route is largely encoded in the initial noise [full text, Section 4.4].
### Goal: What limitation does it report on long-horizon reasoning and rule adherence?
Path length, not obstacle density, predicts difficulty, with a failure threshold at 12 steps; even an oracle seed picker fails because "no single generation contains a complete solution" [full text, Section 6]. Chaining helps but on 14+ step mazes only 2.4% -> 14.6% due to compounding errors across chains [full text, Section 6.3]. When the needed trajectory exceeds the generation horizon, Wan2.2 "cheats": the goal teleports next to the agent or a second agent spawns near the goal; "the model prioritizes goal completion over structural fidelity" [full text, Section 7.1, Fig 9]. HunyuanVideo (8-step distilled) has 77.5% of size-4 failures as constraint violations vs 32.5% for Wan [full text, Section 7.1]. Open question stated: whether training can produce models that plan more reliably or over longer horizons [full text, Section 8].
