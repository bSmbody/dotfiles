# Video models are zero-shot learners and reasoners

## What this paper does
Prompts Veo 2/Veo 3 (image as first frame + text instruction, 8 s 720p) on 62 qualitative and 7 quantitative tasks across perception, modeling, manipulation and reasoning, arguing video models are becoming general vision foundation models and introducing the "chain-of-frames" framing.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What reasoning ability does a frozen video generator show and what fails?
Maze solving: Veo 3 pass@10 78% on 5x5 vs Veo 2 14%, which "often produces illegal moves" [full text, Section 4.5]. Below-chance on reflect and rotate visual analogies, "indicating an erroneous, systematic bias" [full text, Section 4.7]. Prompt choice swings symmetry pass@1 by 40-64 points [full text, Section 4.6]. Method is prompting only; API includes an LLM prompt rewriter, so some solutions may come from the LLM [full text, Section 2]. Authors expect inference-time scaling and post-training with automatic verifiers to help [full text, Section 5].
