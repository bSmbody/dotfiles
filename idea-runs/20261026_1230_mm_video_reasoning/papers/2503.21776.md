# Video-R1: Reinforcing Video Reasoning in MLLMs

## What this paper does
Video-R1 applies R1-style RL to video MLLMs (Qwen2.5-VL-7B, 16 frames during training) with mixed image and video data. Its Temporal GRPO (T-GRPO) samples a group of responses on temporally ordered frames and another on shuffled frames; if the ordered group's accuracy is at least the shuffled group's, a fixed bonus alpha = 0.3 is added to every correct ordered response. It reports more responses judged as temporal reasoning (75.0% vs 60.2%).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does it reward use of temporal evidence, and at what granularity?
r_t = alpha if p >= p_tilde else 0, where p and p_tilde are the proportions of correct answers in the ordered and shuffled groups [full text, Section 3.2 / Eq 1]; R_i = r_i + r_t only for correct responses (Eq 2), then standard group normalization (Eq 3). The signal is question-level: every correct response to the question gets the same bonus, whether or not its own trace used the right moment, and nothing checks which frames a trace refers to.

### Goal: What limitations does it report?
Training with 16 frames limits long-range temporal dependencies; T-GRPO adds overhead from the extra shuffled-group rollouts [full text, Appendix E].
