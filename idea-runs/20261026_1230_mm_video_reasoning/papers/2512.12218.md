# Journey Before Destination: On the importance of Visual Faithfulness in Slow Thinking

## What this paper does
Defines visual faithfulness of reasoning chains as a separate axis from answer accuracy. A reference-free VLM-judge pipeline segments the chain into Perception vs Reasoning steps and labels each perception step Faithful/Unfaithful; validated against two human annotation sets. A training-free self-reflection method detects the first unfaithful perception step and asks the model to regenerate it (retry limit), then continues generation.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism does it use to make the reasoning trace visually grounded/faithful?
Detection + localized regeneration at inference [full text, Sec 4, Algorithm 2]: detector D returns the first unfaithful step; the VLM is re-prompted with the prefix plus a regeneration instruction until the step is faithful or K retries; 90% of successful corrections within three retries.
### Goal: Does its signal test whether the final answer causally depends on the trace's visual claims, or only claim truth / image sensitivity / attention?
Only claim truth: an external judge (best Claude 4 Sonnet) checks perception steps against the image after first describing the image itself [Sec 3.2 Method]. Judge-human correlation for faithfulness labels is ICC 0.66-0.69 for the best judges, vs 0.93 for perception-step typing [Table 1-2], so the faithfulness signal is only moderately reliable. Nothing tests whether the answer depends on the corrected step.
### Goal: What counterfactual/intervention operator does it use, and what limitation does it report?
None on the image. Limitations: extra forward passes, dependence on a strong external VLM detector, and scope limited to perception-heavy tasks [Limitations].
