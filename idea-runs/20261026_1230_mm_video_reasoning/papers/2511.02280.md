# SAIL-RL: Guiding MLLMs in When and How to Think via Dual-Reward RL Tuning

## What this paper does
SAIL-RL post-trains SAIL-VL2 (4B/8B) with a cascading product reward: a Judging Reward for a binary think/no-think decision checked against ground-truth complexity labels, a Thinking Reward from an LLM judge scoring logical coherence, factual grounding (against image, query, world knowledge) and answer consistency, and answer accuracy, after a LongCoT SFT stage in a Judge-Think-Answer format. It reports reduced hallucination and adaptive thinking rates (e.g. 7.5% thinking on OCRBench).

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What signal decides how much reasoning compute an instance gets, and can that signal tell perception-limited from reasoning-limited instances?
The model outputs a think/no-think judgment that is rewarded when it matches "ground-truth complexity labels" [full text, Section 3.2]; the SFT judgments come from a strong teacher [Section 3.4]. The action space is binary: perception-heavy questions are routed to direct answering (OCRBench thinking rate 7.5%, with +2.6 on OCRBench and +3.2 on HallusionBench versus always-think) [Section 4.4]. There is no third option of spending compute on re-inspecting visual evidence; a hard perception question either gets text reasoning or no extra compute.

### Goal: How is grounding of the reasoning rewarded?
An LLM judge checks each step against the image and query and assigns binary d2 (any contradiction gives 0) [Section 3.1, Factual Grounding Reward]; the reward is trajectory-level and multiplied with judge and answer terms [Section 3.3 / Eq 1].
