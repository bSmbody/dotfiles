# VideoTemp-o3: Harmonizing Temporal Grounding and Video Understanding in Agentic Thinking-with-Videos

## What this paper does
VideoTemp-o3 is a single agentic MLLM that unifies VideoQA and temporal grounding as a localize-crop-answer loop: skim at a low rate, predict an interval, receive a densely resampled crop, optionally refine the interval over several turns, then answer. Training: cold-start SFT with a unified masking strategy (only the last two turns supervised) on curated multi-turn grounded-QA trajectories, then GRPO with accuracy, format and a penalty-aware IoU reward. It also releases a long-video grounded-QA dataset and VideoTemp-Bench.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism does it use to locate or re-inspect temporal evidence in long videos?
Skim V at sampling rate s0; at each turn emit reasoning T plus either an interval P or answer A; Crop(V,P,s_d) with s_d > s0 is appended to the context; reflection turns refine an inaccurate first interval [full text, Sec 4, Eq 1; Sec 4.1].
### Goal: How does its RL reward assign credit to the evidence the model uses (outcome-only, IoU vs annotation, other)?
R = R_accuracy (exact match) + R_format + R_penalty-IoU, with IoU against one ground-truth interval [t_s,t_e] and a penalty λ when IoU < σ [full text, Sec 4.2, Eq 3–6]. Plain IoU was hacked: tool-call ratio rose sharply while mIoU degraded ("localize blindly") [Sec 5.3, Fig 5]. Removing IoU rewards costs only ~1.6% on VideoMMMU [Sec 5.3]. Credit is tied to annotated single intervals, not to whether the answer relied on the crop.
### Goal: What limitation or failure mode does it report on long-video temporal reasoning?
"For very long videos, the initial skim is still sparse ... so key evidence may be missed before refinement", worse for events lasting a few frames; the pipeline depends on teacher models and verified temporal annotations; the masking heuristic assumes later turns are more reliable [full text, Sec 7]. Ground-truth-segment input helps baseline models more as videos get longer [App. C].
