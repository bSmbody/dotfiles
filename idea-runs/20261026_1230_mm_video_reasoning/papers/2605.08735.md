# CollabVR: Collaborative Video Reasoning with Vision-Language and Video Generation Models

## What this paper does
Training-free closed loop: a VLM plans the next action, the video generator renders a short clip from the last accepted frame, the VLM verifies the clip and folds its diagnosis into the next prompt; accepted clips are concatenated. Improves open and closed VGMs on Gen-ViRe and VBVR-Bench over Pass@k at matched compute.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does it handle long-horizon drift and mid-clip errors?
Names two failure modes, "long-horizon drift on multi-step tasks and mid-clip simulation errors that compound", attributing both to "the absence of explicit reasoning built upon the VGM's short-horizon visual prior" [full text, Abstract]. Each step conditions only on the last accepted frame f [full text, Section 3.1, Algorithm 1]. Verifier judges single clips for wrong direction / wrong target / scene collapse [full text, Section 3.3]. The VGM is never trained; all reasoning is delegated to the VLM [full text, Section 3].
