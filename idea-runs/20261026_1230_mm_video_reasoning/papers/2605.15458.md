# Video Models Can Reason with Verifiable Rewards

## What this paper does
VideoRLVR post-trains Wan2.2-TI2V-5B (81 frames, 480x832) for rule-verifiable visual puzzles (Maze, FlowFree, Sokoban). Pipeline: SFT on planner-rendered solution videos, then SDE-GRPO (Flow-GRPO style) with dense decomposed rule rewards, a KL penalty to the SFT reference, and "Early-Step Focus" that restricts noise injection and gradients to the first 10 of 20 denoising steps (~40% faster). RL beats continued SFT and proprietary video models, but absolute success on FlowFree (7.9%) and Sokoban (6.1%) stays very low.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism does it use to make a video generator reason, and how is credit assigned?
The MDP is "defined over denoising steps rather than reasoning steps, where the reward is computed after the final video is decoded" [full text, Section 3]. One group-normalized advantage A_i per video is applied to every denoising step k and, via a log-ratio averaged over all D latent elements, to every frame and pixel alike [full text, Section 4.1, Eq 2-3]. Rewards are dense in the sense of partial-credit components (Maze R_conn x R_wall; FlowFree 4 weighted terms; Sokoban 0.5 final-state + 0.5 fraction of valid transitions) but are collapsed to one scalar per video [full text, Section 4.3]. Early-Step Focus shows the first half of the denoising steps carries the reward-relevant structure [full text, Section 4.2, Table 4].
### Goal: What limitation does it report on multi-step / long-horizon reasoning?
Sparse binary reward fails on FlowFree and Sokoban (FlowFree stays ~2.5% over 1000 steps) because success is rarely sampled in a group: "a critical cold-start problem in video RL" [full text, Section 6.2, Table 5]. Even with dense rewards, Table 1 reports SR 72.2 (Maze), 7.9 (FlowFree), 6.1 (Sokoban); SFT already gives 42-44 F1 on FlowFree, so most rollouts are partially right but fail somewhere [full text, Section 5.2, Table 1]. Removing KL yields reward hacking with visually implausible patterns that satisfy part of the verifier [full text, Section 6.1]. Group size must be 16 for stable advantages, with linear rollout cost [full text, Section 6.1]. Training used 8 B200 GPUs [full text, Section 5.1].
