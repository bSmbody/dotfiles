# DeepEyes: Incentivizing "Thinking with Images" via Reinforcement Learning

## What this paper does
DeepEyes trains Qwen2.5-VL-7B (and 32B) end to end with GRPO, without cold-start SFT, to interleave textual reasoning with a single visual operation: the model emits bounding-box coordinates, the environment crops that region of the original image and appends it as observation tokens (iMCoT). Reward = answer accuracy + format + a tool bonus granted only when the answer is correct and at least one crop was made. Data are selected for questions where zooming helps. Reports V* 90.1 (+18.9 over base) and HR-Bench gains, and describes a three-stage evolution of tool use during RL.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: How is credit assigned to the visual operation (crop) during RL, and is there any step-level signal?
Only trajectory-level. "Because intermediate visual actions lack step-level supervision, we evaluate the entire reasoning trajectory based on the final outcome and the presence of meaningful active perception" [full text, Section 3.2, Reward Design]. R(τ)=R_acc+R_format+1[R_acc>0]·R_tool (Eq 2); R_tool fires if at least one crop was made. GRPO advantage is applied to the whole trajectory; observation tokens are loss-masked [Section 3.2, Optimization]. Ablation (Table 5): no tool reward 87.4/53.4/55.4 on V*/HR-4K/HR-8K, unconditional bonus 87.4/72.1/71.8, conditional bonus 90.1/75.1/72.6 [Section 4.3, Tool Reward]. Nothing in the reward checks whether the crop contained the evidence or whether the answer depended on it.

### Goal: How accurate are the crops the trained policy chooses, and what failures are reported for the visual-operation loop?
Grounding IoU against V* ground-truth boxes is measured only as a diagnostic: 0.37 for DeepEyes-7B and 0.53 for DeepEyes-32B (Table 6) [Section 4.4]. Training dynamics show an early "broad sweep" stage of over-querying before efficient use [Section 4.3, Training Dynamics]. Failure cases: "grounding drift" — on a second zoom the target awning left the selected region, a blue area appeared, and the model reversed a correct hypothesis to a wrong answer; and a reasoning failure where the correct region was cropped but the zoomed curve was misread [Appendix D.2]. Limitations name "shortcuts, such as insufficient richness in the reasoning process and inaccurate target localization", attributed to the weak base model [Appendix E]. Only the crop operation is supported [Appendix G].
