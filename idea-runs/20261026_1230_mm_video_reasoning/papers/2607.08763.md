# OpenCoF: Learning to Reason Through Video Generation

## What this paper does
Builds OpenCoF-17K (17,312 81-frame reasoning videos over 11 task families from four curation pipelines) and LoRA-finetunes Wan2.2-I2V-A14B into Wan-CoF, then adds 16 learnable Visual Reasoning Tokens (prepended to the visual sequence in self-attention) or Textual Reasoning Tokens (appended to text conditioning). Gains on MME-CoF, Gen-ViRe, VIPER, RULER-Bench, but absolute VIPER POC stays low (3.3 -> 7.5 for data-only).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Where does the generator keep its intermediate reasoning state?
"The underlying generator still lacks a dedicated channel for maintaining reasoning state, and must express such information implicitly through pixel-level latents" [full text, Section 4]. vt tokens are global, frame-agnostic learned vectors discarded after the last block; tt tokens are fixed key/values "shared, unchanged, across every spatial patch and every temporal frame" [full text, Section 4.1]. Neither is tied to task state variables (positions, walls, object identities). Doubling tokens to 32 does not help uniformly [full text, Section 8].
### Goal: What limitation does it report?
Only that vt and tt are studied separately [full text, Section 6]. VIPER POC 7.5 after training [full text, Section 3.1] indicates most outputs still violate process constraints.
