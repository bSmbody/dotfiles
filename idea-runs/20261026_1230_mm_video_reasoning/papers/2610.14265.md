# Thinking with Images without Coordinates (TiCO)

## What this paper does
TiCO replaces coordinate-based crop calls with a coordinate-free operation: the policy emits a <GROUND> probe token whose final hidden state is dotted with visual patch tokens to form a similarity map; thresholded connected components are importance-sampled as evidence regions and re-injected as token-level regions, after which the model produces answer rollouts. Trained with RL, it reports gains over Mini-o3 and DeepEyes on HR-Bench and other benchmarks with lower latency and fewer tokens.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What limitation of coordinate-based visual operations does TiCO identify?
Two: a "semantic gap" — grounding reduced to numeric box regression, which next-token training models as discrete symbols, so "geometric correctness does not necessarily imply faithful semantic grounding"; and a "resolution gap" between patch-level tokens and pixel-level coordinates [full text, Section 1]. Coordinate regression "is sensitive to localization errors and prone to error propagation" (Fig 1 caption) [Section 1].

### Goal: How does TiCO choose and inject evidence regions?
α_i = softmax(h_<GROUND>ᵀ v_i/√d) over visual tokens (Eq 1); threshold τ gives a mask; connected components are scored and importance-sampled as "evidence rollouts", which condition answer rollouts [Sections 3.1–3.2]. Region choice is driven by the probe-patch similarity and the downstream answer reward; the method does not check whether the answer depended on the injected region.

### Goal: How is the region-selection step credited during RL?
Two-stage training [full text, Section 3.3]: SFT with dense patch-wise supervision of the similarity map from ground-truth boxes (Eq 6) plus next-token loss; then GRPO with accuracy (ROUGE-based lexical match) and format rewards only. Rollouts are nested — N1 sampled evidence regions × N2 answer rollouts per region — and the advantage A_ij is normalized over all N1·N2 rollouts jointly (Eqs 8–9). Region quality is therefore credited only through the answer reward of its child rollouts mixed in one pool; no separate per-region advantage or comparison with a no-region rollout is formed. (Correction to the previous answer: region choice is also shaped by SFT on GT boxes, not only by the answer reward.)
