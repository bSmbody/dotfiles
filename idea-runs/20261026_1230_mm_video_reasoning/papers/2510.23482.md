# On the Faithfulness of Visual Thinking: Measurement and Enhancement

## What this paper does
Studies "thinking with images" models (DeepEyes, Pixel-Reasoner) whose MCoT interleaves zoom-in crops with text. Intervention tests show answers barely change when the crops are replaced by noise but change strongly when text is corrupted, so visual thoughts are largely ignored. It adds a GPT-4o judge pipeline scoring reliability and sufficiency of the visual components, and proposes SCCM learning: an RL reward that pays when an external judge (Qwen2.5-VL-72B) can answer correctly from the crops alone (sufficiency), times a group-relative minimality reward on crop size.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism does it use to make the reasoning trace visually grounded/faithful?
r_final = r_acc + r_format + alpha * r_s * r_m [full text, Sec 5 / Eq 9]. r_s = 1[J_S(V) = A_GT], where J_S is an external LVLM (Qwen2.5-VL-72B in training) answering from the visual components V only [Eq 7]. r_m = mean tokens / own tokens of crops in the group (GRIM) [Eq 8]. Without minimality the policy crops the whole image and training destabilizes [Sec 7].
### Goal: Does its signal test whether the final answer causally depends on the trace's visual claims, or only claim truth / image sensitivity / attention?
The diagnostic does: ATE of replacing crops with noise on the policy's own accuracy, McNemar test [Sec 4.1, Hypothesis 2]. The training reward does not: sufficiency is judged by a different, larger model reading the crops, so it rewards crops that are informative for the judge, not crops the policy's answer actually depends on. After SCCM training the visual-intervention effect is only "partially mitigated" (lower p-value) [Sec 6.2, Table 1].
### Goal: What counterfactual/intervention operator does it use, and what limitation does it report?
Diagnostic: crops replaced with random noise; text intervention by GPT-4o mistake injection [Sec 4.1]. It notes presence-only rewards (curiosity reward) can be hacked by ineffective crops while the answer comes from text reasoning, causing a collapse in visual sufficiency [Sec 5, Sec 7].
