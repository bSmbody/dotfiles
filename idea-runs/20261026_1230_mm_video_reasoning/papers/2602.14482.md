# TikArt: Stabilizing Aperture-Guided Fine-Grained Visual Reasoning with Reinforcement Learning

## What this paper does
TikArt (Qwen3-VL-8B) runs a Think–Aperture–Observe loop with two visual operations, Zoom (box crop) and Segment (SAM2 mask view), and a constrained-decoding "mandatory Observation" step after each operation in which the policy must describe the view in text. Trained with GRPO under a two-stage curriculum using a composite reward: task correctness, a "look-and-win" action bonus, and Relative Uncertainty Reduction (RUR), a dense reward from a frozen Qwen3-VL-8B evaluator measuring how much the trajectory prefix raises teacher-forced confidence in the ground-truth answer. Reports gains on V*, HR-Bench, and RefCOCO/ReasonSeg.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What dense credit does TikArt give visual operations, at what granularity, and what does it actually measure?
RUR(τ) = clip((p_traj − p_base)/max(ε, 1 − p_base), −1, 1), where p is the frozen evaluator's per-token geometric-mean probability of the target answer y* given the input alone (p_base) or the input plus the whole trajectory prefix before <ANSWER> (p_traj) (Eqs 11–16) [full text, Section 3.5]. It is one scalar per trajectory, mixed with task and action rewards (task:action:RUR = 0.8:1.2:1.0, Eq 17) [Section 3.6], and the GRPO advantage is group-normalized per rollout [Section 3.4]. RUR "requires intermediate evidence to be explicitly written into the context" — it scores the policy's own Observation text [Section 3.3]. The authors state it is "a practical stabilization signal... rather than an evaluator-independent objective" [Section 3.5]. Nothing checks that an Observation is entailed by the returned view, and no per-operation attribution is made among several apertures in one trajectory. Action reward = 1[≥1 aperture]·1[success] (Eq 23).

### Goal: What failure does TikArt report when the link between the visual operation and later reasoning is not enforced?
Without the Observation step, training shows higher entropy, "uncontrolled aperture actions, and degraded reward" (Fig 4) [Section 3.6]; "aperture calls are easier to degenerate into weakly grounded or noisy tool usage, because their contribution is not forced into the trajectory" [Section 3.3]. Removing RUR hurts both QA and segmentation (e.g. ReasonSeg 71.2 gIoU w/o RUR) [Section 4 ablations]. Sparse outcome rewards give "weak credit assignment" and within-group reward ties under GRPO [Section 1].
