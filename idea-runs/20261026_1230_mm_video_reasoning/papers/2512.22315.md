# VideoZoomer: Reinforcement-Learned Temporal Focusing for Long Video Reasoning

## What this paper does
VideoZoomer casts long-video QA as multi-turn tool interaction: the MLLM first "glances" at a low-fps uniform version of the video, then may call <video_zoom>[t_start,t_end] to receive a high-fps clip, repeating until it answers (max 4 calls). It is trained by cold-start SFT on ~11K distilled GPT-4o/Gemini trajectories plus expert-corrected "reflection" trajectories, then multi-turn GRPO with a trajectory-end reward. It reports better accuracy at smaller frame budgets on MLVU, LVBench, LongVideoBench and LongVideoReason.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism does it use to locate or re-inspect temporal evidence in long videos?
"First glance, then zoom": the window is proposed by the policy conditioned only on the low-fps uniform overview V_low plus previous zoomed clips [full text, Sec 3.1]. Replacing the uniform glance with a TSPO-selected overview adds +2.0 (MLVU) and +3.0 (LongVideoBench) [Sec 4.3, Table 4], so localization quality is bounded by what the glance shows. Accuracy gains concentrate in the first 1–2 calls and saturate after 2 [App. B.5, Table 11].
### Goal: How does its RL reward assign credit to the evidence the model uses (outcome-only, IoU vs annotation, other)?
Reward is assigned once at trajectory end: R = R_acc (final answer correct) + R_format + R_tool, where R_tool is a tool-use bonus "only awarded if the final answer is correct" [full text, Sec 3.3, Eq 1]. There is no grounding or evidence reward; every zoom in a correct trajectory receives the same credit whether or not the zoomed clip contained the evidence. Without R_tool, tool usage collapses toward zero [Sec 4.3]; without reflection data the policy calls the tool once and answers "regardless of whether the retrieved clip was actually helpful" [Sec 3.2].
### Goal: What limitation or failure mode does it report on long-video temporal reasoning?
Shallow policies (one call then answer) without reflection data; policy collapse without the conditional tool bonus [Sec 3.2, 4.3]. On LongVideoReason both the model and baseline peak around 64 frames, more frames adding noise [Sec 4.3].
