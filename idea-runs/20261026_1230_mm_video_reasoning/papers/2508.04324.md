# TempFlow-GRPO: When Timing Matters for GRPO in Flow Models

## What this paper does
TempFlow-GRPO (text-to-image, SD3.5/FLUX, human-preference rewards) argues that Flow-GRPO's uniform credit assignment over denoising timesteps is the main inefficiency. It adds trajectory branching (deterministic ODE except one SDE step k, so the terminal reward is attributable to step k) and noise-level-proportional reweighting of the per-step policy loss.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it provide finer-grained credit assignment than one terminal reward per sample, and along which axis?
Yes, but only along the denoising-time axis: "sparse terminal rewards with uniform credit assignment fail to capture the varying criticality of decisions across generation timesteps" [full text, Abstract]. Branching at step k replaces the step-k reward with R(ODE(SDE(x_k))) so variance is localized to that step (Theorem 1) [full text, Section 4.1.1]; Eq 7 weights the loss by Norm(sigma_t sqrt(dt)) [full text, Section 4.1.2]. The reward is still a single scalar per image from an outcome reward model; there is no spatial or (for video) per-frame attribution; experiments are image-only preference alignment [full text, Sections 4-6].
