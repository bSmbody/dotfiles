# Open-o3-Video-v2: Seek, Ground, and Reason with Verifiable Spatio-Temporal Evidence

## What this paper does
Open-o3-Video (v1) trains a video MLLM to cite timestamps and object boxes inside its thinking and rewards them against spatio-temporal annotations. v2 adds agentic temporal zoom (iterative crop_video calls) and Clue-Seek GRPO, whose tool reward tracks an evidence state: the temporal IoU between the union of retrieved intervals and annotated answer-relevant clue intervals, plus gain, budget, repetition, fragmentation and drop terms. Total reward sums accuracy, format, grounded-thinking and tool terms.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does it reward cited temporal/spatial evidence, and what does the reward compare against?
Grounded-thinking reward r_thk = r_t + r_s: r_t checks cited timestamps against interval/point annotations, r_s is box IoU against the annotation at the matched timestamp, gated by temporal proximity [full text, Section 3.2 / Eq 1-2; Section 4.4]. Tool reward: gamma_k = tIoU(union of retrieved intervals, union of annotated clue intervals) (Eq 8), r_clue = gamma_K, r_gain counts steps raising gamma by > 0.1, plus penalties (Eq 9-14); all combined into one trajectory-level scalar (Eq 15). For samples without clue intervals, r_tool is only regularization against excessive or repeated calls (Section 4.4).

### Goal: What limitations or failure modes does it report?
v1 limitation: "its grounded reasoning supervision remains limited in scale and diversity" [full text, Section 3.3]. On LVBench errors, precise temporal evidence retrieval and event selection account for about 43%, object grounding and fine perception 27% [Section 12.2].
