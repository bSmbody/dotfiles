# Find, Fix, Reason: Context Repair for Video Reasoning

## What this paper does
FFR adds an observation-level intervention to GRPO for video QA: when a student rollout fails, a frozen tool-using teacher (e.g., GLM-4.5V) diagnoses the error type and supplies a minimal evidence patch (frame ranges, regions) without leaking the answer; the student re-answers with the patch and the patched rollout replaces the failed one in the group via a Robust Improvement Reward. Trained on Video-R1 data at 16 frames; gains on VSI-Bench, MMVU and others. ICML 2026.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism does it use to locate or re-inspect temporal evidence in long videos?
The student does not search; the teacher uses get_frame / zoom_region / get_temporal_segment tools and returns temporal ranges rather than an "answer frame" [full text, Sec 3.2; App. A.4].
### Goal: How does its RL reward assign credit to the evidence the model uses (outcome-only, IoU vs annotation, other)?
RIR: R̃_i = z_i(R(τ_i) + R_fmt) + (1 − z_i)(R(τ*_i) + R_fmt − κ), where R ∈ {0,1} is answer correctness of the original or patched rollout [full text, Sec 3.2]. "Dependency alignment" is supplied by the teacher choosing the patch; the reward itself remains answer correctness and does not measure whether the answer depended on the patch.
### Goal: What limitation or failure mode does it report on long-video temporal reasoning?
Teacher analysis attributes 41.2% of student errors to misconception, 32% spatial, 26.8% temporal [Sec 4.5]. It relies on a large external teacher at training time; no limitation specific to long videos is reported in the body read.
