# Beyond Entropy: Self-Diagnostic Multi-Role Token Optimization for Video Reasoning

## What this paper does
DyCPO targets token-level credit in video RL without annotations. It scores tokens by sensitivity to both a visual counterfactual and an answer counterfactual (JSD and entropy shift) times entropy, keeps the top-k "multi-role" tokens, and adds a contrastive self-distillation loss pulling their distributions toward a lightly jittered view and away from a view with key evidence removed, applied only on positive-advantage rollouts. Key frames for the counterfactual come from frames attended by correct rollouts but not by incorrect ones of an EMA proposal model.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does it decide which video moments are evidence, without annotations?
Rollouts are split by advantage sign into R_r (A >= 0) and R_w (A < 0); per-rollout top-K2 attended frames Psi^(i) are computed from response-to-frame attention (Eq 10-12); critical frames U* = Psi^+ \ Psi^- (Eq 13), "the gap between the visual focus of correct and incorrect rollouts" [full text, Section 3.3]. Evidence is thus inferred from attention differences between outcome groups, not from checking what any trace says about a frame.

### Goal: How does the signal enter the policy update?
L_total = L_GRPO + lambda/|C| * sum_m 1(A_i > 0) * L^MRC (Eq 9): the contrastive term acts only on successful rollouts' selected tokens "to avoid reinforcing spurious patterns from failed rollouts" [full text, Section 3.2]; failed rollouts get only the ordinary GRPO penalty.
