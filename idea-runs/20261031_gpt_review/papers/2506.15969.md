# LazyEviction: Lagged KV Eviction with Attention Pattern Observation for Efficient Long Reasoning

## What this paper does
Identifies Token Importance Recurrence (TIR) in reasoning traces: tokens (problem conditions, intermediate results) get low attention for a while and then are attended again; >95% of tokens show recurrence. Proposes LazyEviction, which evicts only every W steps, tracks each token's last-activation timestamp and Maximum Recurrence Interval (MRI), and keeps tokens whose time since last activation is below their MRI. Reports near-FullKV accuracy at 30-50% budgets on GSM8K/MATH-500/AIME with 4B-32B reasoning models.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What signal does it use to decide which KV entries to keep, evict, or recall, and is the decision reversible?
A token's timestamp updates whenever its attention exceeds threshold alpha; MRI_t = max(MRI_{t-1}, TS_t - TS_{t-1}) [full text, Section 4, Eq 1]. The score combines H1 = 2*sigmoid(-(t-TS)/MRI) and H2 = 2*sigmoid(-1/(MRI-1)) [Eq 2]; every W steps the top B-W by score plus the most recent W are kept, the rest deleted permanently.

### Goal: What does it report as its failure mode or limitation for tokens that are needed later (long chain-of-thought or long context)?
H2O and TOVA lose ~20% on GSM8K at 50% compression because tokens critical later are missing from the top set at earlier steps [Section 3, Fig 2]. Limitations: the window W must be set offline from the MRI distribution of 1% of test samples, which is "inefficient and sub-optimal"; on non-reasoning text recurring tokens have small MRI and the method is no better than H2O [Limitations]. By construction a token with MRI=0 (never re-activated yet) gets H2=0 [Section 4], so a token whose first reuse comes far in the future has no history signal to protect it.

### Goal: What method assumption does its scoring or training rely on (per-token additive utility, per-head independence, teacher-forced or offline training)?
Per-token history statistic (timestamps and MRI from the token's own past attention); assumes the past recurrence interval predicts future reuse. Training-free, per-token independent, no cross-head modelling.
