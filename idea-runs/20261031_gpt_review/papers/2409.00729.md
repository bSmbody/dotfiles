# ContextCite: Attributing Model Generation to Context

## What this paper does
Defines context attribution: score each source (e.g. sentence) of a context by how much including or excluding it changes the probability of a given response. ContextCite samples random ablation vectors over the sources, measures the logit-scaled response probability for each, and fits a sparse (Lasso) linear surrogate whose weights are the attribution scores. It uses the scores to verify statements, to prune the context (regenerating from top-k sources), and to detect poisoning. Evaluated on Llama-3 and Mistral models with summarization and QA data.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How are joint ablation masks sampled, and how does one batch of masked forward passes yield a score for every source?
Step 1 samples ablation vectors v_1..v_n, each "uniformly from {0,1}^d", i.e. each source is kept with probability 1/2 independently, and computes g(v) = logit p_LM(R | Ablate(C, v), Q) for each [full text, Section 3, Algorithm 1]. Step 2 fits one Lasso regression of g on v. Every masked pass therefore informs every source's weight at once; the weight is the effect of ablating that source on the logit-probability. Sparsity is motivated by the finding that only a handful of sources are relevant, so 32 ablations suffice for a 98-sentence context [Section 3, Figures 2-3]. Interactions are assumed roughly additive ("the effect of removing both sources is close to the sum") [Section 3, Figure 2].

### Goal: What is its scope: is it used for KV eviction or decode-time memory management, and in which architectures?
No. It is post-hoc attribution of a fixed response to a fixed prompt context in Transformers. The pruning application is three-step: generate with the full context, attribute, then regenerate from only the top-k sources [Section 5.2]. Masks are uniform, not drawn at a deployment keep rate, and it needs at least 32 extra forward passes per example; there is no learned cross-example predictor, no generated-token (CoT) cache, and no recurrent path.
