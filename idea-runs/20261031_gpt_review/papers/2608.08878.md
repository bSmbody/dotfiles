# DistillCache: KL-Guided Adaptive KV-Cache Eviction for Memory-Efficient LLM Inference

## What this paper does
Formulates decoding-time KV eviction as a sequential decision problem and trains a small MLP policy with REINFORCE (RLOO baseline, entropy bonus) to score each cached token from five features (average attention mass, value norm, cross-head attention variance, attention-weighted query entropy, position). The reward is the per-step negative KL between the full-cache and pruned-cache next-token distributions, computed with a never-pruned teacher cache alongside the pruned student cache. On Mistral-7B-Instruct it retains 94.2% of full-cache LongBench accuracy at 25% budget and is compared with re-implemented ForesightKV and RLKV.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What signal does it use to decide which KV entries to keep, evict, or recall, and is the decision reversible?
A learned scalar s_i = f_phi(x_i) over a 5-d per-token feature vector [full text, Sections 3.1.1-3.1.2]; at inference the top-B tokens are kept deterministically each step and the rest pruned; the retained cache is used by the next step [Section 3.3, Algorithm 1]. Pruning is permanent.

### Goal: What does it report as its failure mode or limitation for tokens that are needed later (long chain-of-thought or long context)?
Limitations: ~130 GPU-hours on 2x RTX 4090 and doubled training memory for the teacher cache; RLKV remains slightly stronger on reasoning at moderate budgets; "the policy scores tokens independently without explicitly modeling inter-token interactions"; ForesightKV/RLKV comparisons are re-implementations [Section 5]. Evaluation generates at most 512 new tokens (GSM8K 8-shot CoT, BBH, LongBench) [Appendix D], so long-CoT (thousands of tokens) is not tested.

### Goal: What method assumption does its scoring or training rely on (per-token additive utility, per-head independence, teacher-forced or offline training)?
Per-token independent scoring (stated in its limitations). The reward R_t = -KL(P_t || P^_t) is a per-step local distribution-matching signal against the full-cache model, motivated by a bound of KL by the squared evicted attention mass [Section 3.1.4]; returns are discounted sums of these per-step KLs [Section 3.2]. Both caches append the same generated token each step [Section 3.1.3], so the objective scores next-token fidelity along a shared token stream rather than task outcome; training is offline, once per model configuration [Section 3.3].
