# ForesightKV: Optimizing KV Cache Eviction for Reasoning Models by Learning Long-Term Contribution

## What this paper does
Learns lightweight per-KV-group MLP scorers that predict each KV pair's long-term contribution and drive periodic eviction during long reasoning generation. Stage 1 trains with a pairwise ranking loss on "Golden Eviction" labels (max future block attention computed from the full-cache attention matrix); stage 2 refines with GRPO, treating eviction as an MDP whose reward penalizes loss increases on low-entropy tokens. The LLM stays frozen. On AIME24/25 with Qwen3 and R1-Distill models it preserves 92%/99% of full performance at 2K/4K budgets and beats R-KV, SnapKV and H2O at half their budget.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What signal does it use to decide which KV entries to keep, evict, or recall, and is the decision reversible?
An MLP over the concatenation of key, value and fixed-length attention features of each KV pair produces a scalar importance phi_n [full text, Section 3.1, Eq 1]. Every L generated tokens the cache is pruned back to the budget by Top-2L-then-multinomial sampling of the lowest-scored entries [Eq 2]. The paper itself calls eviction "sequential and irreversible": "an erroneous eviction cannot be recovered in later steps" [Section 3.1].

### Goal: What does it report as its failure mode or limitation for tokens that are needed later (long chain-of-thought or long context)?
Empirical study: semantic-dependent heads show block-wise attention that shifts as reasoning moves between blocks, so SnapKV "often discards tokens that have little semantic association with the tokens of that window but are subsequently important" [Section 2.1]. Eviction raises loss on low-entropy tokens by +147% (math), much more than on high-entropy tokens, and errors on these tokens are numbers/symbols/entities from earlier context that accumulate and distort later reasoning [Section 2.2, Table 1].

### Goal: What method assumption does its scoring or training rely on (per-token additive utility, per-head independence, teacher-forced or offline training)?
(a) Per-token scalar scoring: each KV pair is scored independently from its own features, and the supervised target is a per-token future score alpha_{i,t} = max over future blocks of pooled attention [Section 3.2, Eq 3-5]. (b) Training is on fixed traces: data are correct Qwen3-4B traces >4096 tokens on STILL [Appendix E.1]; RL samples G eviction traces "on a full reasoning sequence" w, where "the same token will exhibit different hidden states" — i.e., the token sequence is fixed (teacher forced) and only hidden states change [Section 3.3]. The reward is the squared loss increase on low-entropy tokens of that fixed sequence [Eq 7-8], and one sequence-level advantage is broadcast to every eviction decision of all scorers [Eq 9-10]. Training cost ~8h x2 + 6h x8 H800 GPUs for the 7B model [Appendix E.1].
