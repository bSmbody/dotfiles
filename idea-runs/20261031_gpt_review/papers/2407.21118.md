# Palu: Compressing KV-Cache with Low-Rank Projection

## What this paper does
Palu factorizes the K/V projection weights by SVD, caches the low-rank latent h = x A, and reconstructs keys/values on the fly. For values (and for non-RoPE keys) it fuses the reconstruction matrix into neighbouring weights; for RoPE keys it reconstructs online in a fused Triton kernel. It studies decomposition granularity (per-head M-LRD, grouped G-LRD, joint J-LRD), automatic rank allocation, and quantization of the latent.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does Palu compute RoPE attention scores from a low-rank key cache, and does it materialize full keys in HBM?
RoPE prevents matrix fusion, so Palu reconstructs keys during decoding [full text, Section 3.1.2]. Its kernel tiles over sequence length; each thread block reconstructs a tile H_i B, applies RoPE and multiplies by q, with "all intermediate computations ... entirely in on-chip memory" [full text, Appendix B]. Full keys are not written to HBM.
### Goal: What speedups and bottlenecks does it report for that kernel?
The score kernel reaches up to 3.56x over PyTorch GEMV for per-head decomposition (gs-1) and 1.95x for gs-4 at 16K, while joint-head (gs-32) is slower than the baseline at all lengths because reconstruction FLOPs r_joint*d_h*n become compute-bound; speedup also falls beyond 16K as reconstruction "shift[s] from memory- to compute-bound" [full text, Section 4.5.2; Section 3.2.2]. RoPE attention module: 1.89x at 64K; non-RoPE (fully fused): 2.20x [full text, Section 4.5.1].
