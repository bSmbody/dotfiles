# MLAfication: Efficiently Enabling High-Fidelity MLA for Modern LLMs

## What this paper does
Extends MHA2MLA to modern LLMs with QKNorm and MoE (Qwen3-30B-A3B etc.): calibrated KNorm approximation, parameter-efficient distillation, contribution-aware partial-RoPE, and activation-aware joint KV low-rank initialization (SVD-LLM-V2 objective). Up to 5.35x serving throughput vs BF16 GQA.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does the conversion choose the latent subspace and the retained RoPE dimensions, and what objective does it fit?
Partial-RoPE reuses the contribution-aware subspace rule of MHA2MLA-VLM unchanged [full text, Section 2.3 (1)]. The joint K/V latent minimizes ||W_kv X - W'_kv X||_F^2 on calibration activations, i.e. per-token KV output reconstruction [full text, Section 2.3 (2), Eqs 7-8].
### Goal: What does it report about long-context fidelity and limitations?
Training-free conversion keeps short-context accuracy (LLaMA-2-7B commonsense 59.85 -> 58.20 TransMLA / 52.68 MLAfication) but LongBench falls from 27.40 to 1.34 / 1.06; the authors suggest "modification of RoPE weakens positional discriminability, which is more critical for long-context tasks", and so treat training-free numbers only as diagnostics and rely on the recovery stage [full text, Section 3.6, Table 7].
