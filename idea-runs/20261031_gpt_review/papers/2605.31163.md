# Memory by Design: Bayesian Layers for Sequence Modeling

## What this paper does
The paper derives recurrent sequence layers from explicit Bayesian design models of memory. The Bayesian Layer runs exact linear-Gaussian filtering and propagates a mean memory together with a covariance over stored associations. The covariance maps each raw write key to an effective write direction, so resolved (confident) directions receive smaller writes and preserved memories are not overwritten. DeltaNet-family rules come out as covariance-reset reductions, and linear attention, GLA and Mamba-2 as exact filters of a latent-input model. It is tested on controlled collision recall, Zoology MQAR, and WikiText-103 and distillation.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What decides which stored associations are protected, and is any read-side signal used?
The predicted covariance P sets the write direction u_t = P k_t and the directional variance k^T P k [full text, Section 2.3 / Eq 14]. Repeated writes along an address contract its variance. A colliding write then "rotates away from the shared component toward the unresolved residual", which protects the boosted association [Eq 15-16; Figure 2]. Protection therefore comes from accumulated *write* evidence: an address is protected because it was written (boosted), not because it was read. The readout uses only the posterior mean, and the covariance "is not exposed" to the read side [Section 5, Limitations]. The protection acts by redirecting the write itself, not by redirecting decay.
### Goal: What does it cost?
Each head carries G dense D x D covariance blocks: recurrent size O(Dm + G D^2) and per-token cost O(G D^2) [Section 5, Limitations]. Training uses a chunkwise scan with sequential chunk boundaries. Within a chunk, a covariance pass produces the per-step gains, and the mean update is then a chunkwise linear-attention recurrence [Appendix D, Chunkwise scan].


### Goal: How does the Bayesian Layer propagate and forget its dense covariance, and is its chunkwise scan parallel within a chunk?
Appendix D / Eq 29-30 [full text]: P_bar_t = A_t P_{t-1} A_t^T + l_t^2 I, then the rank-one downdate P_t = P_bar_t - beta_t u_t u_t^T, with u_t = P_bar_t k_t. A_t is diagonal (damped 2x2 rotations), and the process noise l_t^2 I is isotropic and full rank at every token. The process noise on the imaginary rows is masked, because isotropic noise there gives "a divergent covariance over long sequences". The covariance is dense D x D per group (D = 256 in the GDN-340M distillation). Chunkwise scan: chunk boundaries are processed sequentially. Within a chunk, "a covariance pass over the chunk's tau steps produces the per-tau gains" from the chunk-entry covariance, done by a custom Triton kernel in fp32. Only the mean update is then WY-parallel [Appendix D, Chunkwise scan]. So the dense covariance is exact, but its within-chunk pass is token-sequential, because the per-step isotropic noise term is not low-rank.
