# Apriel-H1: Towards Efficient Enterprise Reasoning Models

## What this paper does
Distills a 15B reasoning Transformer (Apriel-Nemotron-15B-Thinker, 50 layers) into Mamba-attention hybrids by incremental replacement (H1-25/50 up to H1-40/50), with layer importance estimated before each stage, reverse-KL distillation on ~9B tokens of reasoning SFT traces per phase at 16K length, and reports a smooth throughput/accuracy trade-off on MATH500, AIME, GPQA, etc.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism decides which attention layers to replace, and on what signal?
Leave-one-out (LOO): replace layer l by identity in the teacher and measure MMLU accuracy drop, no training [full text, Section 3.1, Eq 4]. MIL-Mamba-Replacement (MMR): swap layer l to a Mamba mixer, run 100 distillation steps, use the final KD loss as importance [Section 3.1]. Both are teacher-forced or short-answer signals; neither scores the student on its own long reasoning rollouts. Layer rankings change after 100 distillation steps [Figure 2].

### Goal: What limitation does it report for reasoning hybrids?
Accuracy drops roughly linearly as more Mamba layers are added; the token budget for reasoning distillation is "notably high" (tens of billions), suggesting reasoning transfer needs more data exposure [Section 4, Critical analysis; Figure 4]. Recovering quality required extra SFT on reasoning traces [Section 4].
