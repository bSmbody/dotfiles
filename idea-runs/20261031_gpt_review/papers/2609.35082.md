# Cross-Rollout Bellman Closure for Long-Horizon Agentic Reinforcement Learning

## What this paper does
CRBC is a critic-free step-credit method for LLM agents. It merges a rollout group into a finite empirical process over shared anchor states with absorbing success and failure boundaries, using empirical action and transition frequencies. It computes the behaviour-policy Bellman fixed point with one linear solve, V = (I - beta M)^{-1} b. Step credit is Q - V, normalized by the anchor-wise std and combined with the GRPO trajectory advantage. A finite-depth family links visit-local suffix averaging (K=0, GiGPO-style) to the full closure.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does CRBC pool evidence across rollouts that share states, and are the weights variance-optimal?
The visit-local estimator weights each visit's realized return equally [full text, Section 4.1 Eq 6]. The closure propagates evidence across rollouts through the empirical kernel M_g [Section 4.2 Eq 7-9]. All weights are empirical frequencies, fixed by the counts. They are not chosen to minimize estimator variance or gradient MSE.

### Goal: Does it analyze covariance or double counting from shared terminal outcomes?
No. Its theory covers well-posedness, alignment of credit with value improvement on the fixed empirical process, and finite-depth residual transport [Sections 4.2-4.3; Appendix B.1-B.4]. Appendix B.3 "Provenance" decomposes how suffix evidence reaches a successor from a given (s,a) to bound residuals. It is not a variance or covariance analysis.
