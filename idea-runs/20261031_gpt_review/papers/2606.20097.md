# HydraHead: From Head-Level Functional Heterogeneity to Specialized Attention Hybridization

## What this paper does
Converts Qwen3-1.7B into a head-wise hybrid: within each layer a subset of query heads keeps full attention (FA) and the rest use Gated DeltaNet, fused with per-branch RMSNorm. Which heads keep FA is chosen once, before training, by causal intervention (activation patching with frozen downstream attention plus iterated path patching) on counterfactual NIAH pairs at 4K, and the top-K heads by fused score keep FA. Trained with a three-stage pipeline (parameter migration plus layer-wise alignment, logits distillation, long-context fine-tuning on FineWeb-Edu, ~15B tokens). Evaluated on RULER NIAH (native and extended) and lm-eval-harness short benchmarks; claims parity with a 3:1 layer-wise hybrid at a 7:1 LA:FA ratio.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does it decide which attention units keep full attention, and on what data and signal?
Per-head causal importance from activation patching (receiver effect, measured with downstream attention outputs frozen to clean values so compensation is excluded) and path patching (sender effect, iterated about two rounds), on a calibration set of five RULER NIAH sub-probes with eight counterfactual pairs each at 4K; a head is critical if normalized effect >= 0.01; scores are fused across capabilities and the global top-K heads keep FA, with K set by a hand-chosen FA budget [full text, Section 4.1, Eqs 9-12; Section 5.1.1]. The selection is one-shot on the pretrained teacher, not on the hybrid's own generations, and no on-policy stage is used [Section 1; Section 4.3].

### Goal: Does it reduce the full-attention budget below a 3:1 rule, and what evidence supports that?
Yes, at head granularity: it reports that interpretability-guided selection matches the overall performance of a 3:1 layer-wise hybrid at 7:1, and compares 3:1, 7:1 and 9:1 with and without an at-least-one-FA-head-per-layer constraint [Section 1 findings; Section 5.5, Table 7]. RULER Single native drops from 98.7 (3:1) to 81.4 (7:1) under pure global screening, so the "match" is against the weaker layer-wise baseline, not against its own 3:1 [Table 7]. Only about 6.5% of heads are causally critical for retrieval and they are scattered across layers [Section 5.5 discussion].

### Goal: Does it evaluate long chain-of-thought reasoning or long generation?
No. Reasoning is measured with lm-eval-harness default (mostly greedy, short-answer) settings on MMLU, GSM8K, MBPP, BBH and easier tasks; long-context is RULER retrieval [Section 5.1.2]. No AIME, no thinking-mode long generation, no reverse-KL on-policy training.
