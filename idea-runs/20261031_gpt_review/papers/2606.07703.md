# How Much Dense Attention is Necessary? Oracle-Guided Sparse Prefill for Full/GQA Layers in Hybrid Long-Context Models

## What this paper does
Technical report on sparse prefill for the remaining full/GQA layers of Qwen-family (incl. hybrid Qwen3.5) checkpoints: an attention-mass top-k oracle per layer and query measures how much dense attention is needed; a head-collapsed indexer is then KL-distilled from dense attention mass with the backbone frozen, giving 1.7-1.9x TTFT with small quality gaps at 16K/32K.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What criterion selects the support kept by full-attention layers in hybrids, and does it consider the recurrent layers?
Support is chosen per layer from that layer's own dense attention mass (head-averaged top-k), and the indexer is distilled to match it [full text, Sections 4.1, 6.2]. Hybrid layers are framed as "quality-critical global communication points" [Section 2.1]; the criterion does not use or model the linear/recurrent components.

### Goal: What limitation does it state?
Targets prefill only: "dense decode still attends over the cache and therefore receives no decode-side attention, KV-traffic, or KV-storage reduction" [Section 10]. Evidence is mostly Qwen GQA checkpoints and retrieval-heavy RULER-style tasks [Section 10].
