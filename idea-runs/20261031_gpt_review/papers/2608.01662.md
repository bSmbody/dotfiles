# LongCat Sparse Attention: Taming the Lightning via Streaming-aware Hierarchical Cross-Layer Indexing

## What this paper does
A system-algorithm co-design on top of DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) for 69B-560B MoE models up to 1M context. It reserves sink and sliding-window budgets so KV access is coalesced (streaming-aware indexing). It reuses one indexer's selection across a group of layers, trained by cross-layer distillation. It also filters coarsely at block level and then refines at token level (hierarchical indexing). Custom kernels support all three. It reports quality on par with full attention and supports LongCat-2.0.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How is the indexer trained given the hard top-K selection, and does it get a signal on tokens it did not select (dense teacher, exploration, or neither)?
It follows DSA's two stages. (1) Dense warm-up: the backbone is frozen and the indexer is fit by KL to the head-summed full attention distribution over all tokens [full text, Section 2.2 / Eq 4]. (2) Sparse training: the backbone and indexer are trained jointly, and "the indexer is now supervised only over the selected token set S_t, with the attention target renormalized over this subset". The indexer input is detached [Section 2.2 / Eq 5]. LSA keeps this scheme. It distills over the whole selected set, including the sink and SWA portions, for a richer signal [Section 3.1 "Training"], and cross-layer distillation applies in both stages [Section 3.2 / Eq 8]. No exploration of unselected tokens is used. After warm-up, off-support tokens get no signal of their own.
