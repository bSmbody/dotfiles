# STEPQuant: When and Where Errors Matter in Delta-Rule Recurrent State Quantization

## What this paper does
Spatial-temporal post-training quantization for delta-rule recurrent states (Qwen3.8-27B, Kimi-Linear-48B). Temporal part: lifetime-aware bit allocation that weights per-unit reconstruction distortion by how long errors persist under the retention gate, plus FP16 "pivot" units. Spatial part: key-row-aware dual-axis (row and column) scale fitting. Close to FP32 at a nominal 6-bit budget, with SGLang kernels.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How is precision/capacity allocated across state units, and is the allocation fixed or input-dependent?
Bits b_u per unit (a head in Qwen GDN, a key row in KDA) minimize sum_u L_u d_u(b_u) under a budget, with lifetime weight L_u = sum_j exp(2 j l_u) from the mean log retention over calibration tokens [full text, Section 3.2, Eqs 6-8]. "The pivot selection and bit allocation are determined during offline calibration and fixed across requests"; the packed layout is fixed with no per-token allocation [full text, Sections 3.2, 4.4].
