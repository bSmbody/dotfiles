# AIRE-Prune: Asymptotic Impulse-Response Energy for State Pruning in State Space Models

## What this paper does
Post-training structured state pruning for diagonal SSMs (S5, S4D, Mamba) that scores each mode by its closed-form infinite-horizon impulse-response energy and normalizes scores layer-wise (prefix-sum normalization) for one global threshold. Removes 60.8% of states on average with 0.29 pp accuracy loss on LRA and Speech Commands, without retraining.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What mechanism selects which recurrent-state units are removed, and is the selection fixed or input-dependent?
The score is derived for an LTI layer x_{k+1}=Lambda x_k + B u_k, y=Cx, as the H2 energy sum_k ||C Lambda^k B||^2 per mode, depending only on |lambda_i| and the B/C couplings [full text, Section 4, Eq 5]. Scores are "computed once and applied globally" [full text, Section 6]; the selection is a fixed mode set, extending modal truncation from single LTI systems to deep stacks.
### Goal: What method assumption limits it on input-selective models?
The LTI energy is defined for fixed Lambda, B, C. The authors list as future work analysing "input-selective models like Mamba, where state matrices are input dependent", e.g. Monte-Carlo estimation of energy under gating dynamics [full text, Section 6, Future Work]. Mamba results are on LRA-style tasks (Text, Retrieval, Image), not language-model recall [full text, Section 5.2].
