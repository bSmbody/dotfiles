# PACE: Periodic and Channel-Aligned Encoding of Four-Bit Recurrent States

## What this paper does
Post-hoc 4-bit state quantizer for gated-delta hybrids (Qwen3.5/3.6, 4B-35B). Rounds the state once every dP decode tokens while a decode buffer holds the in-between (a, k, v) tokens; uses scale groups along the key axis (each group inside one column, because large entries gather in columns that follow channels of the delta value); keeps a quarter of heads at 8 bits chosen by a retained-error bound. PACE-4/8 approaches DAMP INT8 at about two thirds of its resident memory.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What error-persistence model does its allocation use, and does it include delta-rule erasure and cross-step error correlation?
Eq 5: between roundings the error is multiplied by a_t(I - beta_t k_t k_t^T), whose norm is bounded by a_t; with head mean decay a_h, "independent errors from successive roundings accumulate to at most E_h = e_h / (1 - a_h^{2 dP})" [full text, Section 3.2, Eq 5]. The bound uses decay only (the key-direction erasure is bounded away) and assumes independent errors across roundings. Heads with largest E_h get 8 bits; ranking is "computed once per checkpoint on eight held-out windows of 2,048 tokens" [full text, Section 5.3].
### Goal: When is rounding/quantization committed or precision changed (timing), and is that schedule fixed or input-adaptive?
One global decode period dP (default 8, matching DeltaLog's flush interval), and the prefill period is fixed at the 256-token kernel chunk [full text, Sections 3.1, 7.1]. Although Eq 5 makes the retained error a per-head function of a_h^{2 dP} and the buffer cost grows as c*dP (Eq 6), the period is the same for every head and request. Limitations: prefill period effect not measured; the RULER-32k gap is the same at all decode periods, "which suggests that this part of the gap arises in prefill" [full text, Section 7.1; Limitations].
### Goal: How does it treat the attention-layer KV cache versus the recurrent state, and is the byte budget shared between them?
Only the recurrent state and its buffer are counted ("resident bits per value"); KV-cache quantization is discussed as related work on caches "written once and only read" [full text, Sections 2.4, 5.1]. No joint budget with the attention KV.
