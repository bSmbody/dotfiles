# Preserving Updates in Low-Bit Recurrent State Quantization

## What this paper does
Training-free 4-bit recurrent-state quantization for GDN, GLA, Mamba2 and RetNet. Finds per-step requantization errors add coherently because small updates are lost in rounding (grid spacing fitted to the accumulated state is far larger than one update), and proposes Low-Rank Bypass (buffer small updates in low-rank form, merge periodically) and Bounded Directional Rounding (round reversed elements toward their update). 4.7-5.3x state memory reduction, up to 1.9x decode speedup.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What happens to recurrent-state compression as decode horizon grows, and what limits current methods?
Under 4-bit quantization 68-96% of nonzero update elements are lost per step (median update/grid-spacing ratio 0.02-0.21), and these lost updates carry the coherent error [full text, Section 2.3]. INT4 degradation grows with context length and INT4 hurts recall even at 512-1K tokens (worst on FDA); LRB+BDR narrows the gap to within ~1 point on GDN/GLA [full text, Section 4]. Evaluation goes to 8K PPL; hybrids and joint weight/activation quantization are left to future work [full text, Section 6].
