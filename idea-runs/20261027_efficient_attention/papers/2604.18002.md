# Neural Garbage Collection: Learning to Forget while Learning to Reason

## What this paper does
NGC fine-tunes a reasoning LLM (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B) with outcome-reward RL (Dr. GRPO) so that it learns both to reason and to evict its own KV cache. Every delta tokens an eviction round scores 32-token blocks per layer by the attention of the 5 most recent queries (no new parameters), samples the kept blocks with Gumbel-top-k so the kept set has an exact log-probability, and permanently drops the rest. Replay attention masks reproduce the visibility of each generated token in one forward pass. On Countdown and AMC/AIME it beats SnapKV, KeyDiff, KNorm and StreamingLLM at 2-5x peak-cache reduction.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Is the evictor trained on its own free-running rollouts with a task-level signal, and how is credit for each eviction decision assigned?
Yes to the first: rollouts are on-policy and free-running; tokens and eviction subsets are both actions of one policy, and the only signal is a binary task reward [full text, Section 4.4]. Credit is not localized: one group-normalized advantage A_i = r_i - mean(r) multiplies the token log-probs (Eq 3) and, averaged over eviction rounds, the log-prob of every eviction subset in every layer (Eq 4); "A_i is the same for both L_token and L_mem". The paper notes that per-key eviction "creates a credit assignment problem: the marginal contribution of any single key is difficult to isolate" and answers it only by coarsening to blocks [Section 4.2]. Dropping the eviction term (token log-probs only, with replay masks) gives 35.7% vs 49.6% on Countdown; ignoring eviction off-policyness gives 2.5% [Section 5.2.2].

### Goal: What scale and trace length does it cover, and does it keep the base model frozen?
It does not keep the model frozen: the eviction scores are the model's own Q/K, so the eviction gradient flows into every parameter [Section 4.4]. Scale is one 1.5B model; training traces are short: max completion 1024 tokens with cadence 256 on Countdown, and max 1050 tokens with cadence 350 on DAPO-17k, evaluated up to 3850 tokens [Sections 5.2, 5.4]. A staircase curriculum on the retention rate is needed for stability [Section 4.6]. Baselines are applied to a model trained without eviction, not to an equally RL-trained model [Section 5.1].
