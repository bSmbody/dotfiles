# Not All Thoughts Need HBM: Semantics-Aware Memory Hierarchy for LLM Reasoning

## What this paper does
Reports a "cliff" for reasoning-model KV eviction: with streaming eviction, accuracy collapses from 22% at 70% budget to 0% at 50%, for both attention-based and random selection. Proposes a four-tier hierarchy (HBM, CPU DDR at full precision, compressed, evicted) driven by cumulative attention, where DDR tokens are prefetched before attention so they contribute exactly. Central claim: accuracy depends on the permanently evicted fraction, not on the HBM fraction. Evaluated with DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen 7B-32B on GSM8K, MATH-500 and others.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What signal does it use to decide which KV entries to keep, evict, or recall, and is the decision reversible?
Cumulative attention summed over steps, averaged over layers and heads [full text, Section 3.1, Eq 1]; tiers re-assigned every 64 steps; T1 (DDR) tokens are prefetched back at full precision before each attention step, T3 tokens are permanently removed [Sections 3.2, 3.4]. Prompt, 4 sinks and the 128 most recent tokens are protected.

### Goal: What does it report as its failure mode or limitation for tokens that are needed later (long chain-of-thought or long context)?
Reasoning chains establish variables and partial results "referenced hundreds of tokens later—unpredictably and non-monotonically"; evicting one "is like deleting a variable from a running program" [Section 2.3]. Even 3% permanent eviction keeps 91% of full accuracy on GSM8K but only 71% on MATH-500 and 33% on MATH Level-5 [Section 6]. The compressed tier is "practically constrained": 8-bit quantization drops accuracy from 58% to 20% [Section 3.2, Section 6].

### Goal: What method assumption does its scoring or training rely on (per-token additive utility, per-head independence, teacher-forced or offline training)?
The hierarchy is "a placement layer, not a scoring method": any scorer can be plugged in [Section 6]. Accuracy preservation comes from keeping full-precision copies in CPU memory, so total footprint is not reduced; single model family; scoring is per-token cumulative attention.
