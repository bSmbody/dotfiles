# PIVOT: Efficient Query-Group Indexing for Token-Level Sparse Attention

## What this paper does
(Abstract only, not body-read.) Training-free replacement for the DeepSeek Sparse Attention indexer: aggregates a group of nearby queries into one proxy query, does one shared full-prefix scan to get a candidate set, then either shares the proxy top-k across the group or re-scores per query. Evaluated on DeepSeek-V3.2 and GLM-5.1.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Is sharing one sparse read set across a group of queries already done?
Yes for deterministic top-k: PIVOT shares the candidate scan (and optionally the top-k) across queries in a group, exploiting their high overlap [abstract]. It is about query positions (prefill groups / MTP tokens), not stochastic sampling across heads; it is the closest "share the sparse read across queries" prior, not a coupling of random samples.
