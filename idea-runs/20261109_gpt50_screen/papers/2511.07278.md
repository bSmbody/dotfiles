# StreamKV: Streaming Video Question-Answering with Segment-based KV Cache Retrieval and Compression

## What this paper does
Training-free framework for streaming Video-LLMs: partitions the stream into semantic segments by adjacent-frame cosine-similarity boundaries, encodes segment by segment with a sliding window of past KV plus a per-segment mean summary vector, compresses each segment's KV via a guidance prompt, and at question time retrieves query-relevant KV blocks per layer using representative keys and the mean question query.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What does a strong training-free streaming video KV memory already provide that C6 would have to beat?
Segment-level summaries and segment-level retrieval of full-fidelity KV blocks, training-free [full text, Method: Semantic Segment Partitioning and Encoding; Streaming Video Question-Answering, Eq 11-12]. Segment summaries are cheap (mean of frame features) and the expensive part is the retrieved KV itself; reading a summary of an arbitrary interval is not the bottleneck here. Any C6 efficiency claim must beat this at equal memory and equal answer tokens.
