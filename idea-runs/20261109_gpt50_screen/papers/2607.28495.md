# Stage-Replay Divergence Follows the KV Cache: Fixed-Prefix Precision Controls and Bidirectional Cache Transplantation

## What this paper does
Audits whether continuing from a freshly prefilled token prefix reproduces continuation from the live decode-time KV cache, on a Qwen2.5-14B-derived reasoning model over 200 GPQA items. In BF16 the two cache constructions differ only at rounding level, yet most greedy continuations diverge; FP32 removes decoded disagreement. Swapping the whole K/V cache between the two constructions makes every divergent continuation follow its cache donor, so the cache is a causally sufficient carrier of the divergent trajectory.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How small a perturbation of the KV cache suffices to change a greedy continuation, how soon does it diverge, and is the cache the cause?
The live and prefill caches are "non-bit-exact at the boundary on every item, although their immediate greedy argmax always agrees". Continuations (capped at 768/512/64 branch/merge/answer tokens) then diverge on 166/200 suffixes (83%), 49/200 answers and 20/200 correctness labels; "the first suffix mismatch occurs a median of 88 tokens after the boundary (range 4-453)", while accuracy differs by only one point [full text, Section 3.3; Section 4.1]. Bit-exact replicas never diverge, so the effect is the cache difference, not run noise [Section 4.1]. Transplanting all 48 layers of K/V makes the recipient follow the donor's suffix on 24/24 divergent rows (43/43 on a later checkpoint) [Section 4.4, Table 5; Section 4.5]. FP32 gives no decoded disagreement on these short suffixes [Section 4.1].
