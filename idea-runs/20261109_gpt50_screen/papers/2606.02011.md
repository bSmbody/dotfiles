# Extreme Low-Bit Inference in Reasoning Models: Failure Modes and Targeted Recovery

## What this paper does
Studies GPTQ weight-only W2A16 (and W4A16) on Qwen3-8B/32B over AIME2026, GPQA-D, MATH-500, GSM8K and commonsense tasks at a 32K budget, temperature 0.6. It defines trace-level metrics (reasoning length, hit-limit rate, think-closed rate, time-to-first-answer, n-gram loop rate), shows 2-bit failures are process failures (path-finding vs commitment), builds a four-regime taxonomy, and proposes FP16 planning plus "loop rescue" (commit to an earlier answer or rerun in FP16 on loop detection). An appendix measures FP8/NVFP4 KV batch capacity on B200.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Which trace-level metrics are defined, and how strongly do they track accuracy loss?
Reasoning length, hit-limit rate, think-closed rate, TTFA (token position of first parseable answer), loop rate (repeated local n-grams), plus a normalized commit gap (fraction of trace after first parseable answer) [full text, Sections 4.1, 5]. Correlation of Δaccuracy with Δmetric from FP16→2-bit: loop rate r=−0.85 (ρ=−0.88), hit-max −0.83 (−0.89), length −0.73, think-closed +0.73 (+0.90), TTFA +0.70 [full text, Table 1].

### Goal: How does the generation budget change conclusions?
At 4K/8K budgets loops are truncated, hiding cost; at 32K the 2-bit model's accuracy stays flat while length and commit gap grow sharply, whereas FP16 converts budget to accuracy. The authors argue short-budget (≈8K) evaluations "systematically overestimate the end-to-end benefit of low-bit reasoning" and use 32K [full text, Section 5, Fig. 4].

### Goal: What does loop rescue achieve, and is it KV-side?
On n-gram loop detection: if a parseable answer exists, return it; else rerun from scratch in FP16. In the process-degraded regime it cuts mean/median length by 92.2%/93.7% and gives +5.24 accuracy points over raw 2-bit, turning a net slowdown into a speedup; adding an FP16 plan gives +8.80 but less speed [full text, Sections 7–8, Tables 3–4]. It is weight-only; KV is not quantized in the main study [full text, Limitations].

### Goal: What KV-precision serving numbers are reported?
B200, 32K max generation: max batch for Qwen3-32B FP16 weights is 9/19/39 for FP16/FP8/NVFP4 KV; Qwen3-32B W4A16 15/30/61; Qwen3-8B W4A16 29/59/119. Throughput at fixed batch 8 changes little (e.g., 8B: 1312/1634/1639 tok/s), so the gain is capacity, not per-step speed. NVFP4 quality loss concentrates on AIME and GPQA-D [full text, Appendix C, Tables 7–8].
