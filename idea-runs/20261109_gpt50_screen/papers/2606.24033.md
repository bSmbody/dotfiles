# Block-GTQ: RoPE-Block Rate Allocation for KV-Cache Quantization

## What this paper does
Block-GTQ allocates key-cache precision across the 2-D RoPE frequency blocks of each (layer, KV head), statically from calibration. A blockwise logit-error bound |delta K| <= sum_i ||q^(i)|| ||e_k^(i)|| motivates a label-free score s = 1/2(E||q^(i)||^2 + E||k^(i)||^2) (averaged over the GQA query heads), combined with a codec rate law (TQ-MSE 4^-b) and solved exactly by largest-marginal-gain integer allocation. Values are quantized uniformly. Packed attention over equal-rate block groups without materializing fp16 KV.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it allocate K and V jointly or depend on attention concentration?
No. Separate average K and V rates are inputs; only key blocks get mixed precision and values are uniform; "Joint optimization of the K/V budget split and value-side rate allocation remains unexplored" [full text, Limitations]. Allocation is static per (layer, KV head) from Q/K energy, with no attention-distribution statistic [Sec 3.2 Eq 4].

### Goal: What does it show on long-chain reasoning?
On DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B at K3V2 without fp16 sink/recent buffers: AIME 2024/2025 avg@8 49.6/38.8 vs fp16 54.2/37.9, while uniform TQ-MSE scores 0 on both [abstract; Sec 5.6 Table 5]. At K2V2 on Llama-3.1-8B NIAH 6-task average 70.6 -> 97.4 [abstract]. So within-head key allocation alone rescues 3-bit-key reasoning; generation length is not the focus.

### Goal: Serving evidence?
Packed serving only on Qwen2.5-3B at batch 1 on one H800 (3.24x lower resident KV) [Limitations, abstract]; batched serving is future work.

# Block-GTQ: RoPE-Block Rate Allocation for KV-Cache Quantization

## What this paper does
Block-GTQ allocates cached-key precision across RoPE two-dimensional frequency blocks. A blockwise logit-error bound motivates a label-free query-key energy score, which is combined with codec-dependent rate penalties. Largest-marginal-gain allocation exactly minimizes the separable integer proxy under equal-cost budgets and diminishing returns. It is instantiated with TurboQuant-MSE, grouping equal-rate blocks so attention runs over packed caches without materializing fp16 K/V. Values are quantized uniformly.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Headline numbers?
K-only diagnostics: layer-averaged logit MAE falls 32-80% versus uniform TQ-MSE, and all 367 layers improve at 2 and 3 average key bits. At K2V2 on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, six-task NIAH rises from 70.6 to 97.4 and LongBench-EN from 33.7 to 44.9. On DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B at K3V2, with no fp16 sink or recent buffers, AIME24/25 avg@8 is 49.6/38.8 vs fp16 54.2/37.9, while uniform TQ-MSE scores 0 on both (Abstract, Section 5).
### Goal: Relevance?
This is static (calibration-free score, fixed per block) precision allocation inside the key vector, a RoPE-frequency analogue of PN-Mix's channel-pair allocation. It is another data point that uniform TurboQuant collapses on long reasoning without boundary/sink protection.
### Goal: Limitations?
The K/V budget split and value-side allocation are not optimized. Packed serving was evaluated only on Qwen2.5-3B at batch 1 on one H800 (Limitations).
