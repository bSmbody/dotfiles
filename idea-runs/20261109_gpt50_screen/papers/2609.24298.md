# KV-COBRA: KV Cache Compression via Co-Optimized Bit-Rank Allocation

## What this paper does
KV-COBRA co-optimizes, per (layer, head), the retained SVD rank r and bit width b (Hadamard-rotated scalar quantization of the projected coefficients) under a global byte budget: inner per-head enumeration of (r, b=B/r), outer damped redistribution of budgets across heads. An attention-KL variant reweights SVD directions by sigma_Q^2 sigma_K^2. Joint K+V extension. Calibration once on 32 WikiText-2 samples. Evaluated on perplexity, zero-shot and LongBench from 0.5 to 4 bits per dimension.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does its attention-KL bound treat the attention distribution (relevant to seed 1's K-side Var_p argument)?
Prop 4.1: E[KL(p||p~)] <= 1/2 E[Delta s^T F(p) Delta s] (Taylor, F(p)=diag(p)-pp^T) <= 1/4 sum_t Var[Delta s_t] (using F(p) <= 1/2 I) = C sum_i sigma_Q,i^2 sigma_K,i^2 q(b) [full text, Sec 4 Eq 6]. So it writes the exact second-order Fisher form that seed 1 starts from, then bounds away the dependence on p, so its weights carry no head-level concentration effect. Seed 1's claim lives exactly in the gap between steps (a) and (b).

### Goal: Does it find K/V budget asymmetry, and is allocation online?
V-heavy splits dominate the joint K+V sweep, attributed to V's flatter spectrum [Sec 7, Tables 4-5] -- the opposite of KIVI-style "keys need more". Allocation is computed once offline; "refresh-during-generation is not studied" [Sec 7 Limitations]. KL helps over L2 mainly below 1 bpd; at >= 2 bpd they agree within noise [Sec 7]. No wall-clock end-to-end latency and no long-CoT evaluation.

### Goal: Is the allocator or the proxy the bottleneck?
The proxy: an exact DP solver lowers summed surrogate distortion 12-16% but worsens PPL by about 0.5; replacing the Bennett estimate with measured per-head distortion fixes it [Sec 7]. Consistent with RateQuant's distortion-model-mismatch finding.
