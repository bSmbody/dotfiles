# No Token Left Behind: Reliable KV Cache Compression via Importance-Aware Mixed Precision Quantization

## What this paper does
(Light-mode digest, from arXiv biblio/abstract only; body not read.) MiKV: instead of evicting unimportant KV pairs, keep them in low precision, and keep important KV pairs in high precision. Shows eviction causes hallucination/context loss and that even low-precision retention recovers much of it.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Is per-token mixed precision (some tokens high precision, rest low) already established?
Yes. MiKV is the canonical per-token importance-based mixed-precision KV scheme; "keep literal tokens in high precision" is the same mechanism with a token-type importance rule instead of an attention-based one.
