# Top-K Off-Policy Correction for a REINFORCE Recommender System

## What this paper does
Trains YouTube's candidate generator as a softmax REINFORCE policy over millions of items, learning only from logged feedback: reward is observed only for items a (past) policy actually showed. It corrects the mismatch between the logging policy and the learned policy by importance weighting, adds a top-K correction because a page shows K items (the objective becomes the probability that an item enters the set), controls variance with weight capping / normalized IS / TRPO, and uses Boltzmann exploration (top K' deterministic plus K-K' sampled items) so unshown items get feedback. Live experiments show gains from exploration data and from the top-K correction.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What is the core mechanism of the top-K off-policy correction, and why is it needed?
Feedback exists only for actions the behavior policy beta chose, so a naive policy gradient is biased; the first-order corrected gradient weights each logged action by pi(a|s)/beta(a|s) [full text, Section 4 / Eq 3-4]. Because a set of K items is shown, the policy is rewritten in terms of the inclusion probability alpha(a|s) = 1-(1-pi(a|s))^K, which adds the multiplier lambda_K = K(1-pi)^(K-1) [Section 4.3 / Eq 7-8]. As pi -> 0 the update is scaled up by K (a desirable item with little mass is pushed hard); as pi -> 1 the multiplier goes to 0, so once an item is reliably in the top-K its gradient stops and other items can take mass. The standard correction converges to a top-1-optimal policy, the top-K one gives better top-K sets [Section 4.3, 6.1.2]. Assumptions: at most one rewarded item per set and item returns independent of the other items in the set [Section 4.3].

### Goal: How does it get feedback on actions the deterministic policy never shows, and what keeps that safe and low-variance?
"One of the main biases in learning from logged data is that the model does not observe feedback of actions not chosen", and exploratory data alleviates it [Section 6.2.1]. Exploration is Boltzmann: compute the top M items with approximate nearest neighbours, renormalize their logits, return the top K' deterministically and sample K-K' from the remaining M-K' [Section 5]. Training with 5% exploratory traffic gave a significant +0.07% ViewTime [Section 6.2.1]. Large importance weights arise when pi >> beta; weight capping min(pi/beta, c) gave the largest online gain, and lifting the cap from e^3 to e^5 dropped ViewTime 0.52% (propensity overfitting) [Section 4.4 / Eq 9, Section 6.2.4].
