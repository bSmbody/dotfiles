# Rough Transformers: Lightweight and Continuous Time Series Modelling through Signature Patching

## What this paper does
NeurIPS 2024. Replaces a long time-series input with a short sequence of multi-view signature tokens: for each coarse time point t_k, the global signature S_{0,t_k} and the local window signature S_{t_{k-1},t_k}, truncated at level n, linearly projected to Q/K/V and fed to standard attention. Gains: 1.4x-26x faster training than vanilla attention, robustness to irregular sampling, sequences up to 250K points.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Has someone already used prefix/interval signatures as compressed tokens for a Transformer over a long sequence?
Yes, for time series. Eq 8 defines M(X)_k=(S_{0,t_k}, S_{t_{k-1},t_k}); Eq 9-10 feed these as attention tokens [full text, Section 3]. This is the closest ML prior to C6's "cache block-boundary signatures and read interval signatures" idea; C6's difference is the domain (projected visual features of video, consumed by a pretrained VLM) and arbitrary query intervals rather than fixed windows.
### Goal: What does the paper say about signature dimension?
|S^{<=n}| = d(d^n-1)/(d-1) = O(d^n); they note this is acceptable because in their time-series tasks d is small and n can be small due to factorial decay [full text, Section 3.1, "Spatial Processing"]. Their channels d are raw sensor channels (small), not compressed projections of thousand-dimensional visual features. The model is trained end to end; signatures are not fed to a frozen pretrained LM.
