# SPIN: Shadow Predictive Indexer for Sparse Attention

## What this paper does
Training-free method that reduces the cost of the DSA lightning indexer during decode by not scoring the whole KV cache every step. Per request and per layer, it keeps an EMA of previous iterations' indexer scores pooled to KV blocks (64 compressed indexer slots), scores only the predicted-important blocks plus forced blocks, and reserves a small fraction of the block budget for random exploration of skipped blocks. Implemented for DeepSeek-V4 (Flash and Pro) in vLLM, including with native MTP speculative decoding.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Is the indexer the dominant decode cost of DSA-family models, and which released models use DSA?
"DSA and its variants have seen significant adoption among frontier open-source models, including DeepSeek-V4, GLM-5.2, MiniMax-M3, and LongCat-2.0. Core attention processes a fixed top-K budget, whereas the indexer scales linearly with context length, making it the dominant bottleneck in long-context inference"; V4's 4x compression "does not change the indexer's linear scaling", so at 512K-1M "the indexer remains a major contributor to DeepSeek-V4's decode inter-token latency" [full text, Section 1]. K in practice is 512-2048 [Section 2]. No explicit percentage breakdown of ITL is given.

### Goal: What does skipping indexer work buy end to end and at what quality cost?
DeepSeek-V4-Flash on 8xB300 (TP8/EP8), 507K cached context + 5K new input, batch 96, 16K output: all-keep 4720 TPS / 17.84 ms ITL; 40% block sparsity +10.6% TPS / -9.2% ITL; 50% +14.9% / -13.2% [Section 4.4, Table 6]. Quality: AA-LCR within 2.4 points across 20-50% sparsity; RULER 90.20 -> 88.96 at 50% even though p99 top-K miss reaches 48.83% [Section 4.2, Table 3]. Prev-iteration scores predict better than prev-layer scores (the basis of IndexCache) [Section 1, Section 2].

### Goal: What limitations do the authors state?
History-based prediction "has limited ability to anticipate patterns not represented in its history, such as sudden attention shifts"; skipped blocks are unobserved, which "may create self-reinforcing prediction errors"; exploration "does not guarantee timely discovery or full task-score recovery"; evaluated only on DeepSeek-V4 with native MTP, not on GLM-5, MiniMax-M3 or LongCat-2.0 [Section 6].
