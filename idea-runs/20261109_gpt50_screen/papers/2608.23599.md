# Squeezing the Cache, Preserving the Truth: Monotonic Equipotential Allocation with Geodesia-KV

## What this paper does
Geodesia-KV is a training-free KV policy family that (i) assigns each 64-token block a level on a monotone precision ladder {16,8,4,2,1(centroid)} from causal EMA attention mass aggregated over the GQA group, via a Lagrangian/analytic bit-depth rule; (ii) keeps a small fraction of high "attention x value-isolation" tokens exact; (iii) a compressed-Quest branch that keeps exact per-block min/max key boxes for page ranking over a graded cache, separating resident bits from read bits. Integrated as a vLLM KV cache manager; evaluated with causal incremental perplexity (WikiText-2, PG-19) on Qwen2.5-3B, Qwen3-8B and others.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Is precision promotion supported, or only demotion?
Only demotion. Once a block leaves the recent window its precision is immutable; demotion 16->4 is allowed but "there is no promotion back because the initial dense data does not exist anymore"; monotonicity avoids keeping a dense fallback copy [full text, Sec 3.2]. So it is the opposite design point from seed 4 (keep a residual elsewhere and promote on read).

### Goal: How is per-block precision decided; does it use attention concentration or only mass?
Mass only: m_b is mean attention mass over observed queries (EMA 0.9/0.1 during decode), and b* = ceil(log2(c ||q|| e_b(L) / (2 eps_t) + 1)) with eps_t = tau/(N_b m_b), i.e. bits grow with log of mass x range [Sec 3.1, 3.3 Eq 4]. Same knob for K and V; no K/V asymmetry and no concentration (sum p^2) statistic. An attention-output error decomposition o - o_hat = sum a_hat (v - v_hat) + sum (a - a_hat) v and a block certificate are given [Sec 3.6 Eq 8].

### Goal: What evidence and what limits?
Metrics are perplexity and attention fidelity, not reasoning accuracy: 5-bit point beats KIVI-4 perplexity on Qwen at 16k; compressed-Quest lowers resident 9.83 vs 16.25 and read 1.95 vs 2.32 bits/value on PG-19; 1M-token generation on a 16 GiB GPU [abstract, Sec 5]. No long-CoT accuracy or length measurements.
