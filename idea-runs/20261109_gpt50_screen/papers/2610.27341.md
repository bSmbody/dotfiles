# Look Before You Reason (LoRe): Implicit Visual Thinking for Efficient Multimodal Reasoning

## What this paper does
NeurIPS 2026. Argues multi-turn thinking-with-images (zoom/crop tool calls) causes redundant token computation and latency. Instead, in one turn the VLM emits a <box> region; LoRe maps the box to the already-cached visual-token positions and scales their value-cache entries by gamma>=1 in every layer (keys/positions unchanged), so later decoding emphasises that evidence with no crop and no extra visual tokens. RL (answer, format, localization rewards) trains the box prediction. Introduces COCO-ObjQA.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does LoRe identify re-look token redundancy as an efficiency problem, and what does it do instead of adding crop tokens?
Yes: thinking-with-images "multi-turn interactions with VLMs introduce redundant token computation and slow inference" [full text, Abstract, Sec 1]. It adds no new visual tokens: v'_j = gamma v_j for j in the box's cached positions, o' = o + (gamma-1) sum alpha_j v_j [full text, Sec 3.1].
### Goal: Does it recover detail beyond the original encoding?
No, explicitly: "This is evidence amplification, not a recovery of details lost during vision encoding" [full text, Sec 3.1]. So LoRe is the zero-new-token end of the design space; C5 sits between LoRe (0 extra tokens, no new detail) and full crop re-encoding (all new tokens).
