# ChampionKV: Head-Wise Adaptive Budget Allocation for KV Cache Compression

## What this paper does
ChampionKV is a prefill-time, input-adaptive head-wise TOKEN budget allocator for observation-window eviction (SnapKV/PyramidKV). It uses the maximum aggregated observation-window attention score a_max per head as a concentration proxy: dispersed heads (small a_max) get larger budgets via inverse-max weights, with a 20% floor budget and an abnormal-head mask (a_max < 0.01 gets only floor). Evaluated on LongBench and RULER with Llama3.1-8B/70B, Mistral-7B and similar.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it link head attention concentration to how much compression a head tolerates?
Yes, but for token eviction, not precision. Argument: under Ada-KV's eviction bound, loss is minimized by maximizing retained attention mass; a concentrated head covers its mass with few entries, a dispersed head needs more, hence budget ∝ 1/a_max [full text, Sec 3.1-3.2, Sec 4]. a_max is used as a ranking proxy for the standard deviation of the score vector, sigma ∝ a_max / sqrt(L-W) [Sec 3.2]. This is the eviction analogue of seed 1's K-side direction (dispersed heads need more), but it says nothing about value precision and does not separate K from V.

### Goal: Is it online during decode and how strong are results?
Prefill-only, plugged into SnapKV/PyramidKV token selection; no decode-time update [Sec 4]. Gains are largest and statistically significant on Llama3.1-70B under tight budgets; on 7B/8B results are "competitive but mixed", and it does not uniformly beat Ada-KV [abstract, Sec 5.2]. No reasoning/long-CoT evaluation.
