# Quantized Reasoning Models Think They Need to Think Longer, but They Do Not

## What this paper does
Evaluates PTQ (GPTQ/AWQ 3/4-bit weight-only, and FlatQuant W4A4KV4 / W8A8KV8, which also quantizes the KV cache) on R1-Distill-Qwen 1.5B/7B/14B, R1-Distill-Llama-8B and QwQ-32B over GSM8K, MATH-500, AIME-120, LiveCodeBench, GPQA-D (T=0.6, top-p 0.95). Shows accuracy loss co-occurs with longer CoT, that many quantized failures are "overthinking" (correct intermediate answer not committed), that BF16–quantized KL concentrates on high-entropy positions and hesitation tokens, and proposes a logit penalty on 50 overthinking markers.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How large is CoT inflation, and is there any evidence for KV-cache quantization specifically?
R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B MATH-500: AWQ-3bit 85.6%→47.0% accuracy with CoT 5.2K→23.4K (4.5×); GPTQ-3bit 71.6% at 6.8K (+32%); FlatQuant W4A4KV4 (weights+activations+KV at 4 bit) 66.4% at 8.0K tokens. LCB: AWQ-3bit 19.1K→38.8K tokens [full text, Section 4.1, Fig. 2]. Spearman ρ=−0.73 between accuracy drop and CoT increase across 28 model–quant pairs [full text, Section 4.1]. KV is never isolated: the only KV-quantized setting is joint W4A4KV4, so KV's own contribution to inflation is not separated [full text, Section 3, Appendix A.2].

### Goal: What is the failure mechanism?
Errors categorized (GPT-5 judge, >95% agreement): overthinking errors rise from 19/72 (BF16) to 139/265 (52%) under AWQ-3bit, 39 under GPTQ-3bit, 64 under FlatQuant [full text, Section 4.2]. Token KL between BF16 and quantized next-token distributions (same prefixes) is highest on "Wait", "But", "Alternatively"; position KL correlates with BF16 next-token entropy (ρ=0.92); hesitation/maths token ratio flips 0.57→1.15 [full text, Section 4.3, Fig. 4; Section 6].

### Goal: What mitigation is proposed and how much does it recover?
Subtract λ from logits of 50 curated overthinking markers. On Qwen-1.5B it reduces CoT by 12–23% for every λ in 0.5–4.0 while maintaining or slightly improving accuracy; across five models CoT −4.1% to −28.0%; AWQ-3bit Qwen-1.5B +6.2 accuracy, −28% CoT; overthinking errors 139→58; W4A4KV4 overthinking errors 64→34. Boosting markers (negative λ) inflates CoT by up to 445% [full text, Section 5.2, Table 1, Fig. 9]. The penalty also shortens BF16 CoT by 5.6–10%, so quantization amplifies an existing tendency [full text, Section 5.3].
