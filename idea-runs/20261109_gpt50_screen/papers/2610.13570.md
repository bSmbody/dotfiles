# Constructing Pareto Frontiers of Optimal Reasoning Termination Methods for Overthinking in Reasoning Models

## What this paper does
The paper introduces Step-wise Truncation Probing (STP). For each fixed sampled reasoning trajectory, it truncates at many step boundaries and repeatedly generates answers from each prefix, which yields a token-accuracy curve per trajectory. From the curves it defines the Overthinking Ratio (OR: share of thinking tokens produced after accuracy first peaks) and Accuracy Degradation (AD). It then builds oracle Pareto frontiers for three termination strategies (dynamic routing, fixed budget, dynamic early exit) and releases OTBench (70.5B reasoning tokens) to score public methods against those frontiers.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How large is the overthinking headroom at long generation lengths?
Setup: six models (GPT-OSS-20B, Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507, Qwen3-30B-A3B-Thinking-2507, R1-Distill-Qwen-7B/14B/32B). Benchmarks: AIME24-26, MATH500, GPQA-D, LiveCodeBench; 963 questions with 16 trajectories each. Thinking limit 32,768 tokens [App A.1]. Result: 64.3% of thinking tokens are generated after the highest measured accuracy is first reached, and full thinking costs 9.03 points of accuracy relative to that reference point [Section 1, findings]. Frontier ordering: routing ≤ fixed budget ≤ dynamic early exit [Section 1, Section 4.2].

### Goal: How close do current public methods get?
ThinkSwitcher (routing), Predictive Scheduling (budget) and ThinkBrake (early exit) all sit below their strategy frontiers. That leaves "substantial unrealized space" [Section 5.2, Fig 4].

### Goal: Stated limitations?
[App E]: the frontiers are offline oracles, not deployable policies. STP depends on probe resolution and step boundaries, and its probing cost is not counted in the token metric. Coverage is a fixed set of models and tasks, with one adapted method per strategy. The authors call for finer-grained, trajectory-aware termination that accounts for deployment overhead. Token counts are the only cost measure; KV memory and latency are not measured.
