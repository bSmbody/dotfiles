# A Systematic Analysis of Hybrid Linear Attention

## What this paper does
Trains 72 models (340M/20B tokens and 1.3B/100B tokens) covering six linear-attention variants across five linear:full ratios (24:1 to 3:1 plus pure), with fixed block-wise interleaving, and evaluates language modeling and RULER recall at 4K context.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does the linear-to-full ratio affect recall vs language modeling, and how is the ratio chosen?
LM score is flat across ratios (0.55-0.57); RULER recall rises steadily with more full-attention layers and saturates around 3:1 [full text, Section 4.2, Figure 2]. Ratios are a fixed r:1 repeating block chosen by sweep, not learned [Appendix D]. Standalone linear quality does not predict hybrid quality (GDN best alone, HGRN-2 best hybrid at 340M) [Section 4.1]. Recommends gated/delta-rule mixers at 3:1-6:1 [Section 4.4].

### Goal: What limitation does it state?
Up to 1.3B, 4,096-token context, block-wise ratios only; head-wise or dynamic routing and automated architecture search are left open [full text, Limitations].
