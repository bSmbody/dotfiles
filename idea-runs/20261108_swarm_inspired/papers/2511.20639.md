# Latent Collaboration in Multi-Agent Systems (LatentMAS)

## What this paper does
A training-free multi-agent framework in which LLM agents (Qwen3 4B/8B/14B, Llama3) think and communicate in latent space. Each agent feeds its last-layer hidden state back as the next input embedding for m latent steps, after a linear realignment W_a ≈ W_out^+ W_in that maps hidden states onto the input-embedding space. Agents pass "latent working memory", their full layer-wise KV caches including the latent steps, to the next agent by KV concatenation. The paper tests sequential (planner -> critic -> refiner -> solver) and hierarchical (domain experts -> summariser) designs on 9 benchmarks, including AIME24/25 and GPQA-Diamond. ICML 2026 spotlight.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: How are latent thoughts generated and handed to another agent, and is it training-free?
Latent step: h_t (last layer) -> e = h_t W_a -> appended as the next input, repeated m times [full text, Section 3.1, Eq 3]. W_a is computed once per run (ridge-regularised pseudo-inverse) and reused [Section 3.1; Appendix A.2]. Transfer: after its m steps, agent A1's KV caches from all layers (input context plus latent thoughts) are prepended to the next agent's layers. The implementation concatenates KV through HuggingFace past_key_values, with a vLLM backend and prefix caching also supported [Section 3.2, Eq 4; Section 4 Implementation]. Nothing is trained.

### Goal: Does it help on long-CoT math (AIME) with Qwen3, and at what token cost?
Qwen3-8B, mean of 3 runs, T=0.6, 20K max tokens [Section 4 Implementation; Table 2]. AIME24: single model 50.0, TextMAS 53.3, LatentMAS 56.7 (sequential) / 53.3 (hierarchical). AIME25: single 46.7, TextMAS 53.3/50.0, LatentMAS 53.3/50.0. Output tokens fall from 38-54K (TextMAS) to 7.5-13K, close to or below the single model's 12.9-14.7K. Speed is 3.8-5.7x faster than TextMAS. Qwen3-14B AIME24 hierarchical: 63.3 / 70.0 / 73.3 [Table 2]. Averaged over all 9 tasks, the gains are +14.6% (sequential) and +13.3% (hierarchical) over the single model, and +2.8% / +4.6% over TextMAS [Section 4.1]. On AIME (30 problems) the gains over TextMAS are 0-1 problems, which is within noise.

### Goal: How many latent steps are useful, and what limits are visible?
Accuracy rises with latent steps and "peaks around 40-80 steps"; beyond that it plateaus or declines, which the authors read as redundant information [full text, Section 5, Fig 8]. Latent hidden states deviate strongly from input-embedding statistics, and W_a pulls them back toward the token-embedding distribution [Section 5, Fig 6]. The agents in the paper run before one final answer is decoded. Latent agents are not interleaved with a long text CoT as it is being written, and no agent count beyond a handful is tested [Sections 2, 4].
