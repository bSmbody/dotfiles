# MAC-Attention: a Match-Amend-Complete Scheme for Fast and Accurate Attention Computation

## What this paper does
MLSys 2026. A training-free decode-time scheme that reuses the attention summary (S, Z) that an earlier, similar query computed over the long prefix. It merges that stale summary with a fresh attention over a short recent band plus tail, using the log-sum-exp merge. Matching is pre-RoPE L2 nearest neighbour over a ring of the last K queries per head (threshold tau). "Amend" recomputes a band of r tokens near the match boundary under the fresh query, because that band holds most of the softmax mass. On a miss it falls back to full attention. Evaluated on LongBench v2 (120K), RULER (120K) and LongGenBench (16K generation) with LLaMA-3.1 and Qwen3 MoE in SGLang/FlashInfer.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it already merge a far contribution computed under an earlier query with a fresh near read (C10's core operation)?
Yes. o_m is approximated by (S^{(p)}_{1:p-r} + S^{(m)}_{p-r+1:m}) / (Z^{(p)}_{1:p-r} + Z^{(m)}_{p-r+1:m}). The prefix logits use q_p, while the band and tail use q_m [full text, Section 3.1 and Section 3.3, Eq 4; Section 3.4.2, Eq 7]. The summaries chain: after a hit, the stored summary for m is the reused prefix of p plus fresh terms, with the band removed by online subtraction [Section 3.1, Fig 2c; Section 4.2, K3]. The far prefix can therefore carry logits from queries many steps back. RoPE is handled by matching pre-RoPE and recomputing the high-mass band [Section 3.2.1].
### Goal: How often does reuse succeed and how much KV traffic is skipped; does fidelity hold on long generation?
In long contexts, the hit rate within the K window is >99% [Section 7]. On Qwen3-30B-A3B (tau 0.45, K 512-4096, r 256) the hit rate is 99.5-99.7%, 98.6-99.0% of the prefix is skipped, and accuracy is 36.6-37.6 vs 37.0 for full attention [Section 5.5, Table 4]. Headline: up to 99% fewer KV accesses, >60% lower per-token latency at 128K, >=14.3x attention-phase and up to 2.6x end-to-end speedup, "while maintaining full-attention quality" [Abstract, Section 1]. Settings: K=1024, r=256; tau=0.45 for context-heavy tasks and 0.75 for generation-heavy tasks [Section 5.2].
### Goal: How does error depend on the reuse gap (drift)?
The normalized output error falls quickly with band width: r of about 8 removes most of it, and r >= 256 is "effectively indistinguishable" from full attention. At fixed r, larger reuse gaps are more sensitive and deeper layers need wider bands. Outliers occur when mass extends beyond the band or when query differences act like a uniform logit rescaling [Section 5.6, Fig 4; LLaMA-3.1-8B on LongGenBench]. Error bound: (e^Delta - 1)(1 - rho) E||v||, where rho is the mass captured by the band [Section 3.4.3, Eq 8].
### Goal: Limitations relevant to long-CoT reasoning?
The method has no AIME/GPQA-type reasoning evaluation (grep finds none). Long generation is tested only with LongGenBench 16K. Full KV stays in HBM, plus query and summary rings. A miss falls back to an O(n) read [Section 7].
