# Towards Economical Inference: Enabling DeepSeek's Multi-Head Latent Attention in Any Transformer-based LLMs

## What this paper does
MHA2MLA converts pretrained MHA/GQA LLMs (SmolLM 135M-1.7B, Llama2-7B) into MLA with fine-tuning on 0.3-0.6% of pretraining data. Two components: partial-RoPE (keep RoPE on a few rotary subspaces per head, turn the rest into NoPE) and a low-rank latent for NoPE keys and values initialised by SVD of the pretrained weights (SVDsplit or SVDjoint). Composes with KV quantization; up to 96.87% KV reduction on Llama2-7B.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does the conversion choose the latent subspace and the retained RoPE dimensions, and what objective does it fit?
RoPE subspaces are chosen per head by a fixed rule: S_high, S_low, S_uniform, or S_2-norm = top-r of mean ||q^[2k,2k+1]|| * ||k^[2k,2k+1]|| over calibration sequences (Cauchy-Schwarz upper bound on a subspace's logit contribution) [full text, Section 3.1; Appendix A]. Latent factors come from truncated SVD of the weights [W_k,nope, W_v] (SVDjoint), i.e. weight-space error only, then the model is fine-tuned to recover [full text, Section 3.2]. Both choices are separate from any attention-logit or long-distance criterion.
### Goal: What does it report about long-context fidelity and limitations?
LongBench on Llama2-7B (4K window, fine-tuning seqlen 2048-4096): 27.4 -> 26.7 (d_kv=64) -> 25.1 (d_kv=16) [full text, Section 4.2, Table 2; Appendix C]. S_low loses most, showing high-frequency subspaces matter [full text, Section 4.3]. Limitation: models like Llama3 "require fine-tuning on a 128K context length to mitigate performance degradation", which they could not afford [full text, Limitations].
