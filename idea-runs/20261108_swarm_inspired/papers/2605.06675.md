# RateQuant: Optimal Mixed-Precision KV Cache Quantization via Rate-Distortion Theory

## What this paper does
RateQuant allocates bit-widths per (layer, KV head) and separately for K and V, offline, by reverse water-filling. Per-head sensitivity w_i is the mean squared gradient of LM loss w.r.t. K or V of that head on 16x512-token calibration text; per-quantizer distortion D(b)=alpha*beta^-b is fitted from MSE at 2-6 bits; integer bits come from greedy max-marginal-gain (optimal for convex D). Static 2 KB lookup table at inference. Evaluated with WikiText-2 perplexity and lm-eval commonsense tasks on Qwen3 and Llama3 at 2.5-4 bits, base quantizers TurboQuant, KIVI, QuaRot.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it already give the per-head, K/V-separate water-filling closed form that seed 1 asks GPT to derive?
Yes. Theorem 2: b_i* = b_bar + (ln w_i - mean ln w)/ln beta (continuous, inactive bounds) [full text, Sec 3.1 Eq 2]; Theorem 3: uniform/optimal distortion ratio = AM/GM of sensitivities, about 2.0 on Qwen3 [Sec 3.1 Eq 3]; Corollary 4 lognormal case. K/V separation over 2N components with one shared budget; for KIVI at 2.5 bits it yields mean K 2.85 / V 2.15 bits [Sec 3.4]. So "b ∝ log(sensitivity) per head per K/V under total-byte budget" is published; seed 1 must differ in where sensitivity comes from (online attention statistics, direction-opposite K vs V) and in being online.

### Goal: Is the sensitivity online or attention-statistic based?
No. It is offline gradient-squared (diagonal Fisher), argued via a second-order loss expansion (Prop 5); activation-norm proxy is 1.07 PPL worse at 3.5 bits [Sec 3.2]. Dynamic allocation during inference is listed as future work; allocations vary up to 0.5 bits across domains [Sec 5].

### Goal: Failure modes and evaluation scope?
"Distortion model mismatch": using TurboQuant's beta=3.6 on KIVI (beta about 5.1) inverts head ranking and makes KIVI worse, 49.3 -> 87.0 PPL at 2.5 bits; calibrated RateQuant gives 14.9 [Sec 3.4, abstract]. Gains concentrate at 2.5-4 bits; below 2 bits "all heads are severely degraded regardless of allocation" [Sec 4.5]. No long-CoT, AIME, or long-context retrieval evaluation (WikiText-2 + ARC/HellaSwag/PIQA/WinoGrande/MMLU/TruthfulQA) [Sec 4.1].
