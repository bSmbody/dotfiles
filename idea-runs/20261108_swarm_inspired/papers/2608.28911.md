# SemKV: Semantic Mixed-Precision KV Cache Quantization Guided by the Quality Cliff for Long-Context LLM Inference

## What this paper does
SemKV first maps uniform KV quantization on a fractional-bit grid (log2 of integer level counts: 2.0, 2.322, 2.585, 3.0) under a multi-seed statistical protocol and finds a "quality cliff": Llama-3.1-8B is indistinguishable from FP16 down to 2.322 bits and collapses in (2.0, 2.322], with a spike in runaway generation. It then keeps every token, ranks tokens with a model-internal importance indicator, and assigns two adjacent above-cliff precisions to interpolate average precision (e.g. 2.39 bits); a block-exact deferred scheme handles generated tokens. LongBench-style QA, multi-turn, Llama-3.1-8B and Mistral-7B.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does token-importance-aware mixed precision matter, or is its benefit just grid interpolation?
Above the cliff, eight model-internal importance indicators (hidden-state, KV-norm, logit, attention families) are statistically interchangeable; "the benefit of mixing is grid interpolation" [full text, Sec 1 contributions 2; abstract]. Indicator quality matters only in a widened-gap stress test where most tokens get below-cliff precision [contribution 5]. Mixtures crossing the cliff fail to recover full-KV quality [Sec 1].

### Goal: Link between low precision and runaway / length-cap behaviour?
Yes: generation-token quantization at 2.322 bits is indistinguishable from FP16 and even lowers the max-length termination rate (McNemar p<1e-4), while any configuration with a 2.0-bit component degrades and raises the max-length rate (p=0.015 for uniform 2.0) [Sec 4.4 Fig 9]. With TurboQuant-MSE as base the cliff moves down one grid step (to (1.585,2.0] in prefill; (1.0,1.585] for generation) and the no-loss point rises to 7.9x [contribution 5; abstract].

### Goal: Token deletion vs low-precision preservation?
Under matched memory, FP16 pruning collapses (0.065-0.149) while SemKV at 2.39 bits stays at full-KV level (0.438); random pruning behaves like indicator pruning [contribution 4]. Not tested on long-CoT math.
