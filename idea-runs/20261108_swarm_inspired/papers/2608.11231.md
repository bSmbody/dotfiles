# LinearKV: One Cached State Suffices for Position-Independent Caching in Hybrid LLMs

## What this paper does
Training-free PIC for hybrid LLMs (OLMo-Hybrid and Qwen GDN models, Granite Mamba-2). Full-attention layers concatenate cached chunk KV as in transformer PIC; each linear layer initialises its state with a function f of the K cached chunk states. It compares exact composition (HYPIC-style chaining with cached transitions) against simply keeping one cached chunk state ("last block"), with existing selectors (CacheBlend, EPIC, ProphetKV) recomputing ~20% of tokens. Finds the single state ties exact composition on GDN and beats it on Mamba-2.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: On GDN hybrids, does composing several independently computed recurrent states beat keeping just one of them (with attention KV concatenated)?
No. "On the two GDN models the choice of linear-state initializer is immaterial—the paired ex/lb gap is at most 0.013 Avg-F1 and even changes sign across selectors" [full text, Section 4.2]; per-layer relative error of the single block "tracks exact composition on the GDN models—exactness buys nothing there", and which block is kept barely matters, a random one doing as well [Section 3.2, "Single-block initialization"; Appendix A]. With HYPIC's seam selector, OLMo and Qwen stay within 2 pp between the two initializers [Section 4.4]. Benchmarks: LongBench QA/summarization and RULER at 8K-32K (retrieval-heavy, not long-CoT reasoning).
### Goal: Is multi-source state composition ever harmful, and why?
Yes on Mamba-2 (Granite): exact composition collapses under every selector, e.g. EPIC 46.6% vs 86.8% of full quality for single-block, and more recompute (r up to 0.4) never closes the 35-40 pp gap [Sections 4.2, 4.4, Fig 4]. Diagnosis: each chunk operator is conditioned on isolated-chunk deep-layer inputs; the composition error recursion e_j = T_{C_j} e_{j-1} + delta_j accumulates injected mismatch, and one deep layer spikes to ~2x the state norm [Section 3.2, Eq 7, Fig 3]. The authors attribute the failure to "the aggregation of K independently conditioned deep operators" rather than the architecture.
