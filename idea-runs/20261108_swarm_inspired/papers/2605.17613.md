# VeriCache: Turning Lossy KV Cache into Lossless LLM Inference

## What this paper does
VeriCache uses any compressed KV cache (eviction such as KVzip, or quantization) as a self-drafter: the same model decodes on compressed KV to draft tokens, and periodically verifies them against the full KV cache, which lives off-GPU (CPU/remote) and is swapped in over PCIe/network. Cross-resource staggering overlaps HBM-bound drafting with interconnect-bound full-KV reloads; a runtime places each request's verification in windows with spare interconnect/HBM capacity. Output is identical to full-KV greedy decoding.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it already realize "low-precision resident, exact copy off-GPU, fetch on demand" (seed 4)?
Yes at the whole-request granularity: full KV is kept off-GPU and reloaded entirely for verification every x (typically 20-50) drafted tokens [full text, Sec 4.2-4.3, Sec 5.2]. It does not fetch selectively by block or attention concentration; the full cache crosses the interconnect for each verify. Speedup grows as KV budget shrinks (1.61x -> 2.71x as KV budget goes 0.74 -> 0.2 of HBM) and as the HBM/interconnect ratio falls (1.92x on H100 NVL to 3.01x on GH200) [Sec 7, Fig 13].

### Goal: Acceptance and throughput numbers?
At 4x compaction acceptance stays above about 0.8 at draft length 30; acceptance length about 19 (Qwen-32B) and 23 (Llama-70B) vs Eagle 1-2 [Sec 4.3 Fig 8-9]. Long-context decoding 1.92-2.73x over full KV; with a drafter composed up to 4.26x (Qwen-32B) [Sec 7]. Divergence of lossy-KV outputs grows with output length and breaks code/tool-calling correctness [Sec 1, Sec 3]. Long-CoT math is not the evaluated workload.
