# TreeAdv: Tree-Structured Advantage Redistribution for Group-Based RL

## What this paper does
TreeAdv samples the K rollouts of a prompt as an entropy-branched forest: it branches at high-entropy tokens and shares low-entropy prefixes. It computes the usual GRPO sequence advantage for each leaf. A token's advantage is the mean of the leaf advantages over all rollouts that share that token, and it plugs into GRPO or GSPO clipped objectives.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does TreeAdv aggregate outcome evidence over shared prefixes?
A^tok_{i,t} = (1/|S(a_{i,t})|) * sum over l in S(a_{i,t}) of A_l. Here S is the set of trajectories that share the token, and the normalization keeps near-root tokens from dominating by scale [full text, Section 3.3 Eq 3]. The weights are uniform. There is no variance-optimal weighting, no covariance treatment, and no merging of non-prefix states (the structure is a tree, not a graph).
