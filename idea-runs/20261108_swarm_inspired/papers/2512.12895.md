# Wait, Wait, Wait… Why Do Reasoning Models Loop?

## What this paper does
Empirical + synthetic study (ICML 2026) of looping in open reasoning models (R1-Distill Qwen 1.5/7/32B and Llama-8B, OpenThinker3 1.5/7B, QwQ-32B, Phi-4-reasoning(-plus)). A response loops if some 30-gram appears ≥20 times. On AIME24/25 with 20 samples per (problem, temperature ∈ {0..1.0}), looping is common at low temperature, falls with temperature and with model capacity, and is higher on harder problems. A star-graph task isolates two causes: risk aversion when the progress action is hard to learn, and a Transformer bias toward temporally correlated errors. Says nothing about KV compression; useful as the loop-rate metric definition.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How is looping defined and measured, and how common is it?
Loop = some n-gram with n=30 repeated k≥20 times, ablations in App A.3; averages over AIME 2024+2025, 20 samples per setting, GPQA in App A.4 [full text, Sec 2]. At low temperature Qwen-1.5B loops 76% vs Qwen-32B 37%; distilled OpenThinker3-1.5B 30% vs teacher QwQ-32B 4%; harder problems (bottom 5 by T=0.8 accuracy per split) loop more [Sec 2].
### Goal: What mechanisms cause loops and does temperature fix them?
Hardness of learning puts relatively more mass on an easy cyclic action (Prop 3.1) [Sec 3]; temporally correlated errors: on G(5,5) greedy looping count 11.84 and accuracy 44.43% vs ~2.1 and ~99% at positive temperature; top-1 prediction persistence 80.46% across consecutive root visits [Sec 4.1, Fig 4]. Temperature reduces loops but responses stay longer than necessary (≈2.74× teacher length at T=1 in the hardness setting) — a stopgap [Sec 3-4]. Training with a revisit margin m=0.1 removes greedy looping [Sec 4.1].
### Goal: Implication for KV-compression evaluation?
Any perturbation that raises low-temperature looping (e.g., KV error inflating length) will show up most on small/distilled models and hard problems; loop rate must therefore be reported per temperature and difficulty split. This is an inference from the paper's findings, not something it tests.
