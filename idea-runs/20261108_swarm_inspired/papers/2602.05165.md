# EBPO: Empirical Bayes Shrinkage for Stabilizing Group-Relative Policy Optimization

## What this paper does
EBPO replaces GRPO's per-prompt group-mean baseline with an empirical-Bayes posterior mean. The baseline shrinks the local group mean toward a running global success rate, with shrinkage factor S = (sigma^2/G) / (sigma^2/G + tau^2) from Welford-tracked within-group and between-group variances. Advantages are then batch-standardized. The theory covers non-vanishing gradients under saturation, MSE reduction of the baseline, and clustered sampling to improve the prior.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Is variance-ratio shrinkage of the baseline already used in GRPO-style RL, and over what structure?
Yes, at the prompt level. V^EB_q = (1 - S_q) mu_group + S_q mu_glob [full text, Section 3.2 Eq 2-3], with advantage (r - V^EB) standardized over the batch [Eq 4]. The pooling is across prompts or topic clusters, not across states of a rollout graph, and the objective is baseline MSE rather than gradient MSE with cross-edge covariance [Section 3.3].
