# Attention Amnesia in Hybrid LLMs: When CoT Fine-Tuning Breaks Long-Range Recall, and How to Fix It

## What this paper does
Finds that chain-of-thought SFT on math data systematically degrades long-context recall (NIAH) of distilled hybrid linear-attention models (HypeNet 2B-9B, Jet-Nemotron), worse at harder NIAH variants and longer contexts, while instruction (non-CoT) SFT does not. Attributes it to CoT's local Markov structure concentrating attention-logit gradients on short distances, which drifts W_Q, W_K of the few retained softmax layers. Proposes QK-Restore: copy W_Q, W_K of those layers back from the pre-SFT checkpoint (plus a Procrustes variant). EMNLP 2026.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How large is the long-context recall loss in hybrids after reasoning post-training, and where does it come from?
HypeNet-9B NIAH-Single-2 at 256K: 67.2 -> 9.4 after CoT-SFT; HypeNet-2B NIAH-Single-3 at 128K: 30.4 -> 7.6; degradation grows with context length and retrieval difficulty, NIAH-Single-1 stays saturated [full text, Abstract; Section 6.2, Table 1]. Non-CoT SFT on Tulu-3 preserves or improves NIAH (HypeNet-9B S3@256K 52.0 -> 86.8) [Section 7.1, Table 2]. Mechanism: under a CoT-Markov assumption the expected gradient on attention logits decays exponentially with token distance (Theorem 4.6), validated empirically [Section 4.2-4.3]; damage concentrates in the query-key routing of the retained softmax layers [Section 1, Section 5.1].

### Goal: How much does QK-Restore recover, and what is not tested?
HypeNet-2B S3@256K 10.6 -> 30.2; HypeNet-9B 22.8 -> 42.6; HypeNet-5B S3@256K 65.4 -> 76.4; with OpenThoughts-3 SFT, HypeNet-9B S2@256K 22.4 -> 45.0 [Section 6.2]. Reasoning cost small: HypeNet-5B MATH500 -0.6, GSM8K -0.4 [Section 6.2]. Recovery is partial (e.g. 9B S2@256K 45.0 vs 67.2 before SFT). Only distilled hybrids are studied; natively pretrained hybrids (Qwen3.5, Kimi Linear) and dense Transformers are mentioned but not tested for the same effect [Section 1]. NIAH is the only long-context probe in the sections read.
