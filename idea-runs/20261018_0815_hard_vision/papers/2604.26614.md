# State Beyond Appearance: Diagnosing and Improving State Consistency in Dial-Based Measurement Reading

## What this paper does
Studies clock and gauge reading as recovery of a latent dial state under appearance nuisance (viewpoint, illumination, reflection, blur). Builds controlled LLM-code-driven synthesis pipelines for clocks and half-circle single-pointer gauges, probes MLLM visual features with same-state / neighbouring-state pairs, and proposes TriSCA: (1) triplet state-distance alignment of the visual tower (400K synthetic triplets), (2) metadata-grounded SFT (200K samples), (3) GRPO with a state-distance reward on 1,273 real dial images, on Qwen3-VL-4B.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What does probing show about whether off-the-shelf visual features encode dial state under appearance change?
Same-state samples across appearance conditions are "not consistently compact", and neighbouring states (12:00 vs 12:01) "remain insufficiently distinguishable"; features are organised more by appearance than by physical state [full text, Sec 3.4, Fig 4]. Same-state Recall@1 is 77.33% and silhouette 0.0725 for Qwen3-VL-4B's features; 90.00% / 0.3409 after alignment [full text, Tab 1]. The probe compares whole-image features across appearances; it does not test within-appearance comparisons of counterfactual states.

### Goal: How do frontier models do, and what does the trained method reach on real dials?
On the controlled clean clock set, Gemini-3.1-Pro reaches Tol-1 22.0%, Tol-5 41.0%, MAE 63.12 min (Combined split: 13.6 / 25.4 / 72.93); Molmo2-8B 39.4 / 63.6 / 32.31 [full text, Tab 4]. On MeasureBench real dials TriSCA lifts Qwen3-VL-4B from 12.0% to 28.5%, below or near Gemini-2.5-Pro's 31.5% [full text, Sec 5.3, Tab 3].

### Goal: What does it cost and what does it assume?
Visual-tower training for 3 epochs plus full-parameter fine-tuning of a 4B VLM and a real-image GRPO stage; the gains depend on all three stages (Combined: 20.0 clock / 18.4 gauge only with all three) [full text, Sec 5.1, Tab 5]. It modifies weights, so it cannot be applied to an API frontier model; the gauge synthesis covers only single-pointer half-circle dials [full text, Sec 3.3].
