# Vision-Language Models for Analog Gauge Reading: An Empirical Study of Specialization, Transfer and Reliability

## What this paper does
An empirical study (submitted to EAAI, Aug 2026) of Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct as a direct analog-gauge reader without any pointer-segmentation or geometric pipeline. Compares zero-shot, ICL (1-50 examples) and QLoRA fine-tuning on a synthetic set, a video-derived pressure-gauge set and a proprietary Siemens Energy set, with and without supplied gauge ranges; reports range-normalised MPE, leave-one-dataset-out transfer, corruption robustness and token-confidence calibration. Best fine-tuned MPE 2.39% / 2.61% / 4.43%; test sets are small (126 / 36 / 43 images).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does this paper build a training-free semantics-plus-geometry hybrid around a black-box VLM (overlap with a frontier-semantics + measured-ticks proposal)?
No. Its stated contribution is evaluating a VLM "without an explicit pointer-segmentation and geometric-reading pipeline" [full text, Abstract, Sec 1 contributions 1-4]. The only VLM used is an open 7B model that is fine-tuned with QLoRA; no frontier API model, no tick lattice, no hybrid. Geometric/OCR verification appears only as a proposed, unvalidated safeguard: "(v) redundant verification by OCR/geometric reasoning or a second model" [full text, Sec 6]. Not a novelty threat for a training-free hybrid; it is related work for "trained VLM readers transfer poorly".

### Goal: What does it find about zero-shot accuracy, transfer and abstention signals?
Zero-shot/ICL is unstable: best ICL MPE 15.83% (synthetic), 5.89% (pressure gauge), 27.07% (SE) and more examples do not help monotonically [full text, Sec 5.1]. LODO transfer "degrades substantially" on synthetic and SE [Sec 7, limitation 5]. Token-sequence confidence gives ECE ~0.05 and AURC ~0.06 on 205 pooled predictions but still yields high-confidence errors (conf >= 0.99 with error > 5% FS), so "autoregressive token confidence must not be used as a sole validity signal" [Sec 5.7, Sec 6]. Blur is the strongest corruption [Sec 6]. Supports the motivation for a reliability signal independent of the VLM's own confidence, but on small, non-MeasureBench test sets.

# Vision-Language Models for Analog Gauge Reading: An Empirical Study of Specialization, Transfer and Reliability

## What this paper does
An empirical study of Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct for direct single-gauge reading: zero-shot, in-context learning, and QLoRA fine-tuning with and without supplied gauge range, on a public synthetic Kaggle set (126 test), a video-derived Pressure Gauge set (36 test) and a proprietary Siemens Energy set (43 test). Reports range-normalised MPE with bootstrap CIs, leave-one-dataset-out transfer, corruption robustness and token-confidence calibration.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it propose a geometric or tool-augmented reader, or a training-free method that overlaps a frontier-semantics + measured-geometry dial reader?
No. It deliberately studies a VLM "without an explicit pointer-segmentation and geometric-reading pipeline" and fine-tunes weights (QLoRA) [full text, Abstract, Sec 1 contributions]. Best fine-tuned MPE 2.39% synthetic, 2.61% Pressure Gauge, 4.43% industrial; LODO transfer degrades substantially [full text, Abstract, Sec 5.4].

### Goal: Is VLM confidence a usable abstention signal on gauges?
Partly. Token-sequence confidence gives ECE 0.053 and AURC 0.064 on 205 pooled test predictions, but there are high-confidence errors (absolute error > 5% FS at confidence >= 0.99) [full text, Sec 5.7, Tab 8]. The authors conclude confidence "is informative enough to rank some predictions but is not a reliable stand-alone safety signal" and that abstention needs independent validation [full text, Sec 5.7, Sec 7].

### Goal: How large are its test sets and how strong is its statistical evidence?
Small: 126 / 36 / 43 test images; random splits for two of three sets; authors state bootstrap CIs do not replace a larger independent benchmark [full text, Sec 7 Limitations 1-2].
