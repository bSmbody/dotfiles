# Learning to Look Again: Loss-Gap Supervision for Free-form Crop Routing in Vision-Language Models

## What this paper does
GapSight learns when and where a VLM should re-read a crop. Offline, it compares the target VLM's answer NLL (or correct-option margin) under a global-only view with the same quantity under candidate crop-augmented views. The best gain becomes a review/preserve label, a utility target and a box target. A lightweight router reads the VLM's hidden states on the global preview and predicts gate, utility and a free-form box. One crop is injected if it chooses review. Evaluated on LLaVA-1.5-7B, InternVL2.5-8B and Qwen2-VL-2B over TextVQA, DocVQA, ChartQA, InfoVQA, V* and MME-RealWorld-Lite.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism decides whether to call a tool and whether to trust its output over the model's own reading?
A learned gate on hidden states of the global preview decides whether to review, before any crop tokens exist [full text, Method "Free-Form Crop Router"]. Once a crop is injected, the VLM answers from the global-plus-crop context. There is no explicit arbitration between the first reading and the crop reading [full text, Method "Training and Inference"].

### Goal: What signal (and at what granularity) is the trust/necessity decision based on, and does it require the VLM's weights or logits?
The signal is instance-level and model-specific: Δ_i = NLL(y|q,g(x)) − NLL(y|q,g(x),c(x,b_i)), or the gain in option margin [full text, Method "Loss-Gap Supervision"]. It needs answer-token likelihoods for label mining and hidden states as router input, so it is white-box. Labels must be mined per backbone because crop utility transfers poorly: off-diagonal retention averages 0.44 [full text, Mechanism Analysis "Useful Crops Are Model-Specific", Fig 3].

### Goal: What limitation does it report or show on handling incorrect tool outputs or confidently wrong VLM readings?
On InternVL2.5-8B, review creates 107 repairs but also 27 regressions (right-to-wrong), with higher harm on InfographicVQA [full text, Mechanism Analysis, Table 4]. The extra view sometimes overrides a correct first reading, and nothing in the method arbitrates between the two. The only "tool" is a crop of the same image; specialist outputs are not considered.
