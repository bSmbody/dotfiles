# Cost-Aware Vision–Language Model Arbitration for Fabric Structure Recognition: A Deployable Multi-Agent System

## What this paper does
A specialist-first cascade for 14-class fabric recognition. A hierarchical ConvNeXt-Tiny CNN classifies every image. A hand-designed confidence router (top-1 dominance plus top-1/top-2 separation, minus a confusion-pair penalty) sends only low-confidence cases to a cloud VLM. The VLM picks among the CNN's top-3 taxonomy-consistent candidates with textual visual cues, and a rule-based reconciler merges the two.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What mechanism decides whether to call a tool and whether to trust its output over the model's own reading?
The direction is reversed: the specialist is primary and the VLM is the called "tool". The router fires when c(x) = 0.6·c_dom + 0.4·c_sep − δ_conf < 0.55, or when a known confusion pair has margin < 0.15 [full text, Sections III-B, III-C]. The reconciler blends 0.7·P_cnn + 0.3·P_vlm on agreement. It accepts the VLM override only if the VLM picks the CNN's second candidate with self-reported confidence > 0.8, and flags all other conflicts for human review [full text, Section III-D].

### Goal: What signal (and at what granularity) is the trust/necessity decision based on, and does it require the VLM's weights or logits?
Per instance, from the specialist's softmax over a closed taxonomy (tuned by grid search on validation for a 5% trigger budget), plus the VLM's verbalised confidence for overrides. The VLM is black-box [full text, Sections III-B, III-D].

### Goal: What limitation does it report or show on handling incorrect tool outputs or confidently wrong VLM readings?
The router depends on a calibrated closed-set softmax with known confusion pairs. Weights and thresholds are hand-set for one 14-class taxonomy [full text, Section III-B]. The override rule trusts the VLM's self-reported confidence, so a confidently wrong VLM pick of the second candidate is accepted. The paper reports no analysis of override errors. It is applicable only where the specialist outputs a class distribution, not to open-form answers such as counts or measurements.
