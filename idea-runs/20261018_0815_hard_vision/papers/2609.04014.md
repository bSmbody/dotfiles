# InSituMeasure: Probing Situated Measurement Grounding in Industrial Scenes with Multimodal Large Language Models

## What this paper does
A benchmark of 2,922 real industrial monitoring tasks over eight instrument categories (digital indicators, SF6 pressure dials, pressure gauges, oil-temperature gauges, leakage-current meters and trip counters, oil-flow relays, oil-level gauges), single- and multi-gauge scenes, with noise/root-cause tags and matched unanswerable counterfactual cases. It scores 24 MLLMs on value+unit accuracy under per-task tolerance, answerability F1, coverage/selective risk and overconfidence. It proposes no reading method.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it propose a training-free semantics-plus-geometry reader or any tool-augmented method that overlaps a frontier-semantics + measured-tick dial reader?
No. It is an evaluation benchmark only; the related-work section describes classical detection/segmentation/rectification/OCR/scale-parsing pipelines as limited to specific instruments and degrading under clutter, glare, occlusion and viewpoint [full text, Sec 2 "Gauge Measurement Methods & Benchmarks"]. No method is proposed or evaluated beyond off-the-shelf MLLMs [full text, Sec 4, Limitations].

### Goal: How well do frontier MLLMs do and what failure causes dominate?
Best of 24 models reaches 25.7% joint value-unit accuracy (best of five runs) and 51.8% confidence-diagnosis F1 [full text, Abstract, Fig 5]. Unit is consistently easier than value. Value errors: mixed causes 37.1%, view deviation 20.7%, soft occlusion 18.3%, environmental noise 11.6%, hard occlusion 5.7% [full text, Sec 4 "Noise Impact Analysis", Fig 7]. Models evaluated are named as Gemini 2.5 Pro, GPT-5, Qwen3-VL class; no GPT-6 Astra numbers.

### Goal: Is it usable as an additional real-image test set for an analog-dial reader with abstention?
In scope it fits (real industrial dials, viewpoint and occlusion tags, answerability labels, selective-risk metric) [full text, Sec 3 Evaluation Criteria]. The text gives no release URL or licence in the body I read, so availability is unconfirmed; it also contains digital and liquid-level categories outside an analog-dial method's scope.

# InSituMeasure: Probing Situated Measurement Grounding in Industrial Scenes with Multimodal Large Language Models

## What this paper does
A benchmark (arXiv Sept 2026) of 2,922 real industrial-monitoring gauge-reading tasks in eight instrument categories (digital indicators, SF6 pressure dials, pressure gauges, oil-temperature gauges, leakage-current meters, oil-flow relays, oil-level gauges), single- and multi-instrument scenes, with dense attribute and noise tags (view deviation, environmental noise, soft/hard occlusion, pointer-like interference) and deliberately unanswerable counterfactual cases to test abstention. Evaluates 24 MLLMs on value, unit, joint accuracy, coverage, selective risk, overconfidence.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does it propose a method (semantics + geometry, or abstention) that overlaps a training-free hybrid dial reader?
No. It is an evaluation benchmark only; it proposes no reading method [full text, Sec InSituMeasure, Experiments]. Its related work characterises specialised pipelines (detection, segmentation, rectification, OCR, scale parsing, pointer localisation, rules) as limited to specific instrument types and imaging settings [full text, Related Work].

### Goal: What does it report about frontier accuracy and abstention, and is it a natural second real-image benchmark for dial reading?
Best of 24 MLLMs reaches 25.7% joint value-unit accuracy and 51.8% confidence-diagnosis F1 [full text, Abstract]; unit is consistently easier than value, and models are poorly calibrated with a non-negligible share of confident errors [full text, Main Results, Confidence Analysis]. Value errors are attributed to mixed causes 37.1%, view deviation 20.7%, soft occlusion 18.3%, environmental noise 11.6%, hard occlusion 5.7% [full text, Noise Impact Analysis]. It explicitly scores selective risk, coverage and overconfidence on unanswerable cases [full text, Evaluation Criteria], so it fits an abstention claim directly. Splits keep same-instrument/same-scene tasks together [full text, Data Split]. The text I read does not state a public download link; availability must be checked.


### Goal: Is InSituMeasure publicly released (usable now under a public-data-only constraint)?
The full text contains no URL, repository or licence for the data [full text, whole body; grep for github/huggingface/http returns nothing]; data-split text says only that same-scene and same-instrument tasks stay in one split [Sec "Data Split and Leakage Control"]. A web search on 2026-10-18 found the paper but no dataset or code release. It cannot be used as a public test set until released; its analog-pointer categories (SF6 pressure dials, pressure gauges, oil-temperature gauges) are the relevant subset if it is.
