# Hybrid Linear Attention Done Right: Efficient Distillation and Effective Architectures for Extremely Long Contexts (HALO / HypeNet)

## What this paper does
HALO distills Qwen3 Transformers into attention-RNN hybrids with 2.3B tokens: attention-weight transfer, hidden-state alignment, attention layer selection, knowledge distillation and finetuning. HypeNet adds a hybrid position encoding (HyPE) and other changes for length generalization.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does HALO choose which attention layers stay full, and is the count chosen from data?
Each layer i gets a one-swap score: the drop in recall performance when only layer i is replaced by its stage-1 RNN, divided by the drop in commonsense-reasoning performance (Eq 8), and the top-k layers are kept with k fixed at floor(L/4) [full text, Section 4.3, Eq 8-9]. Selection is single-layer, before end-to-end distillation, on recall/CSR benchmarks, not on reasoning rollouts; the budget is hand-set. It is compared against Jet-Nemotron and KL-LS selection inside the HALO pipeline [Section 6.5, Table 4].
