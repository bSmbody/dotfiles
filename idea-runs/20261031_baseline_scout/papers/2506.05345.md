# Inference-Time Hyper-Scaling with KV Cache Compression (Dynamic Memory Sparsification, DMS)

## What this paper does
Retrofits pretrained Transformers (Qwen-R1 1.5B-32B, Llama 3.x) with a learned per-head token-eviction policy so reasoning models can generate longer or more traces at the same memory and latency. Each token's eviction decision is predicted from its hidden state with a Gumbel-sigmoid relaxation, applied as an additive attention mask in training, and executed only after a delay (a sliding window). Trained by logit distillation from the original model plus a compression-target loss; reaches 4x compression in ~300 steps and 8x in ~700.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What does DMS train, with what objective, and where does an evicted token's information go?
A per-head binary eviction decision alpha_t is predicted from h_t by a repurposed q/k neuron (no new parameters), relaxed with Gumbel-sigmoid; during training the decisions form an additive mask log(1−alpha) on the attention logits [full text, Section 3.1, Eq 1, Figure 3]. The objective is logit distillation from the unmodified model plus a one-sided L1 loss that anneals the mean eviction rate to 1 − 1/CR [Section 3.1 "Training Objective"]. Evicted tokens are simply gone; delayed eviction keeps a flagged token readable for w more steps so "the model is given a number of generation steps to integrate its information" into later tokens [Section 3.1 "Delayed Eviction"]. Immediate eviction "drastically deteriorates scores", while delayed eviction keeps reasoning up to 4x with a 16-token window [Section 5.3, Figure 4].

### Goal: What are its scope and limitations, in particular for hybrids or other attention variants?
Models 1B-32B, contexts up to 32K, CR up to 8x [Appendix A]. It is demonstrated only on standard multi-head attention; extension to MLA and other attention variants is left as future work [Appendix A "Integration with Other Efficient Attention Mechanisms"]. There is no recurrent layer that could hold an evicted token, so the only fallback DMS can train is later tokens' residual streams. Learned compression is lower early in a sequence and more aggressive beyond 10K tokens; early layers are compressed less [Section 5.3, Figure 5].


### Goal: What headline results, cost and baselines does DMS report?
Averaged Pareto-frontier gains at matched KV memory reads: +12.5 AIME24, +2.3 MATH-500, +5.8 GPQA Diamond, +8.3 LiveCodeBench; Qwen-R1 32B +12.0 AIME24, +8.6 GPQA, +9.7 LCB [full text, Section 5.1; abstract]. Baselines: TOVA, H2O, Quest, DMC; all models are GQA [Section 4]. Retrofitting: logit distillation with CR(t)=t/100+1, i.e. ~100 steps per unit of compression ratio, default eviction delay 256 [Section 4]. Scope limit: 1B-32B, <=32K context, CR <= 8x, standard attention only; MLA left to future work; DMS is orthogonal to Quest-style retrieval [Appendix A]. No code repository URL found in the body.
