# OSCAR: Offline Spectral Covariance-Aware Rotation for 2-bit KV Cache Quantization

## What this paper does
INT2 KV-cache quantization for long reasoning traces. Offline, from one small calibration pass (8878 tokens of MMLU-style text), it estimates attention-aware covariances and builds fixed per-layer rotations for keys (R_K = U_Q H_Had P_K: query-importance eigenbasis, Hadamard, group-balancing permutation) and values, plus per-layer clipping thresholds. Online, sink (64) and recent (256) tokens stay BF16 and the rest is rotated-clipped INT2 in the paged SGLang cache, decoded by custom Triton kernels. Value rotation is absorbed into weights.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What models, lengths and headline results are reported?
Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507, Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-32B, GLM-4.7-FP8 (8xH100); GPQA, HumanEval, LCB v6, AIME25, MATH500; 32K generation cap, 5 seeds [full text, Section 5.1]. Mean drop vs BF16 at 2.28 BPE: -3.78 (Qwen3-4B-Thinking), -1.42 (Qwen3-8B); QuaRot-INT2 -74.24 / -60.70; TurboQuant (no mixed precision, 3.25 BPE, vLLM PR #38479) -43.90 / -13.96; Saw-INT4 -2.53 / -0.87 [Section 5.2, Table 2]. AIME25 at 32K: Qwen3-8B BF16 66.00, OSCAR 66.67, KIVI-2 52.33, Kitty 59.67 [Table 1].
### Goal: What runtime/kernel support and speed are reported?
Integrated into SGLang paged attention with prefix cache; fused Triton write and decode kernels; up to 6.2x throughput at 100k length and ~2x per-user speed and per-GPU throughput [Section 1, Section 4, Section 5.4]. Qwen3-8B decode step 9.9 ms -> 8.0 ms at B=1 [Table 8]. No code repository URL is stated.
### Goal: What limitations do the authors state?
Theory holds under a frozen-error surrogate, "not yet a full proof ... for the complete autoregressive decoding process"; no NVFP4 or mixed formats; "fixed bit-width ... does not study per-token bit allocation or thought-adaptive token precision" (cites ThinKV); kernels tuned for H100 only; combining with channel-wise schemes "while preserving an efficient serving layout remains open" [Appendix D].
