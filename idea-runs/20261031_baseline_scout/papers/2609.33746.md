# PQ-HSA: Reusing Product-Quantized Scores for Hybrid Sparse-Approximate Attention

## What this paper does
Training-free decode-time sparse attention. Keys are indexed with direction-norm residual IVF-PQ (n_list=512, M=8 subspaces, 16 codewords, 4 bytes/token/KV head). Every indexed token gets an approximate logit from one LUT lookup per subspace; the top-k (1-2%) are attended with their exact K/V, and the unselected "background" enters the same softmax through the PQ logits, summed per inverted list and multiplied by the list's mean value. Near full-attention accuracy on 128K RULER with Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen3-30B-A3B; 1.6x faster than FA3 decode at 128K inside vLLM.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does PQ-HSA scan every token's codes at decode, and what is that scan used for?
Yes. "We scan every list at decode time, so every indexed token receives a score" [full text, Section 3.1]. The one score stream has two uses: TopK selection of the exact set E and the background logits for B [Section 3.2, Eq 3-4]. The mechanism study shows that scanning every token (variant T, all lists) instead of probing a few lists (variant A) raises truncation accuracy by 0.160 macro [Section 4.3]. So the per-token scan is what makes the selector accurate, not only the background.

### Goal: How is the background numerator/denominator computed, and how much does value fidelity of the background matter?
Z_B = sum of per-list masses M_j (sum of exp of PQ logits of unselected members); N_B = sum_j M_j * vbar_j with vbar_j the list mean over all members, maintained at index updates, so the value side reads n_list means and none of the unselected rows [Section 3.2, Eq 4-5]. Query-dependent value weighting was rejected because it needs reading every unselected value. List means add only 0.02-0.03 macro over a single global mean (B minus C); PQ logits add 0.067-0.090 over a shared logit; B is within -0.010 [-0.031, +0.011] of full attention at 1% [Section 4.3]. Median relative output error: 0.063 (B) vs 0.124 (truncation) vs 0.070 (global-mean value) [Section 4.3, Appendix B].

### Goal: What bounds the speedup, and what limitation is stated?
Fitted cost t_pq = F + c H_kv G L: one LUT and one score row per query head, so the per-row scan grows with H_kv*G*L, the group size G bounds the attainable speedup a/(cG), break-even 57K-100K [Section 4.4, Eq 6]. Limitations: short contexts, few KV heads or large GQA groups, where "a fixed per-layer overhead and the per-query-row scan dominate"; evaluation only on two GQA models; MLA untested [Section 5].
