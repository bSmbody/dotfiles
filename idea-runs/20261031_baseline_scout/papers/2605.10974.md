# Vertex-Softmax: Tight Transformer Verification via Exact Softmax Optimization

## What this paper does
Gives an exact solver for the row-level softmax subproblem inside CROWN-style transformer verification: minimize c^T softmax(s) over an independent score box with a fixed downstream coefficient vector c. Via y=exp(s) the problem becomes a linear-fractional program over a positive box; the optimum is at a vertex, and after sorting c it is one of K+1 threshold vertices, so it costs O(K log K). Proves this is the tightest bound obtainable from independent score intervals alone, and plugs it into a Vertex-CROWN verifier on small ViT-style models (MNIST/F-MNIST/CIFAR-10).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What exactly is the score-box problem and its optimality result, and is the value coefficient fixed?
L_box = min_{s in B} sum_j c_j e^{s_j} / sum_j e^{s_j}, with c "fixed with respect to s" and B a product of independent intervals [full text, Section 2, Eq 6]. Theorem 3.1: optimum at a vertex of B (coordinate-wise the ratio (c_i y_i + A)/(y_i + D) is monotone); Theorem 3.2: optimum is min over K+1 threshold vertices tau_m after sorting c (heavy-cheap / light-expensive split) [full text, Section 3, Eq 9-11]. Corollary 3.3: any sound bound that depends only on (c, l, u) is no tighter than L_box, so "any tighter certificate must exploit score correlations, score–value coupling, or a reachable score set smaller than the independent box" [full text, Section 3, Cor 3.3]. The paper itself notes the vertex property of linear-fractional programs is classical (Dinkelbach 1967); its contribution is the threshold reduction and verifier integration.
### Goal: What does the paper say about score–value coupling as a remaining source of slack?
Limitations list four slack sources outside the oracle: independent score boxes drop score correlations; "value lower bounds discard score–value coupling; nonnegative softmax weights preserve soundness, but weak value bounds can dominate the final certificate"; row-wise worst cases that no single input attains; nonlinear suffix relaxations [full text, Section 6]. The conclusion names "solving correlated score polytopes or jointly optimizing over scores and values" as the natural next step [full text, Section 7]. So a joint score–value oracle is the authors' own stated future work, not an unflagged gap.
### Goal: Does the paper address KV-cache quantization or LLM-scale settings?
No. Experiments are one-block ViT-style image models; the perturbation set is an input pixel box propagated by CROWN, not a quantization cell of a cached tensor [full text, Sections 5-6].
