# DeltaLog: Deferred Materialization of Recurrent States for Linear Attention Decoding

## What this paper does
Identifies a "state-update tax" in linear-attention decoding: each token adds only O(d_k+d_v) compact factors but eager kernels read and write the full d_k x d_v state per head every step. DeltaLog stores a dense base state plus a bounded log of recent compact updates, appends most steps, and periodically merges, which is exact in exact arithmetic. Implemented for GDN, KDA and RWKV6, value-tiled on GPU, and integrated into a vLLM-based serving path for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and Kimi-Linear-48B-A3B.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How large is recurrent-state traffic in hybrid decode latency, measured?
In Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at a 1K context, "recurrent-state materialization grows from 25.2% of decode latency at B=64 to 44.6% at B=256", with shares at 2K/4K also rising with batch [full text, Section 2.3, Figure 2]. Roofline: arithmetic intensity of eager dense update is 0.5 FLOPs/byte (FP32 state) or ~1.0 (16-bit) vs ~208 FLOPs/byte machine balance on H200 [Section 2.4]. Example: B=512, H=32, d_k=d_v=128, FP32 state = 1 GiB, ~2 GiB moved per decode step for state read+write alone [Section 2.4].

### Goal: What speedups and limitations are reported?
Kernel latency vs FLA: 1.19-1.69x on H200, 1.30-1.86x on RTX 4090; state write traffic reduced up to 7.83x; end-to-end TPOT 1.08-1.20x (Qwen/GDN, merge interval 8) and 1.05-1.12x (Kimi/KDA, M=4) at B in {64,128,256}, 128 generated tokens [Section 1; Section 5.6, Table 4]. Limitations: gains bounded by Amdahl (MLP, norms, scheduling); "workloads with small batches or small recurrent states offer less opportunity"; serving evaluated only on GDN and KDA in graph mode [Section 6]. Contexts measured are short (1K-4K); the interplay with the full-attention layers' KV growth at long context is not measured.
