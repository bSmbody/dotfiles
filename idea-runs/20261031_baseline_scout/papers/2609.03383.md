# TIGPO: Temporal Instance-Graph Policy Optimization for Long-Horizon LLM Agents

## What this paper does
TIGPO extends graph-based credit for LLM agents across policy updates. It keeps a persistent per-task transition graph (the union of historical and current graphs) and assigns current transitions shortest-path-based credit C*gamma^{d(s', goal)} on that graph. It splits the rollout budget into Exploration and Revisit slots, and it normalizes Revisit outcomes against earlier Exploration scores as a cross-temporal baseline.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does TIGPO combine evidence across rollouts and policies, and does it treat estimator variance?
Credit comes from graph connectivity, via shortest-path distance on the cross-update graph, normalized among current transitions with the same source state. Historical transitions affect only connectivity, and only current transitions get gradients [full text, Section 4.1 Eq 4-5]. The cross-temporal advantage concatenates the old and current outcome sets for normalization [Section 4.3 Eq 7]. There is no variance or covariance-optimal pooling, and the evidence is deliberately cross-policy.
