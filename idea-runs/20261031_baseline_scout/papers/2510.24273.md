# SALS: Sparse Attention in Latent Space for KV cache Compression

## What this paper does
SALS compresses pre-RoPE keys of all heads jointly into one rank-r latent (calibrated PCA over stacked multi-head keys), scores tokens with a RoPE-free inner product on the leading r* latent dims, picks top-k tokens, and reconstructs and rotates only those keys for exact sparse attention. Values are not low-rank compressed but group-quantized. A Triton kernel fuses selection, reconstruction and RoPE. Reports 6.4x KV compression and 5.7x attention-operator speedup vs FlashAttention2 at 4K, RULER-128K on LLaMA3.1-8B.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What exactly does SALS Section 3.1 show about the cost of pre-RoPE low-rank reconstruction plus rotation?
Eq 3 contrasts pre-RoPE (Q R_i R_j^T U U^T K^T) with post-RoPE (Q R_i U U^T R_j^T K^T) projection. Post-RoPE would allow fusion but RoPE raises key variance/rank, so pre-RoPE is preferred; then reconstructing K~U^T and applying RoPE "grows linearly with sequence length and soon dominates the total attention runtime at 32K tokens" (Figure 1a) [full text, Section 3.1]. The Figure 1a comparison is of full attention vs a low-rank-with-reconstruction pipeline; the paper does not state that this pipeline is a fused on-chip kernel. Related work notes Palu's grouped-head decomposition as the prior way to cut reconstruction cost [full text, Section 2.1].
### Goal: How does SALS avoid full reconstruction, and what does it keep exact?
Algorithm 1: project q into latent, score s_j = q~_{:r*}^T k~_{j,:r*} without RoPE, TopK, reconstruct K_C = K~_C U_r^T for selected tokens only, apply RoPE to them and to q, then exact softmax over C [full text, Section 4.3-4.4, Algorithm 1]. The full softmax over all positions is not computed; non-selected tokens are dropped. Memory-bound speedup model: 2sd / (s r* + 2kr) [full text, Section 4.5].
### Goal: What latent ranks and value treatment are used (relevant to per-head NUFFT counts and byte savings)?
Joint multi-head compression at d_r = 25% and 12.5% of n*d (so r is hundreds to ~1000 for 7-8B models), r* = 0.5r for scoring. "Since value tensors are almost full rank ... we forgo low-rank projection for them" and use 4-bit / 2-bit group quantization instead [full text, Section 5 setup].
