# Dustin: Draft-Augmented Sparse Verification for Efficient Long-Context Generation with Speculative Decoding

## What this paper does
Dustin (ICML 2026) makes the verification pass of speculative decoding sparse. In multi-batch long-context SD, verification is the bottleneck: up to 87.5% of step latency at 32K input and batch 16 [Section 1, Fig 1]. Dustin chooses a KV subset (fixed budget, e.g. 512 tokens) by fusing two signals: lookahead attention from the draft model and historical attention from the target. Importance is scored only on a searched small set of "Semantic Retrieval Heads", keeping estimation overhead near 0.8% of full hybrid scoring. The method is NOT lossless, since the verifier itself sees sparse KV. Accuracy is reported as "near-lossless".

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What setting and headline speedups?
Models: Qwen2.5-72B and Llama-3.3-70B with same-family small drafts. Hardware: 4x H200 with pipeline parallelism. Workloads: PG-19 story continuation for throughput and LongBench for accuracy [Section 5.1]. Self-attention speedup: 9.35x at 16K / batch 8 and 27.85x at 32K / batch 16 [Section 5.3.1]. End-to-end decode speedup on Qwen2.5-72B: 3.01x → 6.61x (batch 8, 8K → 32K) and 4.76x → 9.17x (batch 16). Llama-3.3-70B: 6.28x / 7.18x at 32K [Section 5.3.2]. All of these beat ClassicSD and MagicDec. The observation study uses ~13.5K context and 512-token outputs [App, line ~3213]. Long generated CoT is not evaluated.

### Goal: How does the gain scale with batch and context?
Unlike plain SD, the advantage grows with both batch size and context length [Section 5.3.2]. The reason: verification KV loading grows with B×L and Dustin makes exactly that part sparse.

### Goal: Stated limitations?
[Section 6; App L]: (1) the draft/target signal mix m is fixed by offline profiling, while an oracle shows tasks prefer different mixes, which leaves a gap to lossless; (2) the full KV is kept and indexed, so memory footprint is not reduced and longer sequences or bigger batches do not fit. Implicit: since verification is approximate, the SD output-equivalence guarantee is lost.
