# Kimi Linear: An Expressive, Efficient Attention Architecture

## What this paper does
Introduces Kimi Delta Attention (KDA): the gated delta rule with a per-channel (diagonal) decay Diag(alpha_t) instead of GDN's scalar decay, with a constrained DPLR chunkwise kernel that binds both low-rank vectors to k. Kimi Linear (48B total / 3B active MoE) interleaves 3 KDA layers with 1 full MLA layer (NoPE in MLA), trained on 1.4T tokens (5.7T for the released checkpoint), and compared with full MLA and a GDN hybrid (GDN-H) under the same recipe. KDA kernels in fla and a vLLM implementation are open-sourced, and Kimi-Linear-48B-A3B checkpoints released [full text, Abstract].

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What is the KDA state update and what is the per-token decode state size?
S_t = (I - beta_t k_t k_t^T) Diag(alpha_t) S_{t-1} + beta_t k_t v_t^T, o_t = S_t^T q_t [full text, Section 3 / Eq 1]; alpha_t in [0,1]^{d_k} via low-rank projection; q,k ShortConv+Swish+L2Norm; head-wise RMSNorm and a low-rank sigmoid output gate before W_o [Section 4 / Eq 10]. Prefill uses a chunk kernel (C=64), decode a recurrent kernel; the state is fixed at d_k x d_v = 128 x 128 per head regardless of length [Section 6.3 "Inference strategy and cost"]. KDA kernel is ~2x faster than general DPLR [Section 3.2].

### Goal: What decode/prefill speedups and KV savings are measured, and what bounds them?
KV cache reduced "by up to 75%" (only 1 in 4 layers keeps KV) [Abstract]. Prefill: equal to MLA at 4K-16K, 2.3x faster at 512K, 2.9x at 1M [Section 5.6]. Decode: "the I/O-bounded decoding time approaches a maximum hybrid efficiency ratio of 3:1 compared to full attention", measured 2.3x speedup at 1M with batch size 1 [Section 6.3]; the headline 6.3x TPOT (1.84ms vs 11.48ms at 1M) comes from using the freed memory for larger batches [Figure 1(b) caption; Section 6.3 calls it "theoretical"]. So at long length, the remaining MLA layers' KV reads dominate decode, bounding speedup by the 3:1 ratio.

### Goal: What quality results and stated limitations are reported?
At 128K: RULER 84.3 and RepoQA 68.5, avg 54.5, best vs MLA and GDN-H, except LongBench V2 and Frames [Section 5.5.1, Table 5]. GDN-H falls behind MLA on long context, while KDA stays ahead [Section 5.5.1 summary]. Hybrid ratio ablation: 7:1 gives "significantly worse validation performance", 1:1 costs more inference [Section 5.2]. Stated design limitation: "Long-context retrieval remains the primary bottleneck for pure linear attention", hence full MLA layers [Section 4]. Removing the output gate degrades quality, swish gate worse than sigmoid [Section 5.2]. Note an internal inconsistency in speed claims: Section 5.6 says "6x faster than full attention" at 1M decoding, while Section 6.3 measures 2.3x at batch 1 and labels 6.3x theoretical.
