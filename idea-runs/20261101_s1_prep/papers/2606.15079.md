# Ling and Ring 2.6 Technical Report: Efficient and Instant Agentic Intelligence at Trillion-Parameter Scale

## What this paper does
Technical report of Ling-2.6 (instant) and Ring-2.6 (reasoning) up to 1T parameters. Instead of pretraining from scratch, the Ling-2.0 GQA checkpoint is retrofitted by "architectural migration pre-training" into a hybrid of Lightning Attention and MLA at a 7:1 ratio; remaining GQA layers are converted to MLA (QK-Norm fused away by calibration, partial-RoPE decoupled for TransMLA-style PCA). Also covers token-efficiency post-training, KPop RL, MTP speculative decoding and fused kernels.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How is the hybrid ratio chosen and how is a dense GQA checkpoint converted?
Scaling-law comparison under equal FLOPs of groups of M layers with 1 MLA + (M-1) Lightning layers, M = 2, 4, 8, 16; M=8 (7:1) "achieves the best scaling performance", and "as the total FLOPs budget scales larger, the proportion of Linear Attention layers can be moderately increased without degradation" [full text, Section 2.1.2, Figure 3, Table 2]. Lightning conversion expands GQA to MHA with randomly initialized new parameters and adds gating [Section 2.1.3]. MLA conversion: "QK Norm is a nonlinear operation that prevents the KV weight matrix absorption required for efficient MLA inference", so QK-Norm is fused into W_q/W_k using calibration RMS statistics (Eq 1-2); partial RoPE is handled by applying PCA rotation only to RoPE dims [Section 2.1.3]. Ablations at 16B-A1.3B and 100B-A5B: converting all GQA to MLA and training 700B + 600B tokens surpassed the GQA baseline [Section 2.1.3].

### Goal: What inference-cost observations and limitations are stated?
"At context windows exceeding 32K tokens, the computational cost shifts decisively from MoE layers to attention layers, and at 256K+ tokens, Full Attention (GQA) becomes the absolute performance bottleneck" [Section 2.1.3]. The 2.6 family delivers "higher decoding throughput than our GQA-based 2.0 generation" and ~4x token efficiency on reasoning workloads from post-training [Section 1]; no per-component latency numbers in the sections read. Limitations: tighter deliberation budget of Ling-2.6-flash constrains reasoning depth; long-horizon agentic robustness weaker than short-horizon; future work on "low-precision training and inference, KV-cache management" [Section 5].
