# Speculative Acceptance Does Not Decay with Position: A Full-Length Diagnostic on Long Chain-of-Thought with Independent Drafts

## What this paper does
A measurement study (single author, Oct 2026) of speculative-decoding acceptance as a function of generation position over full long CoTs. Setup: all of MATH-500, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B as target, and two independent drafts that were not trained on target outputs (R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B as same-family, Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct as cross-family). Greedy and Leviathan sampling, gamma=5, about 1.78M verification rounds. Within-problem paired tests separate the effect of position from changes in composition. Rejections are traced to token types, and a LoRA trajectory-alignment fix is tried.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Does acceptance decay with position in long CoT?
Not for independent drafts. After a greedy repetition-loop artifact is removed, greedy argmax-match stays at 0.91-0.94 over 0-8K and sampling at 0.856-0.882 over 0-12K, with no downward trend. The second half is slightly but significantly higher than the first [Section 4, Fig 1, Table 1]. The authors say this does not contradict the trained-drafter decay reported elsewhere ("Kumar et al., 2026", not read here). That literature attributes decay to a mismatch between training and inference length, and a flat curve for independent drafts is what the mismatch account predicts [Section 4, Scope].

### Goal: Where do rejections concentrate?
On reflection trigger tokens (wait, however, check, verify). Number and operator tokens are accepted at ≥0.93. Reflection tokens score 0.747 under greedy (0.367 cross-family) and alpha=0.788 under sampling (0.444 cross-family) [Section 6, Fig 3]. A three-arm LoRA alignment (uniform, reflection-marked, entropy-weighted) does not improve mean accepted length [Section 7, abstract].

### Goal: Limitations?
[Section 8]: support is thin at long positions (greedy has 15 problems at 8K; sampling has 11 at 12K). Single target family, single seed, fixed gamma=5. No trained drafter (EAGLE-3/PARD/DFlash) was run under this protocol; the authors call that "the single most important extension". The cross-family draft runs at 24.3 tok/s vs 46.3 tok/s target AR, so it is diagnostic only. Single domain (math).
