# vAttention: Verified Sparse Attention

## What this paper does
Training-free, decode-time sparse attention for off-the-shelf LLMs. It composes sink, local window and an (approximate) top-k set computed exactly, with a uniform random sample of the residual tokens whose contribution is rescaled by the inverse sampling rate, and chooses the sample size adaptively per head so numerator and denominator are (epsilon, delta)-accurate. Improves RULER-HARD by up to ~4.5 points over HashAttention at 10% sparsity on Llama-3.1-8B and R1-Distill-Llama-8B.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does vAttention combine deterministic top-k with random sampling to estimate the full attention numerator and denominator, and why does it work?
It splits the sum into a deterministic part (sink, local, predicted top-k, inclusion probability 1) and a residual part estimated by uniform sampling with probability |I_dyn|/n_s, rescaling sampled terms by n_s/|I_dyn| for both numerator N and denominator D [full text, Section 4.1 / Eq 5-7]. Rationale: if a sum is dominated by a few terms, picking them is best; if the remaining terms are similar in value, a small sample estimates them well; "if the heavy tokens are correctly identified, the contribution of the residual tail can be approximated with a small sample" [Section 1, Section 4.1]. The sample size comes from a CLT bound on estimating a sum [Lemma 4.1, Appendix D].

### Goal: Is vAttention used to train a selector or the model?
No. It "targets inference-time attention approximation to accelerate decoding in off-the-shelf models"; the top-k component is any off-the-shelf approximate top-k method (e.g. HashAttention, oracle top-k) and nothing is trained [Section 2, Section 4.1]. It contrasts KNN-Attention, whose emphasis is training [Section 2].
