# Tensor-Train Density Estimation

## What this paper does
Density estimation where the density is expanded in a tensor-product basis f_{i1..id}(x) = f_{i1}(x_1)...f_{id}(x_d) and the exponentially large coefficient tensor is kept in low-rank tensor-train format, trained with Riemannian optimization. The TT form gives exact sampling, marginals, CDF and partition function. (The cached HTML body is truncated after Section 2.2; only the abstract, introduction and problem statement could be read.)

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What does the TT format buy for a product-structured coefficient tensor, and under what condition?
The coefficient tensor alpha over a Cartesian product basis has size K^d, exponential in dimension; the paper restricts it to a low-rank TT subspace, which makes functionals that sum over the product basis (partition function, marginals, CDF) exactly computable by contracting the cores with per-mode vectors [full text, Abstract; Section 2.1; Section 2.2 'Tensor-product basis']. The saving holds only if the target density is well approximated at low TT rank; the readable part does not give rank-vs-error results (body truncated), so empirical rank behavior cannot be confirmed from this read.
