# RoLaQ: Joint Rotation and Range Calibration for 2-Bit KV Caches in Multi-Head Latent Attention

## What this paper does
RoLaQ quantizes the shared MLA content latent to 2 bits. Offline, with frozen weights, it refines a shared rotation within subspaces defined by the frozen key and value up-projections (K/V-aware) and jointly learns quantization ranges whose thresholds and reconstruction gains are coupled, supervised by prediction matching and attention-output losses. The RoPE key stays in BF16.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Headline numbers?
Six-task average with no token protection: 46.7 (DeepSeek-V2-Lite), 60.1 (Moonlight-16B-A3B), 59.4 (MiniCPM3-4B), beating the strongest baseline (OSCAR/KVarN/QuaRot/RTN) by 3.8 / 2.8 / 1.1 points. GSM8K on DeepSeek-V2-Lite rises from 18.3 (OSCAR) to 32.1 (Section 4.2, Table 1). An SGLang implementation on an A100 gives 3.96x BF16 KV capacity and 2.51x decode throughput at 256K input (Abstract, Section 5.5).
### Goal: Does it cover long-CoT reasoning?
No. The benchmarks are MMLU, CMMLU, TriviaQA, GSM8K, HumanEval, MBPP and RULER retrieval on small MLA models, and accuracy is measured with simulated quantization in HF Transformers (Section 4.1). No reasoning-length evaluation.
### Goal: Limitations?
The RoPE key and group metadata stay at full precision and become a large share of the remaining cache. Serving gains depend on the workload: at short contexts, quantize/unpack overheads can offset the savings (Limitations). Compare RAQ-MLA 2610.33625 (quantizes the positional key too) and QuantMLA 2609.36760.
