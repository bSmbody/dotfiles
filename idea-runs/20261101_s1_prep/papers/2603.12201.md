# IndexCache: Accelerating Sparse Attention via Cross-Layer Index Reuse

## What this paper does
Observes that DSA's lightning indexer is O(L^2) per layer (O(NL^2) total) and its top-k choices overlap 70-100% between adjacent layers. IndexCache keeps indexers only in a few "Full" layers and lets "Shared" layers reuse the nearest preceding Full layer's top-k indices. Training-free variant: greedy layer selection minimizing calibration LM loss. Training-aware variant: multi-layer distillation of each retained indexer against all layers it serves. Evaluated on a 30B DSA model (SGLang, H100) and preliminarily on GLM-5 (744B).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How large is the indexer's share of DSA inference cost at long context, measured?
Profiling a 30B DSA model shows "the indexer's share of total latency rises sharply with context length, particularly during the prefill stage, while the rest of the computations grow only modestly" [full text, Section 1, figure]. Decode: "the decode phase in DSA involves a per-token indexer pass over the full context, which becomes the bottleneck at long sequences" [Section 4.2 "Decode"]. Removing 75% of indexers: prefill at 200K 19.5s -> 10.7s (1.82x), 1.27x even at 10K; single-request decode at 200K 58 -> 86 tok/s (1.48x); saturated-KV total decode throughput +22-51%, 197 -> 297 tok/s at 200K (1.51x) [Section 4.2, Table 1]. GLM-5: at least 1.3x prefill and decode beyond 100K [Section 4.2].

### Goal: How much quality does index reuse cost, and where does it break?
Uniform interleaving loses Long Avg 2.8 (1/2) and 7.2 points (1/4) (50.2 -> 43.0); the greedy-searched pattern recovers 49.9 at 1/4 and 50.3 at 1/2; at 1/8 retention Long Avg drops to 35.3 (uniform) / 46.1 (searched) [Section 4.3]. Early and transitional layers are most sensitive to indexer removal [Section 3.1.1]. Reasoning (G&R Avg) stays within 1 point of 74.6 except uniform 1/8; 1/4 searched gives AIME 2025 92.6 vs 91.0 [Section 4.3]. With training-aware multi-layer distillation, uniform interleaving matches DSA [Section 4.4]. Remaining cost: the retained indexers still scan the full context per token, and the full KV cache stays resident.
