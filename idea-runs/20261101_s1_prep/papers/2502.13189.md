# MoBA: Mixture of Block Attention for Long-Context LLMs

## What this paper does
MoBA partitions the context into fixed-size blocks and routes each query to the top-k blocks by the inner product of the query with the mean-pooled keys of each block (an MoE-style gate with no new parameters), always including the query's own causal block. It is implemented on top of FlashAttention (varlen) and can be switched to and from full attention at any time, layer by layer. Evaluated by continued pretraining of Llama-3.1-8B to 1M context. The authors say it is "deployed to support Kimi's long-context requests"; code at github.com/MoonshotAI/MoBA [full text, Abstract].

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does MoBA select blocks and what does its selection cost at decode?
Gate score s_i = <q, mean_pool(K[I_i])> per block, top-k over blocks, plus forced current block with causal mask; future blocks masked [full text, Section 2.2 / Eq 5-6]. Per query the selection cost is one dot product per block (N/B blocks), so it grows with N/B; selected KV is k*B tokens. The full KV cache is still stored (selection only, no compression). Configs: 1.5B experiments B=2048, k=3 at 32K; Llama-8B-1M B=4096, k=12 (95.31% sparsity at 1M) with the last 3 of 32 layers kept as full attention [Section 3.2, 3.3].

### Goal: What efficiency and quality numbers are reported, and in which phase is the sparsity actually used?
Attention-layer forward time: up to 6.5x faster than full attention when prefilling 1M tokens; 16x at 10M tokens with fixed k and block count [Section 3.4, Figure 2]. Quality: RULER 0.7818 vs 0.7849 for the full-attention twin [Section 3.3]. Crucially, "across all evaluation tasks, MoBA is used for prefill only, while we switch to full attention during generation for better performance" [Section 3.3]. So the reported results do not test sparse decoding at all.

### Goal: What limitations do the authors state?
MoBA-only training gives higher position-wise loss for trailing tokens; a MoBA(90%)->full(10%) recipe is needed to match full attention [Section 3.2, Figure 5a]. MoBA "sometimes results in suboptimal performance during SFT", which they attribute to loss masking giving sparse gradients, fixed by keeping the last layers full [Section 3.2 "Layer-wise Hybrid"]. Future work: better block-selection strategies and "improving generalization in complex reasoning tasks" [Section 5]. Mean-pool gating is coarse; the authors note Quest is MoBA with smaller blocks and min/max pooling [Section 4].
