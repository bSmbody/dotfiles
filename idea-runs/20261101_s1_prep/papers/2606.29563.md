# Coverage-Driven KV Cache Eviction for Efficient and Improved Inference of LLM

## What this paper does
Finds that performance loss under prefill-time KV eviction (SnapKV etc.) correlates with reduced coverage — the number of unique tokens retained in at least one head/layer — because eviction scores are recency-skewed and overlap across heads and layers. Gives an information-bottleneck argument that low coverage limits input-output mutual information. K-VEC adds cross-head coverage (wider observation window for low-focus heads) and cross-layer coverage (bonus for tokens not yet selected in earlier layers). Gains up to 10.35 points at 128-token budget on LongBench, 1.61 on average.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What signal does it use to decide which KV entries to keep, evict, or recall, and is the decision reversible?
SnapKV observation-window attention per head [full text, Section 4.1, Eq 8-9]; for the delta heads with lowest score std the window is widened [Eq 10-11]; cross-layer focus = global importance x (1 - fraction of earlier layers that selected the token), added to the priority [Section 4.2, Eq 12-15]; top beta*B per head always kept [Eq 16]. One-shot after prefill; permanent.

### Goal: What does it report as its failure mode or limitation for tokens that are needed later (long chain-of-thought or long context)?
SnapKV keeps similar end-of-prompt tokens across heads and layers, so answer tokens early in the prompt are retained by no layer [Section 3.1, Fig 3]. Limitations: slight prefill overhead and incomplete coverage [Section 6].

### Goal: What method assumption does its scoring or training rely on (per-token additive utility, per-head independence, teacher-forced or offline training)?
Coverage counts unique token positions; the bound assumes "token contributions are additive (no contextual dependencies), and all tokens as equally informative" [Section 3.2]. Coverage is position-level and heuristic; it does not model that the same information may be restated at different positions (as in reasoning traces). Evaluated only on prompt (prefill) compression on LongBench.
