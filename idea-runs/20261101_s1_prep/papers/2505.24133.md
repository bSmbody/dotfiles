# R-KV: Redundancy-aware KV Cache Compression for Reasoning Models

## What this paper does
Targets decoding-time KV compression for reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1-Distill 7B/8B/14B), whose outputs are >8x longer than reference solutions and highly repetitive. Every buffer of generated tokens, R-KV rescores candidates with a joint score: attention importance from the last alpha observation tokens minus a redundancy score from average key-key cosine similarity, keeping the most recent of near-duplicate tokens. Keeps the top-k per head within a fixed budget and reports near-full accuracy on MATH-500/AIME at 10-34% cache.

## Goal-by-goal answers

### Goal: What signal does it use to decide which KV entries to keep, evict, or recall, and is the decision reversible?
Joint score Z = lambda*I - (1-lambda)*R per head [full text, Section 3.4, Eq 7]. I is attention from the last alpha observation tokens, max-pooled over a 2W window (SnapKV style) [Section 3.2, Eq 4]; R is the softmax-normalized mean cosine similarity of a token's key to all other keys, after zeroing similarity to the beta most recent similar tokens so that the newest copy of a repeated span is kept [Section 3.3, Eq 5-6]. Compression runs after each fixed-length buffer; entries not in the top budget are discarded permanently [Section 3.1].

### Goal: What does it report as its failure mode or limitation for tokens that are needed later (long chain-of-thought or long context)?
Shows that attention-only selection (SnapKV) over-retains repetitive self-reflection text because repeated content receives high attention, and has narrow, recency-concentrated coverage [full text, Sections 2.2, 5.2]. Its stated limitations are systems-level (paged attention compatibility, serving-framework support) [Appendix D]; it does not analyze tokens needed only much later.

### Goal: What method assumption does its scoring or training rely on (per-token additive utility, per-head independence, teacher-forced or offline training)?
Training-free and per-head. Redundancy is measured pairwise in key space within one head, so the score is a per-token scalar combining importance and similarity; it does not consider whether a copy of the same information survives in other heads or layers. lambda is a hand-tuned trade-off [Section 5.1].


### Goal: What models, lengths, headline results and code does R-KV report?
R1-Distill-Llama-8B and R1-Distill-Qwen-14B on MATH-500 (16K cap) and AIME24 (32K cap), pass@1 over 64 samples, buffer 128, alpha=8, lambda=0.1; baseline only SnapKV adapted to decoding [full text, Section 4.1]. R1-Llama-8B: lossless at 34% budget on MATH-500 and 10% on AIME24 (fixed budgets 1024 and 1536 tokens); 105% of FullKV accuracy at 16% budget; R1-Qwen-14B lossless at 54% (MATH-500) and 25% (AIME24); up to 40% accuracy over SnapKV [Section 4.2]. Code: https://github.com/Zefan-Cai/R-KV [front matter]. ThinKV measures that R-KV's gather compaction costs up to 37x TPOT at large batch with a sequential gather kernel [2510.01290, Section 5.1].
