# CARE: Covariance-Aware and Rank-Enhanced Decomposition for Enabling Multi-Head Latent Attention

## What this paper does
Post-hoc GQA->MLA conversion at KV-parity (Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-4B, others to 70B). Replaces weight-only SVD by activation-covariance-weighted SVD of sqrt(C)W for W_K and W_V, allocates rank across layers by greedy water-filling on normalized tail energy, adds a new decoupled RoPE channel (DeepSeek-style) with trainable W_Q^R, W_K^R, then "heals" with CE+KD fine-tuning (1-3B tokens).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: How does the conversion choose the latent subspace and the retained RoPE dimensions, and what objective does it fit?
Objective is per-token projection reconstruction min ||X W - X W_hat||_F^2 = ||sqrt(C)(W - W_hat)||_F^2 for K and V separately, with C the (uncentered) input second moment from calibration batches [full text, Sections 3.1, 3.3, Eqs 2-3]. Queries, softmax and position do not enter the objective. The RoPE path is new trainable matrices that are learned only during healing [full text, Section 3.4]. Default calibration is 256 samples of sequence length 32; "longer sequences tend to overfit", and covariance computation OOMs beyond length 512 [full text, Section 4.2.2, Figure 4]. Section 2 shows singular values are non-monotone proxies for accuracy importance [full text, Section 2, Observation 2].
### Goal: What does it report about long-context fidelity and limitations?
Very-low-rank one-shot conversion "remains challenging for all methods" [full text, Section 4.1]. NiH to 32K: CARE stays near the teacher while uniform-rank/SVD baselines degrade most at 18K-24K, and longer calibration sequences (Alpaca-2048 vs 256) improve stability [full text, Appendix I.2]. Limitations: stale statistics under domain shift, extreme compression needs SFT, no co-optimization with eviction/quantization; future work names "dynamic rank schedules that adapt latent capacity with context length" [full text, Appendix G].
