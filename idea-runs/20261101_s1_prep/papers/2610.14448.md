# KV4-RL: Unlocking Faster Asynchronous Rollouts for RLVR with INT4 KV Cache Quantization

## What this paper does
KV4-RL uses a calibration-free INT4 KV cache for asynchronous GRPO rollouts. Each 128-dim token-head vector gets one affine INT4 group (scale and offset in FP16, 4.25 bits), with a fixed Hadamard transform on post-RoPE keys and queries. Bounded INT8 protection keeps copies of the first s=16 and most recent w=128 positions in a side cache. The integer paged-attention kernel processes the INT4 and protected regions as disjoint sets and merges them exactly with regional softmax statistics. The paper also characterizes "KV concentration": asynchronous refill raises the per-step KV workload (7.82x resident KV volume on a matched trace).

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: Which quantization target is most valuable at high concurrency?
In matched decode replays, KV4 speeds up decode-forward 1.53-1.86x (4B) and 1.44-1.97x (8B), KV8 1.30-1.41x, while W4A16 (Machete) and W8A8 stay near or below BF16 (Section 4.2, Fig. 3b). This is evidence that at memory-saturated long generation, KV precision, not weight precision, is the lever.
### Goal: Does plain INT4 KV hurt long-reasoning trajectories?
Yes, under RL. KV8 tracks BF16 learning, but plain KV4 fails to match the late-stage improvement (lower MATH-500 avg@8) even with TIS. Sink+recent INT8 protection (16+128) cuts shared-prefix KL by 7.0x and restores MATH-500 avg@8 to 87.68 vs 88.16 BF16 (Section 5.2, Fig. 4). Final six-benchmark mean: KV4 is 4.6 points below BF16 at 8B; KV4-RL is within 0.5 points (Section 6.3). Response lengths are closely matched between KV4-RL and BF16 runs (Section 6.2).
### Goal: End-to-end gains?
Qwen3-8B-Base, 400 iterations: 1.80x cumulative rollout and 1.27x full-iteration speedup over BF16, and 1.97x rollout over the final 50 iterations. Qwen3-4B-Base: 1.57x / 1.17x (Section 6.2).
### Goal: Limitations?
Two dense Qwen3 models, math RLVR, colocated asynchronous GRPO. MoE, hybrid-attention and disaggregated settings are untested (Section 7.2). INT4 only; no comparison against FP8/FP4 attention kernels.
