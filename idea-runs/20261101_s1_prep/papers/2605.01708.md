# SplitZip: Ultra Fast Lossless KV Compression for Disaggregated LLM Serving

## What this paper does
GPU lossless codec for KV transfer between prefill and decode workers. Observes BF16 KV exponents have 2.89-3.59 bits of entropy and that the top-16 exponents cover >=99.3% of entries across dense, MoE and hybrid models; encodes exponents with a fixed 4-bit code from an offline-calibrated top-16 codebook, keeps the sign-mantissa byte exact, and sends rare exponents via a sparse (position, value) escape stream. Integrated with Mooncake transfer and SGLang; also applies to FP8 E5M2.

## Goal-by-goal answers
### Goal: What compression ratio and speed does lossless KV compression reach?
Ratio 1.324 on Qwen3-32B BF16 KV, with 613.3 GB/s encode and 2181.8 GB/s decode on H200, versus DFloat11 1.423 (encode 0.004 GB/s), ZipNN 1.515 (1.2 GB/s), nvCOMP Bitcomp 1.056 [full text, Section 4.2, Table 2]. End-to-end: up to 1.32x faster BF16 KV transfer, 1.30x TTFT, 1.23x request throughput; up to 1.14x over native E5M2 FP8 [abstract].
### Goal: What limitations do the authors state, and is code available?
"For short contexts or compute-bound regimes, codec overhead may outweigh the reduced transfer volume"; ratio "bounded by the redundancy in floating-point exponent values", complementary to lossy methods [Section 5]. It compresses transfers, not resident HBM KV read by attention. Code: https://github.com/Intelligent-Microsystems-Lab/SplitZip [abstract].
